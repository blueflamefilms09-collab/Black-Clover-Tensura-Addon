DEVIL CONTRACT RITUAL: HOW TO SET IT UP
(written from the mod's own data by tools/gen_devil_ritual_layout.py; see docs/devil_ritual_layout.png for the picture)

1. GET THE CONTRACT
   Find an Infernal Contract (relic loot) or craft one (server setting devil.contractCraftable, on by default):
      D O D
      B N B
      D S D
      D = Diamond
      O = Crying Obsidian
      B = Royal Blood, Zane Blood or a Gneiss Crystal (the tag #anime_multiverse:devil_contract_blood)
      N = Nether Star
      S = Wither Skeleton Skull

2. CHOOSE YOUR GROUND (one is enough)
   A. A dark magic at the circle: anyone within 8 blocks with a grimoire magic from Dark, Shadow, Curse, Curse Warding, Demon Fire, Demon Ice, Demon Light, Demon Water, Demon Beast, Blood, Bone, Soul, Soul Corpse, Anti Magic, Black Oil,
      or a dark devil attribute (Curse, Anti Magic, Ghost, Soul, Rot), or one of the 19 listed skills (assets/anime_multiverse/devil_dark_magic.json, add your own).
   B. A dark dimension: minecraft:the_end.
   C. A dark biome: deep dark, dark forest, soul sand valley, the end, small end islands, end midlands, end highlands, end barrens.
   D. A dark place: light level 4 or lower at your feet (block and sky together).
   The game checks once when you begin, and once a second while the ritual runs for ground D only.

3. STAND IN THE CIRCLE
   The circle is 3.5 blocks around the place you began. Leave it and the ritual breaks. The devil rises 3 blocks in front of you, facing you.

4. WHAT EACH WORLD'S RITUAL ASKS
   Black Clover - Devil-Binding Ritual (10 s): Bring a relic tied to the devil (an Infernal Contract in your inventory, it is spent). The devil is called into a circle and tries to take your body: stand your ground inside the circle through four blows.
   Chainsaw Man - A bargain of flesh (4 s): Nothing to prepare: call the devil's name and it comes. It names its price and takes it from your body.
   Black Butler - Faustian contract (7 s): A demon answers only despair: your health must be at half or lower.
   Blue Exorcist - Tamer's summoning circle (8 s): A sheet of paper for the circle (it is spent) and a drop of your blood (two hearts now). Keep your will: stay in the circle and above a third of your health, or the demon turns on you.
   That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime - Daemon summoning (9 s): An offering of bodies (32 rotten flesh, spent) and a name: naming the demon costs half of your maximum magicules.
   Dororo - Pact in the Hall of Hell (8 s): A roof over your head (no open sky): the demons of the hall answer a wish for prosperity.
   Berserk - The Behelit's Eclipse (10 s): It wakes only at the lowest point: your health at a quarter or lower, and 30 experience levels to offer.
   Fairy Tail - A Book of Zeref (7 s): A book to open (it is spent) and ether to wake it: a third of your maximum magicules.

5. THEN
   The devil makes its offer and waits 30 seconds. Sign or refuse.
   A devil keeps one holder (One of One): if another player holds it, it is not called again until the bond lapses or a bound duel is won.
   After any ritual wait 60 seconds before the next one (a failed or refused one too).

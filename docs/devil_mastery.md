# Devil Awakening: the devil contract as a mastered extra skill

Status: written and compiled with plain `javac` against the cached classpath (no Gradle in the session); the pure tests were run. **Nothing here was seen in a game.** See "Not verified".

A devil contract no longer gives its power at the press of a key. It puts the **extra skill Devil Awakening** in the Tensura extra-skill slot. The skill has a **mastery** that starts at 10 percent and grows only by using the devil. The mastery is the **ceiling of the assimilation level**, so horns, wings and the Devil Union open slowly. It is a temporary, demon-lord-style awakening: timed, with a cost, a rest and an aftermath, and the devil's own arts and passives come from the devil's anime (Black Clover, Chainsaw Man, Black Butler, Blue Exorcist, Tensura, Dororo, Berserk, Fairy Tail).

## The mastery curve (`MasteryRules`)

Mastery `m` is percent, 10 to 100. One percent costs `720 * (1 + 3 * ((m-10)/90)^2)` effective seconds (x1 at 10 %, x4 at 100 %). A use adds effective seconds, at most 120 per use, stepped so a big gain still follows the curve.

| use | effective seconds |
|---|---|
| each second awakened (x anti-AFK factor) | 1 |
| an art that hits / misses | 12 / 2 |
| a kill (victim max health >= 8, one kill per 4 s, scaled by health up to 20) | up to 10 |
| a blow dealt (one counted a second) | 1.5 |
| trial won / impressed (start bonus, in percent) | +3 / +1 |

- **Anti-AFK**: the awakened second counts fully when the player dealt a blow, cast an art or killed within the last minute, 0.2 when they only moved, 0 when they stood still. Blows taken do not count (a spawner AFK farm cannot work).
- **Daily cap**: 360 effective minutes a UTC day by default (`devil.mastery.dailyCapMinutes`). The whole climb is 36 effective hours, so **at least 6 real days** at the default cap.
- **Time to 100 %** (model `MasteryRules.hoursToMax`, a player who awakens as soon as the rest is over, hours of real play including the rests): heavy (6 arts, 5 kills, 30 blows a minute) 19 h; active (4, 3, 20) **26 h**; light (2, 1, 10) **40 h**; walking about without fighting 362 h; standing still never. Pinned by `MasteryRulesTest` (25 to 40 h for the active profile). `gainRate` x2 halves it (active: 13 h).

## Tiers and the awakening

| mastery | opens |
|---|---|
| 10 % (start) | first art, first passive, the level up to the mastery |
| 20 % | horns (level 20; the existing assimilation stage) |
| 25 % | second art |
| 50 % | wings and flight (level 50), third art, second passive |
| 75 % | fourth art, third passive |
| 100 % | the Devil Union (level 100), the union passives and the fusions |

| mastery | awakening | rest after | art power | passive power | fatigue after a run-out at level 100 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 % | 90 s | 300 s | 0.37 | 0.35 | 30 s |
| 25 % | 125 s | 270 s | 0.48 | 0.46 | 27 s |
| 50 % | 183 s | 220 s | 0.65 | 0.64 | 23 s |
| 75 % | 242 s | 170 s | 0.83 | 0.82 | 19 s |
| 100 % | 300 s | 120 s | 1.00 | 1.00 | 15 s |

- The awakening costs magicules per second as before (the existing `DevilRules.drain`). Pushing the devil back by hand rests a third of the time and has no fatigue. A run-out (time or magicules) at level 20 or more leaves Slowness I and Weakness I for the fatigue time. Under level 20 there is no fatigue.
- An art costs the tier's art price (`DualRules.cost`) times the art's `cost`, paid once through Tensura; its damage is `baseDamage(tier) * dmg * artScale * damageScale * Phase2.power`, capped at `DualRules.MAX_RAW`, through `BalanceLaw.damage` as `devil_magic` damage.
- Passives scale with `passiveScale * Phase2.power` (the kind's share), with hard caps (resist 60 %, regen 1 hp/s, stat caps per attribute).

## Black Clover: unions and transitions (audit of `devil/dual` and `devil/phase2`)

- **Transition (horns, wings, union)** is the existing assimilation stage (20 / 50 / 100 %), now gated by mastery. Each Black Clover devil names its own three stages and shows the name once when the stage is reached: Asta and Liebe: *Black Asta* (the horned, black form), *Anti-Magic Aura*, *Devil Union*. The other five devils use their own stage names.
- **Devil Union passives** (tier 4 = mastery 100 and inside the union): Liebe gives *Upgraded Swords* (every sword is upgraded in the union: Demon-Slayer, Demon-Dweller, Demon-Destroyer) and the *Union Anti-Magic Coat*; Lucifero: the king's might and a crushing weight on hit; Beelzebub: rule of the underworld and the folded skin; Astaroth: master of time and the stopped blow; Megicula: the curse as the weapon and curse wards; Lucifugus: the word as the weapon and *Command*.
- **Double unions (fusions)**: with the Devil Union running, a second devil that the player also holds (Phase 2 ledger) adds the fusion's passives. Four are in the file: Lucifero + Megicula, Lucifero + Beelzebub, Megicula + Beelzebub (the Dark Triad siblings Dante, Vanica, Zenon each host one in canon) and Liebe + Astaroth. **All four fusions are flagged `canon: false` (design)**: the series shows one devil union at a time, so the fused passives are this mod's design, built from the two canon unions.
- **Not mapped** (could not be sourced): Zagred, Nacht's four devils (Gimodelo, Slotos, Walgner, Felis), Yuno and the spirit unions (these are spirits, not devils in this mod), Marx/Mars, and Julius's Time devil other than Astaroth. They are not in the devil roster of `WorldDevil`, so they have no kit. Nothing was invented for them.
- `devil/dual/DevilSkillGrant`, `DevilMagic`, `DualRules` and `devil/phase2/*` were read and only touched where needed: `Phase2.joined` tells the mastery about the trial result; the ledger is read-only here.

## Worst-case cost (rule 11)

- **Per tick**: none. No tick event of its own; everything hangs on the existing once-a-second step of `DevilContracts` (one `peek`, nothing for a player without a contract).
- **Per second per holder**: a few additions, up to 6 attribute writes (each only when its value changed), one ledger lookup per second while in the union with a fused partner.
- **Per damage event**: one `hasData` check for a player without a contract; a holder: one mastery add and one on-hit effect, each at most once a second.
- **Per art cast**: one entity query, at most 6 / 8 / 4 hits, at most 3 self effects, one velocity write (rush), one small particle burst per hit through `SkillFx` and one cast VFX. Cooldowns are 6 to 120 s per art, four arts per player, so at most about 0.67 casts a second per player in theory, and the price in magicules makes it less. **No entity is spawned.**
- **Per stage change**: one Rukh burst, at most three per awakening.
- **Network**: no new packet type. `DevilNet.Sync` grows by three small varints (about 5 bytes) and is sent only when something changed (the mastery changes about once every 72 s at the start); the HUD counts the timers down on the client between packets.
- **Client**: the HUD text is rebuilt once a second; one extra bar and two strings per frame.
- **Memory**: the contract grows by a few numbers and a map of at most 32 stashed masteries; the kit table is parsed once (26 kits).

## Not verified (honest caveats)

- **Nothing was seen in a game.** No Gradle: the changed Minecraft classes were compiled with `javac` against the cached classpath (they compile), and the pure tests were run (see the hand-over). The CI server smoke test (`devil.double.mastery`, `devil.double.grant`, `devil.double.corruption`) is what proves it runs; it was not run here.
- The wikis could not be reached (English Black Clover wiki, Japanese wiki, and the wikis of the other series: the hosts do not resolve from the build machine). Names and powers come from web-search summaries (sportskeeda, comicbook.com, fandomwire, animehunch, epicdope for Black Clover); the `source` field of each kit says so, and every number is this mod's design. The kits outside Black Clover are flagged in their `source` as partly design. **Every kit should be checked against the wikis by the owner.**
- Existing contracts: a player who already had assimilation 80 is pulled down to the 10 % ceiling and has to earn the rest. This is the point of the change; `devil.legacyAssimilation = true` restores the old behaviour.
- The skill's own mastery bar (Tensura's) only mirrors the contract's mastery; the game's own `addMasteryPoint` is not used for it.
- The strike test of the self-test assumes a zombie two blocks ahead is inside the cone; if the cone's rule differs, the CI will say so.

## Decisions for the owner

1. "10-star mastery": it was read as the 10-percent start and a 10-tier-like growth (10 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100). If a different ladder is wanted, only `MasteryRules.TIER_AT` and the table change.
2. Mastery is **per devil** (a swap keeps each devil's own mastery). The alternative is one mastery per player.
3. The four fusions are design, not canon. Keep, rename or remove?
4. The old pact devils (the grimoire ones) have no kit; they only get the awakening.
5. Should an art cost health or a "burden" instead of magicules?
6. 25 to 40 hours is the active-to-light range; the default `gainRate` can be moved by the server.
7. The strict reading of "temporary demon lord awakening" has no aftermath under level 20; say if the fatigue should start earlier.

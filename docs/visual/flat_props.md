# Flat props: the pale paper cut-outs are gone (key `flat_props`)

## What the owner saw (builds 1.110 / 1.111, screenshot of a Bone Armor cast)

Next to the caster, pale white translucent flat shapes floated in the air: a big slab with a white blob, a flat white
sword silhouette, and a flat white half-moon "axe" on a thin stick. Owner: "remove this from any and all spell that
have it, it's ugly".

## What it was

Not a prop and not the Bone VFX. It is the effect shape `VfxShape.WEAPON_CONSTRUCTS` ("spectral swords / axes /
shields orbiting the caster"), drawn by `vfx/client/layer/AuraLayer.constructs()`. It billboarded three flat,
single-colour, white sprites (`textures/particle/construct_sword.png`, `construct_axe.png`, `construct_shield.png`,
all pure white, no shading inside the shape) with additive blending and tinted them with the book colour. Bone's
colour is pale bone white, so the result was pale white flat paper. The shield is the "slab", the axe is the
half-moon pair on a stick, the sword is the sword. The "before" preview scene reproduces the screenshot exactly
(`docs/previews/flat_props/constructs_before.png`).

The same kind of flat figure exists a second time: `textures/particle/arc_silhouette.png`, a white paper figure of
the caster, drawn upright by Mirror Magic's "Real Double" (`ArcaneSpellLayer2.doubles`) and by the double that steps
out of the glass (`MirrorLayer.step`).

## Spells that had it

| Sprite | Draws | Spells |
|---|---|---|
| `construct_sword`, `construct_axe`, `construct_shield` | `AuraLayer.constructs` (`WEAPON_CONSTRUCTS`) | Bone Armor (Bone), Iron Maiden (Steel), Creation: Arsenal, Pack Hunt (Beast), the hero Sword float and the hero Shield ward, the weapon arts constructs (`WeaponArts`, `MagicWeaponItem`), every relic act that names `WEAPON_CONSTRUCTS` |
| `arc_silhouette` | `ArcaneSpellLayer2.doubles`, `MirrorLayer.step` | Mirror Magic: Real Double and the double stepping out of the glass |

## What changed

* New client config **`visual.flatProps`** (file `config/nusmp-flatprops-client.toml`, CLIENT, default **false**).
  Off: the flat cut-outs are never drawn. On: the old look comes back exactly as it was. Nothing was deleted.
* One gate for every layer: `VfxVertexBuffer` refuses any quad of a texture on the list `vfx/FlatProps.FLAT` while the
  switch is off (so it also covers any other builder's layer that names one of these sprites). The list and the
  decision are pure (`vfx/FlatProps.java`, no Minecraft class).
* The spells that would be left without a body now draw **solid, shaded, low-poly models** through the same vertex
  buffer (`vfx/client/SolidParts.java`, `SolidModels.java`): a steel sword with gold guard and pommel, a double-bit
  axe (oak shaft, two wedge heads), a bevelled shield with a gold bar and boss, and a glass mannequin for the Mirror
  doubles. Each face is lit by a fixed sun direction (lit side, dark side), textured from a new material sheet
  `textures/flatprops/solid.png` (steel, gold, bone, glass, oak, dark steel, leather, mirror silver; generated by
  `tools/gen_flatprops_textures.py`). The constructs keep their rune circle and a small glow on each weapon, the
  doubles keep the glint, the ground ring and gain a soft inner glow.
* Previews: `tools/vfx_preview` scenes `constructs_*`, `constructs_close_*`, `double_*`, `step_*` (`*_flat` = before,
  `*_solid` = after), output in `docs/previews/flat_props/`. The preview tool itself was repaired (its `drain()` still
  expected the pre-1.99 buffer, and the Arcane layer's seal helper was not copied), so it builds again.

## Audit (all magics)

Gated (flat object cut-outs, removed by default): `construct_sword`, `construct_axe`, `construct_shield`,
`arc_silhouette` (above). The scan ran over every `textures/particle/*.png` that is a single-colour fill with a hard
edge and over every `billboard / plane / quad / stand / upright / beam` call of the layers, props and auras.

Reviewed and **kept** (not flat object stand-ins, or owned by another lane; listed so the owner can order more):

* Prop painters (`client/prop/**`): the bone, sand, slash, legion and recombination props are real 3D parts already.
  The only single flat quads are 14 ground shadow / ring decals (`PropDraw.quad`); `FlatPropsSourceScanTest` pins that
  list, so a new flat quad fails the build until it is reviewed.
* `BoneLayer` (Bone, other builder's lane): arms, spikes and claws are shaded cel-art ribbons, not flat white fills.
  Not touched.
* Soul Corpse / Soul (other builder's lane, being rebuilt): `scorpse_hand`, `scorpse_grave`, `scorpse_shard`,
  `soul_skull` are flat white single-colour silhouettes drawn as additive billboards (`SoulCorpseFxLayer`,
  `SoulFxLayer`). **Reported, not changed.** If the rebuild keeps them, add them to `FlatProps.FLAT` (one line each,
  the gate then hides them everywhere).
* Wing (`wing_wing`, `wing_feather`), Zogratis blood spear (`bfx_lance`, red-tinted), shadow hands (`arc_hand`,
  dark ink), painted sketches (`paint_sketch_*`, a painting on purpose), Kotodama `koto_sword` / `koto_trident`
  (shaded art), Legion chess pieces (shaded art), small tumbling debris shards (`gravity_shard`, `snow_shard`,
  `spatial_shard`, `mirror_reflex_shard`, `memory_shard`, `slash_rip_shard`, the wiki shard group): kept; none is
  an object cut-out the size of the construct sprites. Whether the owner wants wings, shards or the Soul sprites
  hidden too is his call.

## Cost (rule 11: no lag)

* Server: nothing. No packet, no entity, no tick work. The switch is a client config; the server never reads it.
* Client, per effect: a solid model keeps only the faces that look at the camera and is capped by the buffer's
  existing **400 vertices (100 quads) per effect** (`SolidParts.MAX_FACES = 100`); a model that no longer fits the
  remaining budget is skipped whole; a model further than 40 blocks is not built; the weapon count follows the
  effect's level of detail (`ctx.seg(4, 3)`: 4 at full detail, 3 below).
* Measured with the preview (vertices, 4 per quad): constructs 276 (was 76 for the flat sprites), Real Double with
  three doubles 276 (was 156), the step-out double 136 (was 96). Worst case per effect is the 400 cap.
* Per frame across effects: unchanged ceilings from the performance pass: at most 64 running effects, distance LOD,
  per-frame vertex cap by quality, `PerfProbe` sections. A cast of constructs is a short effect on one caster.
* Allocation: `SolidParts` reuses one scratch model, one set of scratch vectors and fixed arrays; building and
  drawing a face allocates nothing (pinned by a source scan). The layers add one `ctx.rel` per effect, as before.
* Draw calls: all solid faces of an effect go through one texture and one blend (one batch).

## Tests

`FlatPropsTest` (pure decision, the listed sprites really are flat single-colour fills, the material sheet is
opaque with grain, off by default), `FlatPropsSourceScanTest` (the buffer gate, every file that names a flat sprite
branches on the switch, no prop painter or aura names one, the 14 reviewed flat quads, the solid branch and its cost
bounds, no render type of its own, Time Magic untouched). `SafeAdditive*`, `FxSourceScanTest`,
`NoFlatCardPictureTest`, `PerfSourceScanTest`, `SquareBugAssetsTest` still pass. Self-test `visual.flat_props`
(pure, runs on the dedicated server).

## Not verified (nothing visual can be run here)

* The models were only seen in the headless preview rasteriser (no world lighting, no depth against blocks, no GL).
  How they look in the game (size, brightness against sky and ground, the glass doubles' transparency) is unseen.
* Faces are drawn far to near through one batch without a depth write: overlapping parts of one model are ordered
  correctly, but a model against another translucent effect may sort differently than in the preview.
* The Mirror step-out double is dim in the preview from a side camera; it may need a brighter alpha in game.
* The Gradle build and the in-game config load (`visual.flatProps` file creation) were not run; the Java files were
  type-checked against the stubs only (the two remaining errors are the known stub gaps in the untouched flush code).
* The audit of "flat" is by texture statistics plus reading the draw calls; a flat shape built another way (a vanilla
  texture, a render type quad) would not have been found.

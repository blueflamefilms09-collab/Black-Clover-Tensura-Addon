* **Repo copies of the English wiki (`.github/wiki/*.txt`)** were read for World Tree, Beast, Body, Briar, Cherry Blossom, Fungus and Demon Beast Magic (the attribute page and its `-- spell pages:` line) and for the spell pages Great Tree Mistilteinn, Magic Tree Descent and Demon-Dweller Sword: Spirit Light Tree.
  The lists name very few spells that are not already pages: World Tree: Dracaena Formation and Spirit Light Tree (both built here, the first as a ring of roots, the second as the sharing half of the union spell); Beast: Holy Hunting, Holy Howling, Bogeyman (all built here). The other books' lists are fully covered by the existing pages.
* **English wiki through WebSearch** (blackclover.fandom.com): Poison Plant Magic, Fungus Magic (Talking / Running / Towering Mr. Mushroom, already pages), Beast Magic. Confirmed: Vetto's Bear Claw, Cheetah Charge and Rhinoceros Armor (already pages); Yrul's Holy Howling (manga ch.344, summons a five-headed water dragon, the mod keeps the shock wave).
  Not confirmed by any result: Holy Hunting and Bogeyman have no description in the results (they are listed in the repo's copy of the Beast Magic page), so their effect is the mod's reading and is marked as such in the lang text.
* **Character set notes** (`character_spells/mimosa.json`, `charmy.json`, `gordon.json`, `kirsch.json`, `charlotte.json`, `william.json`, `sources` and `planned`): the spells named there but not built became canon candidates: Mimosa's Magic Flower Bullet and Princess Healing Flower Garden (built here).
* **Japanese wiki / Japanese search (fallback)**: searched for Vetto (獣魔法: ベアクロウ and ライノセラスアーマー confirmed, the roar blast and the regeneration have no name), Gordon (毒魔法) and Mimosa (植物魔法). No further spell names were found; Gordon's and Mimosa's lists could not be read at all.
* **Could not be fetched at all**: the English wiki spell lists of Poison Magic (Gordon: only Aufwachen Dachs, Violett Schirm, Verbotenes Obst, Poison Cloud's Abode are known and already pages; a search for "Violett Schirm" and "Verbotenes Obst" returned nothing), Cotton Magic (Charmy), Plant Magic (Mimosa), Tree Magic, Vine Magic, Body Magic and the Fungus / Demon Beast lists beyond the names above.
  Because the wiki yields almost no unbuilt spell for these books, 79 of the 86 pages are original pages in the book's own style (the "mod's own page" class that `basic_grimoires.md` already accepts); they are marked `original` in the table. **Decision for the owner:** whether original pages are acceptable for the majority.

## Worst case of the batch (rule 11)

* A page is one server cast. Standard casts come from `Pages15Kit` and keep its clamps (targets 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 by tier, ranges and radii capped, a field at most 100 ticks at 20-tick steps).
* The nine private helpers in `Batch7` (`groupFx`, `selfFx`, `foeFx`, `single`, `siphon`, `leap`, `pull`, `pulse`, `wall`) are built only on `Pages15Kit.Ctx`: hits go through `Ctx.hit` (clamped to 1.5 standard hits, through the book's balance law), targets through `Ctx.foes` (at most the tier's number), effects through `Ctx.fx` (at most 10 packets per cast), zones through `Ctx.zone` (at most 100 ticks, at most 2 per player).
  * `groupFx` / `pulse` allies: one box query of the spell's own size (radius at most 10) and at most 8 allied players are touched. `foeFx`: one box query, at most the tier's targets.
  * `leap`: one velocity set on the caster and ONE delayed task (14 ticks, cancelled if the caster died or left) that hits at most the tier's targets. `pull`: at most the tier's targets get a velocity set. No hard control (no stun) is applied by these helpers; `bind` pages use the kit's bind with the shared 12 second control lock.
  * `siphon` / `pulse` drain: `EnergyBridge.drain` of at most 3 % of a foe's magicules per call, on at most the tier's targets (one call per pulse, 5 pulses at most).
  * `wall`: the existing `ElementBook.wall` (at most 12 temporary blocks for 160 ticks, only into free space, through `SpellRuntime.tempBlock`).
* Packets: at most 3 effect packets per cast (cast circle, one effect, one landing burst); a zone page sends ONE packet for its whole duration. Per-tick cost while nobody casts: none (the lists are built once per book and cached by `GrimoireBook`).
* Nothing spawns an entity, places a persistent block, loads a chunk or keeps static state. The 86 pages add no client work of their own: every picture is a `VfxShape` the book or the element family already draws.
* Largest single cast: Deluge of Venom / World Tree Awakening: 10 targets per pulse, 5 pulses, 1 packet for the field plus the cast circle.

## New and replaced (rule 5)

All 86 pages of this batch are NEW; nothing is replaced and no old page, id, mode number or lang text is touched (pages are appended after every old page of their book, so every unlock bit and mastery stays).
The character spell sets of William, Gordon, Charmy, Mimosa, Kirsch and Charlotte only gain appended entries (see below).

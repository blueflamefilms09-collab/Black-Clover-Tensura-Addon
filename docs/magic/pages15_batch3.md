# Pages 15, batch 3: Wind, Air, Sound, Mind and Soul

Owner goal: every grimoire has 15 spells. This batch owns these books (and no other): `book_dream`, `book_illusion`, `book_air`, `book_smoke`, `book_vortex`, `book_wing`, `book_wind`, `book_sound`, `book_memory`, `book_permeation`, `book_soul`, `book_soul_corpse`, `book_song`.

How to build a page: `docs/magic/pages15_recipe.md`. Files this batch edits: `src/main/java/com/newuniverse/nusmp/book/pages15/Batch3.java`, `tools/lang/pages15_batch3.json`,
`src/test/resources/armour_policy/pages15_batch3_new_pages.json`, this file, the pure test `Pages15Batch3Test`, and the character set files of the characters whose book is in this batch (`dorothy`, `yuno`, `lucius`, `kahono`; `rades` already shows 15).

**What was built.** 87 new pages (66 original, 21 with a canon source), all NEW, nothing replaced; they are the LAST family pages of their book (no mode number, unlock bit or mastery moves), behind `magic.pagesFifteen`.
Per book: dream +11, illusion +10, air +7, smoke +7, vortex +7, wing +7, wind +6, sound +6, memory +6, permeation +6, soul +6, soul corpse +6, song +2. Every basic grimoire of the batch now shows at least 15 pages.
Most pages are the kit's standard casts (the tier fixes the damage); about 25 use the kit's bounded `Ctx` (party buffs, projectile guards, zones, a leap, a hard-control signature) through the small helpers at the top of `Batch3.java`.
No new asset, entity, packet or block is used. **Nothing here was seen in a game** (only compiled against the API stubs and unit tested).

## Worst case of the batch (rule 11)

Every page is one server cast of the kit: at most the tier's targets (4 / 6 / 8 / 10) hit, at most 3 effect packets (the kit allows 10), and no effect after the cast ends except the pages with a zone. Zones: Pastel Garden, Endless Corridor, Spirit of Notos, Sound Barrier and the standard `field` pages run one kit zone of 60 to 100 ticks in steps of 4 or more ticks (at most 2 per player, enforced by the kit). Helpers added in `Batch3.java`: party effects touch at most 6 players; `calm` clears at most 8 mobs; `clearShots` removes at most 6 to 8 projectiles in a radius of at most 8 blocks; `pull` and `shove` set one velocity per target; no entity is spawned, no block placed, no chunk loaded, no state is kept between casts. Eternal Slumber (signature) takes the shared control lock (`BalanceLaw.beginControl`) and the control-time law before freezing, so it cannot chain. The queries are the same `GrimoireBook.around` ones as the old pages (radius at most 12). Packets per second: a cast sends at most 3 effect packets to nearby players and the shortest cooldown is 4 seconds (starter), so at most 0.75 packets per second per player from this batch, plus one effect per zone pulse (5 at most).

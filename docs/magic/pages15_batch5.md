## Research sources

Rule 10 order: English wiki first, Japanese wiki as the fallback. The fandom pages cannot be fetched from the build machine, so every wiki fact below was read from (a) the repo's own copies in `.github/wiki/` (`bone_magic.txt`, `blood_magic.txt`, `light_magic.txt`, `curse_magic.txt`, `curse_warding_magic.txt`, `demon_light_magic.txt` and the spell pages `spell_jets_of_blood.txt`, `spell_lifeblood_scythe.txt`, `spell_curse_breaking_blood_cocoon.txt`, `spell_elemental_quintet.txt`), (b) web-search summaries of the English fandom pages (the search tool returns a short summary, never the page), and (c) the `sources` and `planned` notes of the eight character spell sets of this batch, which hold earlier search summaries (English and Japanese).

**Summary of the marks:** 94 pages in all (92 needed + 2 extra); canon 22, original 72. Counting the extras: Dark gets 14 (10 shared + the 4 Yami-only spells of `yami.json` "planned"), Shadow 12 (10 shared + Unite Mode Canis x Equus and Walpurgis Night, Nacht only), Light 9 (7 shared + Karna's Gibbous Ray and Moon Trap). The Nacht, Yami and Karna pages are marked `exclusive` in their set (the basic grimoire does not show them), so every one of the 11 basic grimoires shows exactly or more than 15 pages and no signature of a character is given away.

## Worst case of the batch (rule 11)

Every page is one server cast of the kit (`Pages15Kit`) or a custom cast that uses only the kit's `Ctx` (hits through `Ctx.hit`, at most the tier's targets, at most 10 packets, zones of at most 100 ticks in steps of at least 4, at most 2 kit zones per player). No page spawns an entity, loads a chunk or keeps static state; nothing runs per tick while nobody casts. The per-page figure is in the last column of the table. The pages that go beyond the plain kit shapes, and why they are still bounded:

* **Armed traps** (`pitfall_trap`, `mine_trap`, `karna_moon_trap`): one kit zone of 100 ticks, one small foe query (radius 1.8 to 2) every 4 ticks (at most 25 queries per cast), the blast happens once (an `AtomicBoolean` per cast), at most the tier's targets are hit, 2 to 3 packets.
* **Magic-Negating Barrier**: one kit zone of 80 ticks, one projectile box query (5 blocks) every 5 ticks (16 queries), `discard()` of other people's projectiles only, 1 packet.
* **Black Guard, Dusk, Protecting Light, Causality Break**: instant, one box query (4.5 to 8 blocks), at most 6 targets / 6 players, 1 packet.
* **Self heals and wards** (`bone_regrowth`, `curse_breaking_blood_cocoon`, `black_cocoon`, `radiant_ward`): the caster only; the heal goes through `BalanceLaw.heal` (healing decay applies), at most 40 percent of the maximum health.
* **Walls** (`bone_wall`, `ash_barricade`): the existing `ElementBook.wall`, 12 temporary blocks that go away after 8 seconds (`SpellRuntime.tempBlock`).
* **Forbidden pages**: the older pages' price (`ForbiddenMagic.payPrice`, health that stacks) is paid first and the dark subtitle goes to players within 48 blocks, the same as the 8 older pages.
* Client cost: no new effect, model or texture; every picture is an existing `VfxShape` of the book, sent through the book's own `vfx` (the perf budgets of the shapes apply). The most any page sends is 3 packets (the kit allows 10).
* Worst case of the whole batch: 94 pages add zero per-tick cost while nobody casts; one cast is at most 10 targets, 3 packets and (for a zone page) 25 queries over 5 seconds.

## New and replaced (rule 5)

All 94 pages are NEW; nothing is replaced and nothing is removed. Every page is appended after the older pages of its book (the kit's hook), no id, name or mode number moves, and the setting `magic.pagesFifteen` (server, default true) brings every list back to what it was.

Character grimoires (the files under `data/nusmp/character_spells/` were only appended to, no entry was changed; the "planned" lists were left as they were): `zenon` +10, `yami` +4 (his four "planned" spells, now real pages, exclusive), `nacht` +4 (Doppelganger, Grasping Hands, and the exclusive Canis x Equus and Walpurgis Night), `zora` +10, `vanica` +11, `karna` +9 (Gibbous Ray and Moon Trap exclusive), `lemiel` +11, `asta` +7.

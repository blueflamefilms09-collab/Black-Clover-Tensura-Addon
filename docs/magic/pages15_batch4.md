# Pages 15, batch 4: Earth, Stone and Metal

Owner goal: every grimoire has 15 spells. This batch owns these books (and no other): `book_mercury`, `book_sand`, `book_steel`, `book_rock`, `book_red_ochre`, `book_stone`, `book_mineral`, `book_sandstone`, `book_earth`, `book_shakudo`, `book_crystal`, `book_glass`, `book_iron`, `book_copper`, `book_corundum`, `book_bronze`.

How to build a page: `docs/magic/pages15_recipe.md`. Files this batch edits: `src/main/java/com/newuniverse/nusmp/book/pages15/Batch4.java`, `tools/lang/pages15_batch4.json`,
`src/test/resources/armour_policy/pages15_batch4_new_pages.json`, this file, the three character set files `nozel.json`, `klaus.json`, `mars.json`, and the test `Pages15Batch4Test`.

Before it was built (plain words): 96 new spell pages are appended to the 16 books of this batch, behind the setting `magic.pagesFifteen` (false = the old lists). Each page is one bounded server cast
(standard kit shapes plus five small custom casts); the books' mode numbers and unlock bits do not move. Not included: new art, new entities, new packets, per-page icons, anything in another batch's books.
Of the 96 pages **15 are canon** (a spell name from the English or Japanese wiki, or from the character set notes) and **81 are original** (this mod's own, in the book's style).

## Custom casts (the only code beyond the kit's standard shapes)

All five live in `Batch4.java`, are built on the kit's `Ctx` and use nothing else: `guard` (Resistance and absorption on the caster for at most 200 ticks, 1 packet), `leap` (a jump of at most 10 blocks, one
`later` task of 16 ticks that hits the tier's targets, 2 packets), `pull` (the foes round the aim point are pulled in once and hit once, 1 packet), `strikeAt` (a marked spot that hits the tier's targets after
at most 100 ticks, 3 packets), `thornWall` (the existing `ElementBook.wall`, 12 temporary blocks, plus the existing `ThornsUntil` tag for 160 ticks). `ElementBook.decoy()`, `ElementBook.wall(..)` and `healAllies` are the existing shared casts.
The book classes that do not extend `ElementBook` (Mercury, Steel, Crystal, Glass, Iron, Copper, Corundum, Bronze) get no `empower` page: their melee-bonus hook does not exist, so `guard` (a plain effect) is used there.

## Worst case of the batch (rule 11)

Every page is one server cast. Bound per page: at most the tier's targets (starter 4, mid 6, zone 8, signature 10), at most one zone of 100 ticks at 4+ tick steps (the kit allows 2 per player at once), at most
one delayed task of at most 100 ticks (`leap`, `strikeAt`; both do nothing if the caster has died or left), at most 10 effect packets to nearby players (the kit's budget; most pages send 1 to 3), 12 temporary
blocks for the three wall pages (`SpellRuntime.tempBlock`, short-lived). Nothing runs per tick after a cast ends; 96 new pages add no per-tick cost while nobody casts them. No page goes beyond the project-wide figure. No new entity,
packet, asset or client code; the client draws only the existing, already budgeted effects (`*_FX1..3`, `EARTHFAM_*`, `SAND_MAGIC`, `STONE_SPIKES`, `MERCURY_*`, `EARTH_RAMPAGE`).
The `pull` page moves at most the tier's targets by 0.5 blocks per tick once; `leap` moves only the caster.

## New and replaced (rule 5)

All 96 pages are NEW; nothing is replaced and nothing is removed. Character grimoires: 3 set files extended by appending (Nozel +7, Klaus +10, Mars +11, of which 3 are existing hidden pages opened for him).

## Honest caveats

Nothing here was seen in a game. What was checked offline: the type-check against the API stubs (the only errors are the stubs' "MobEffectInstance is abstract", present in every file that builds an effect, and a negative control
proved the check bites), the pure tests listed in the report. Not verified in a game: that each effect looks right with the book's colour (the earth-family layer reads the colour; Steel, Rock, Stone, Red Ochre,
Mineral, Sandstone and Earth share its three shapes), the feel of the leap, pull and strikeAt casts, and the balance numbers (they are the kit's tier numbers, scaled by the book's balance law).

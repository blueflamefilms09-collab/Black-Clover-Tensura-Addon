# 3.2.0 — Wind effects in pixel-anime style (Crescent Slash, Slicing Wind Emperor, Gale, Wind Ring)

### NEW
- **Pixel-anime Wind effects ("C")** for Crescent Kamaitachi (Slash), Slicing Wind Emperor, Gale and the Wind Ring: 32 px pixel-art sprites with a dark green outline, a 5-colour palette, hard 4-step alpha, a 2-frame flip-book that swaps every 3 ticks, motion "on twos" and held smear ghosts on the crescents. New client layer `WindMcLayer` (textures `wmc_*`), client option `vfx.element.wind = MC_C | V1` (file `nusmp-wind-mc-client.toml`, default MC_C).
- **Gale gust lane (`FX_WIND_GUST`) is now actually drawn**; before it only spawned dust.
- Pages that reuse the Crescent Slash (six non-wind melee pages) keep their book colour.
- Self-test `magic.wind_mc`; tests `WindMcTest`, `WindMcAssetTest`, `WindMcSourceScanTest`.
- Docs and previews (Wind pixel styles A / B / C, in-game render, Fire / Water / Earth research and proposals, Water 3D-toon previews) under `docs/vfx_remake/` and `docs/previews/vfx_remake/`.

### REPLACED (look only, behind the V1 setting)
- Crescent Slash, Slicing Wind Emperor, Gale, Wind Ring now draw in the pixel-anime look. `vfx.element.wind = V1` restores the old drawing exactly.

### KEPT (not touched at the owner's request)
- **Wind Tornado** and **Spirit of Zephyr** (both old layers, all settings), the swallow sprites, every old texture, Time Magic.

### Cost
- Worst case per activation (quads, old cap in brackets): Slash 8 (72), Emperor 18 (80), Gale 16 (68), Gust 16, Ring 13 (100). No packets, no server work, nothing allocated per frame; goes through the existing distance / particle-level LOD.

### Not done / caveats
- Not seen in a game (software render, no bloom; the nearest-neighbour texture filter is unverified in game). Tinted pages use a grey twin of the textures multiplied by the colour; a very dark colour loses the outline. The Wind Ring is a flat decal and vanishes edge-on.
- Fire and Water remakes are previews only (not in the game); Earth is not started.

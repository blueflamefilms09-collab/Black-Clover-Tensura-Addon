# 3.31.0 — Veldanava casts the base Tensura moves

FIXED: the 284 skill-tree files moved from `data/nusmp/skill_trees` to `data/nusmp/unique_skills`, so Ako's Evil Skill Tree no longer syncs a 2.9 MB string (limit 1,048,576) that disconnected players on world load.

SMALLER: the 14 weapon skins at 2048 px are now 1024 px (about 47 MB less in the jar).

See CHANGELOG.md and `docs/veldanava.md`. NEW: his Absolute Severance, Black Lightning, Black Lightning Blast, Death Tornado, Disintegration, Hell Flare and Megiddo are drawn by the base Tensura Reincarnated entities (Tensura's models, animations, sounds), found by registry id; their own damage, knockback, fire and effects are turned off and his own capped damage stays. REPLACED: nothing removed; the 3.30.0 effects are the fallback. KEPT: everything older; `tensuraMoves = false` in `nusmp-veldanava-server.toml` restores 3.30.0 exactly.

Code: `entity/veldanava/VeldanavaTensura.java`, `VeldanavaMoves`, `VeldanavaBoss`, `VeldanavaConfig`.

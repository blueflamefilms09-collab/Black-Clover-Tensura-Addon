3.47.0 (minor): Ultimate pair 2. Gate of Babylon (Fate) and Ki (Dragon Ball) are two new Ultimates with ten modes each.

NEW: Gate of Babylon (Gate, Volley, Rain, Chains of Heaven, Golden Armour, Vimana, King's Aura, Gae Bolg, Ea, Elixir); Ki (Ki Blast, Kamehameha, Instant Transmission, Solar Flare, Afterimage, Super Saiyan, Spirit Bomb, Kaioken, Ultra Instinct, Ki Sense); the shared hold rule ControlRules; two icons; settings ultimates_3_47.gateEnabled and ultimates_3_47.kiEnabled; tests and the self-tests skill.gate.registry / skill.ki.registry.
REPLACED: nothing (both are new; the Gate of Babylon Crest relic item is a different thing and stays).
KEPT: every other skill, Nen and All Fiction exactly as in 3.46.0; shared files were only appended to.

Obtain: /tensura edit <player> ability grant nusmp:gate_of_babylon and /tensura edit <player> ability grant nusmp:ki. Each can be switched off in the server config (gateEnabled = false, kiEnabled = false: the modes refuse and the damage hooks do nothing).

Not seen in a game: balance and feel of the numbers, the Vimana leap, the blink on slopes, the gate and beam effects. The English and Japanese wiki hosts could not be fetched in this build, so the modes follow the series as the author knows it, unchecked against a page. Not included: Gilgamesh's other Noble Phantasms as items, Dragon Ball fusion and the other transformations, per-mode animated icons.

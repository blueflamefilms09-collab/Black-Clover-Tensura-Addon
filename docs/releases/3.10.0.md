# 3.10.0 — Head and feet relic slots

NEW: Curios slots `relic_head` and `relic_feet` (1 each). All 115 HEAD and 49 FEET relics are in their tags (`data/curios/tags/item/relic_head.json`, `relic_feet.json`, written by `tools/relics/gen_curios_tags.py`).
Open the Curios screen and put the Mask of Mind Melting in "Head Relic" and the Ruby Boots of Planestriding in "Feet Relic".

How it is drawn: `Relics.scanExtra` accepts head/feet relics found in Curios (Where.CURIOS); `WornPlan` already draws an armour-slot relic from the model library whatever its source, so the blocky feet models and the head models are drawn by the normal worn layer. The Curios eye icon hides them.
KEPT: armour slots, the armour layer, all old models. REPLACED: nothing.
Worst case: one extra stack read per Curios slot per scan (10-tick cache), no packets, no entities.
Not seen in a game: the build only compiles and runs pure tests.

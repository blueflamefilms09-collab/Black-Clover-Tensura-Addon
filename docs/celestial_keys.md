# Celestial Spirit Magic, the Celestial Keys and the Celestial Spirit King

> **Removed (owner request, see `docs/fairy_tail_celestial_books_removed.md`):** the skill `nusmp:celestial_spirit_magic` (`skill/CelestialSpiritMagicSkill`) and its book
> `celestial_spirit_magic_book` no longer exist. The keys, the Gate, the Spirit World and the Spirit King below are still in the game; the spirit summoning, Star Dress and
> King modes of section 3 are historical (nothing can call them any more). `skill/CelestialArts` and the spirit props stay because `celestial/CelestialProps` and the King use them.

The Celestial Keys (physical items), the Gate that opens when all twelve Gold Zodiac keys are carried, and the Celestial Spirit King behind it. It does not use the skill tree or any grimoire.

Code: `celestial/*` (rules, ledger, items, Gate, Spirit World, king), `skill/CelestialArts` (what the spirits do),
`client/prop/CelestialPropPainter`, `client/celestial/*` (renderers). Assets: `tools/celestial/*` (every texture, model and json is generated by a script and can be redone).

## 1. Learning it

Removed: there is no book and no skill to learn any more. `/multiverse celestial give <player> <key|all|gold|silver|sceptre>` still hands out the keys and the sceptre.

## 2. The keys (physical items)

17 items from `CelestialKey` (append-only: the order is the save format), each with a 16x16 icon, a real 3D model in hand (`WeaponRenderer`, mesh `weapon_meshes/<id>.txt`, skin
and additive glow, `orient manual`), a Curios tag entry and Tensura engravings (`item/WeaponEngravings`).

* **12 Gold Zodiac keys**: Aquarius, Taurus, Leo, Virgo, Sagittarius, Gemini, Cancer, Scorpio, Capricorn, Pisces, Aries, Libra.
* **4 silver keys** (lesser spirits, not needed for the Gate): Horologium, Crux, Lyra, Nikora (Canis Minor). Pyxis was left out: it could not be confirmed as canon.
* **The key of the Celestial Gate** (`spirit_king_gate_key`): handed over automatically the moment the twelfth gold key is noticed, exactly once; `/multiverse celestial gate open`
  makes a lost one again for a player whose collection is complete.

Where a key must be carried: the **main inventory, the off hand, or the Celestial Key slot of Curios** (12 places, `data/nusmp/curios/slots/celestial_key.json`; Curios is read
reflectively through `CuriosLink`, so nothing needs Curios to be installed).

### Collection tracking (server side)

`CelestialData` (a player attachment, `CelestialAttachments`, saved with the player, copied on death, never trusted from the client) records the keys ever held, the contracts, a bond
per key, the Star Dress worn, and the King's record. Once a second `CelestialLedger.scan` notices carried keys (a key that carries another player's contract does not count),
says so the first time ("n of 12"), and detects completion. A read-only copy is sent to the client (`CelestialNet`, packet `nusmp:celestial_sync`) only so the mode wheel can skip
what the player does not have; the server re-checks everything.

### The contract (bind ritual)

Hold the key's use button for 3 seconds (a ring of stars, 3% of the maximum magicules, at least 40): the key now carries the player's name, the ledger marks the spirit bound and the
bond with it starts. A key bound to someone else is refused. Sneak + use breaks the contract (the key can then be given away). Rules: `CelestialRules.canBind`.

## 3. The skill and its modes

`NUSkill` (type UNIQUE). The mode wheel shows only what the player may use (`nextMode` skips the rest, on both sides):

| Modes | What |
|---|---|
| 0-15 | the spirit of each key the player has collected (12 gold, 4 silver) |
| 16 | Send the Spirits Home (dismiss) |
| 17-28 | Star Dress: a gold spirit's power worn for a minute |
| 29 | The Spirit King's Blessing (after recognition or victory) |
| 30 | Summon the Spirit King (after victory only) |

A summon needs: the key carried (and usable), the contract sealed, no cooldown, room among the spirits already out (1; 2 from 50% mastery; 3 from 90%) and the magicules
(`costPermille` of the maximum, 3% cheaper per bond level, never under 40). A summon gives 1 bond point (2 for a Star Dress) and mastery. Bond levels 0-5 (3, 8, 16, 30, 50 points):
each is 4% shorter cooldown, 3% cheaper and 5% more power.

### The spirits and what they do

a hard control is cut to `bossFactor` of its time and at most one second; every hard control also goes through `BalanceLaw.beginControl` (the shared 12 s limit) and
`BalanceLaw.controlTicks` (3 s on players, 5 s on mobs, diminishing returns). Healing goes through `BalanceLaw.heal`.

### Star Dress (mastery unlocks)

20% ... Libra 64%). It lasts 60 s, costs 15% of the maximum magicules, has a 2 minute cooldown, and gives attribute modifiers plus one effect (the table below is in `CelestialKey`;
the modifiers are transient and are taken off when the minute ends, at logout and when another dress is put on). The forging VFX is the star circle and the halo; a render

## 4. The spirits as props (models, animations, circle)

Each spirit is a `MagicPropEntity` of the new `PropKind.CELESTIAL_1` (param: key ordinal | phase << 8 | ticks since the cast << 16). Behaviour: `celestial/CelestialProps`:
steps out of the circle (14 ticks, clip `summon`), casts at tick 14 (clip `cast`, 1.2 s), keeps acting for as long as its move lasts (`CelestialArts.sustain`), stands (`idle`,
loop), steps back (`leave`). It ends when its owner dies, logs out or dismisses it.

Models: `geo/entity/celestial_<id>.geo.json` + `animations/entity/celestial_<id>.animation.json` + `textures/entity/celestial_<id>.png` and `_glow.png`, built by
`tools/celestial/gen_celestial_geo.py` (box UV, pivots on the joints, emissive glow map; checked with `tools/geo_check.py`; previewed with `tools/celestial/preview_celestial.py`).
Every spirit also has its own animated pieces: Aquarius' urn and tail, Taurus' axe, Leo's ring Regulus and mane of light, Virgo's chains, Sagittarius' horse body, bow and
arrow, Gemini's twins, Cancer's claws and shears, Scorpio's tail and sand, Capricorn's horns, Pisces' two orbiting fish, Aries' wool, Libra's floating scales, Horologium's
doors, pendulum and hands, Crux's turning cross, Lyra's harp and notes, Nikora's tail.

The magic circle (`CelestialPropPainter`): a zodiac wheel (two rings of ticks, twelve constellation cells, a dodecagram) turning slowly, a counter-turning rune ring, the
constellation of the spirit in the middle, a shaft of starlight while it steps out, a star glint at the cast, everything additive and full bright in the colour of the key
(`textures/particle/celestial_*.png`, made by `tools/celestial/gen_celestial_art.py`).

## 5. The Celestial Gate and the Spirit King

### Opening the Gate

Use the gate key while carrying all twelve gold keys **with their contracts**: a pillar of light, the Gate (a tall arched door of marble and gold, `celestial_gate` geo model, its
doors swing open, a keyhole of light, twelve stars orbiting a large circle) rises in front of you (`PropKind.CELESTIAL_2`, param 0) and, for 90 seconds, two clickable lines
appear in the chat (they run `/multiverse celestial gate challenge|recognition`):

* **[Challenge the Celestial Spirit King]** (option A): a fight in the Spirit World with the people near you (party mates, allies and members of your Mega-Dungeon party within 12 blocks, at most six).
* **[Ask for his recognition]** (option C): no fight. Earned when all twelve spirits are bound with a bond of at least 2, the skill is half mastered, and you were not already recognised.
* Option B, a temporary summon of the king, is the mode "Summon the Spirit King" (unlocked by a victory): 14 seconds, 40% of the maximum magicules, 20 minute cooldown: six waves of marked falling
  stars and, at the end, a Judgement (14 blocks).

Rules in `KingRules` (pure, unit tested): `canOpen`, `recognitionEarned`, `refightCooldown`, `health`, `partyDamage`, `phase`, `points`, `pick`, `reward`.

### The Spirit World (`SpiritWorld`)

Modelled on the pocket realms of the Mega-Dungeon (`labyrinth/mega/rift/PocketRealm`): a private cell per fight in its own dimension `nusmp:spirit_world` (a void under the star sky of the End,
night for ever; `data/nusmp/dimension*/spirit_world.json`), 256 blocks from the next, built on demand, tracked in saved data, collapsed when over (everyone is sent back where they came from, the
blocks and entities are removed). The cell: a marble disc 26 blocks in radius with a gold-and-white **safe circle** in the middle, twelve zodiac inlays, twelve pillars of starlight on the rim,
an invisible wall and roof, the way in on the west rim. At most 16 cells at once; a realm lasts 20 minutes.

### The boss (`SpiritKingEntity`)

Built on the shape of the Zogratis bosses and the Decennial bosses (`BudgetedBoss` per-second damage budget, boss bar, telegraphs, scaling), written for one arena:

* **Phases**: form 1 the Sovereign of the Gate (above 2/3 health), form 2 the Star-Crowned (above 1/3), form 3 the Celestial Ascendant (blows 25% harder, actions faster). Each phase has its own geo
  model (`celestial_king_p1..p3`: robe and stepped crown; a mantle of night and a halo of twelve star shards; gold plate, a nine-spike crown and the twelve keys floating round him), is announced, and gives the
  players a breath and a small mending.
* **Legendary actions**: 3/4/5 points per round (5 s), one more for a party of three or more; spent between the party's moves and in reaction to being hit (a blow hastens his next move): Star Fall (rings of stars
  on every player), Gate Slam (a ring you must **jump**), Constellation Bind (the farthest player rooted to a star), Zodiac Beam (twelve beams from the rim, each with a warning line), and in phase 3 the
  **Judgement** (a 3 second telegraph: only the gold circle in the middle is safe).
* **Lair actions** every 12 s: star tremor, spirit wind, the Gate's light (heals the party a little).
* **Legendary resistances**: he shrugs off his first three harmful effects, loudly.
* **Control and damage caps**: no blow takes more than 45% of a player's maximum health; hard control on the players is shortened by the balance law; spirits do 60% to him.
* **Scaling**: health `600 x EP scale (1-12)` at least 1200, +75% per extra player (up to six); blows `14 x sqrt(EP scale)` with +12% per extra player. The EP scale is the average of the party.
* **Re-fight cooldown per player**: 5 minutes after a defeat or a retreat (set when the fight starts), 1 hour after a victory.

### Rewards (`KingRewards`, claimed once per outcome per player who owns all twelve keys)

* **Victory**: +4 hearts of maximum health (permanent), +30% maximum magicules and aura (the large EP gain), bond +8 with all twelve spirits, a full refill, the **Sceptre of the Celestial Spirit King**
  (unique 3D item: Starlight Mend, heals you and allies within 8 blocks, 30 s cooldown, engraved holy / restoring / warding), and the modes Blessing and Summon the Spirit King.
* **Recognition**: +2 hearts, +12% maximum magicules and aura, bond +4, a half refill and the Blessing mode (the Ultimate-tier party buff: cleanse, regeneration II, resistance, speed, absorption III, 30 s, 5 minute cooldown).
* Both together add up. The permanent buff is put back every second from the record (transient attribute modifiers), so it survives death and relog. A helper without the collection is mended and thanked.

## 6. Where keys come from

* **Chests and vaults** of the Mega-Dungeon (`CelestialLootModifier`, global loot modifier `nusmp:celestial_keys`, added beside what the table gave): vault/treasure/relic tables 6%, other chests 1.2%.
* **Mobs killed by a player** in the Mega-Dungeon (`CelestialEvents.onDrops`): bosses 5%, mobs of a `celestial_court` sector 1%, any other hostile mob 0.15%; luck +10% per level, depth +1% per floor.
* One key per roll; gold keys only from floor 3; the gate key never drops; a key the player already has counts a quarter (the missing ones come first, duplicates stay possible).
* Admin: `/multiverse celestial give <player> <key|all|gold|silver|book|sceptre>`.

## 7. Commands

`/multiverse celestial give|bind|reset|king|gate challenge|recognition|open|leave|info` (give, bind, reset, king need permission level 2; the rest is for players).
`bind <player> <key|all|gold|silver>` seals the contracts in the record and in the keys the player carries; `reset` forgets everything; `king [player]` makes a test king beside the player.

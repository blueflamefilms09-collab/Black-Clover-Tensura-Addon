# Relics are wearable and shown all the time unless switched off (relics_wear)

Owner request: "also fix my relics so they are wearable and show on players all the time unless toggled off".

Nothing in this change could be run in a game. Everything below is read from the sources, type-checked against the API stubs and covered by pure tests; what only a running game can confirm is listed at the end.

## What was missing (root causes, with the evidence)

| # | Finding | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Five of the seven Curios slot types the mod uses were defined nowhere in the mod.** The relics of the neck, back, belt, hands and charm slots (607 relics in the wave sources) need the slot types `necklace`, `back`, `belt`, `hands`, `charm`. `data/nusmp/curios/entities/relics.json` hands exactly those five to the player, but `data/nusmp/curios/slots/` held only `dungeon_relic.json` and `celestial_key.json`. The mod relied on Curios to ship the other five; this was written down in `docs/relic_worn_fix.md` as "not verifiable here ... if a build of Curios does not ship them, relics cannot be equipped in those slots at all". No Curios jar is available in this environment (not in the gradle caches, not on disk; the mod reads Curios by reflection because its maven is out of reach), so whether Curios ships them could not be checked. | file listing of `src/main/resources/data/nusmp/curios/slots` before this change; `docs/relic_worn_fix.md` (table row "Curios slot types") |
| 2 | **No right-click equip for Curios relics.** `RelicGearItem.use` fired the relic's first ability (or showed the attunement hint); nothing put the item into a Curios slot. A relic could only be put on in the Curios screen, and only if the slot type existed (finding 1) and Curios accepted the tag. Curios' own right-click equip belongs to its item interface, which the mod cannot compile against. | `relics/RelicGearItem.java` `use`, `relics/RelicEngine.useHeld` |
| 3 | **The list a client keeps for another player's Curios relics was never dropped.** `WornSync` stored the server's list per wearer UUID and only cleared it when the wearer sent an empty list to players who were tracking at that moment, or when the world was left. A viewer who was out of range when the wearer took a relic off kept the old list and drew the relic again when the wearer came back into range (a "ghost" relic), and a login, respawn or change of dimension waited for the next 10-tick turn of the staggered check before the list was sent again. | `relics/WornSync.java` `put`, `onStartTracking`, `check` (before this change) |
| 4 | **A body form drawn by a renderer that is not a `PlayerModel` got no worn layer at all.** `RelicLayerGuard` attached `RelicWornLayer` only when the renderer's model was a `PlayerModel`; any other player-drawing renderer (another mod's body form with a plain `HumanoidModel`) logged a warning and drew no relic. | `client/relics/RelicLayerGuard.java` (before this change) |
| 5 | **The look at the local player is cached for 10 ticks**, also while the inventory is open. A relic put on in the inventory or the Curios screen appeared on the preview and the paper doll up to half a second later. | `relics/Relics.cached`, `Relics.ttl()` |
| 6 | **There was no switch for "all worn relics"** (only the Curios eye of one slot, and `relicfx.hideArmourRelics` for armour). | `client/relics/fx/RelicFxOptions.java` |
| 7 | **Every fallback was silent.** A relic without a model, one found only in the server's list, a renderer that got the layer late, a slot type the player does not have: nothing said so. | `RelicWornLayer`, `RelicLayerGuard`, `Relics` |

**What was not found.** The report that head, chest and feet relics do not show on the player and in the inventory preview (1.110/1.111 builds) could not be traced to one line. The 1.111 analysis (`docs/relics/armor_visible.md`) read the whole armour path and found nothing that explains it alone, and a second reading for this change found the same: the layer is added to both skins, the guard covers a renderer the event missed, the empty-model hook is decided each frame by the same plan, the painted textures are checked after every reload. What this change adds is (a) the fixes above for everything that could make a relic not wearable or not drawn, (b) one definition of when a worn relic may be missing (below), checked against the code that really draws, and (c) named causes: if a relic is still missing in the game, `latest.log` now has a `[nusmp] worn relic: <id> <cause>` line and `/multiverse relic debug` prints the facts per relic (slot, equipped, slot defined, tag, synced, visible, reason hidden).

## The visibility contract (`client/relics/worn/WornVisibility`)

A worn relic, Curios or armour, is drawn on every player: you in third person, other players, the inventory preview entity and the paper doll of the Curios screen (they are all a living player drawn by a player renderer, so the same layer serves them). It is NOT drawn only when:

1. the wearer is a spectator or invisible;
2. the player switched all worn relics off: `relicfx.showWorn` (new), `/multiverse relic show <on|off|toggle>`, the key "Show or hide all worn relics" (unbound by default);
3. Curios relic: the eye icon of its slot in the Curios screen is off;
4. armour relic: `relicfx.hideArmourRelics` is on (a head or boots relic is hidden whole; a chest relic loses its 3D ornaments and glow but keeps the painted chest piece, as before: pinned by `ArmorSlotPlanTest.theArmourSwitchHidesTheHelmetAndBootsButNotTheChestBody`);
5. distance: a Curios relic is not drawn beyond 32 blocks from the camera (`WornFxPlan.CULL_RANGE`); an armour relic keeps its painted armour layer at any distance, only its 3D ornaments are culled;
6. first person: a head armour relic is not drawn from inside the wearer's own head (it would block the camera); everything else is unaffected, and a relic with a renderer of its own handles the camera itself.

`relicfx.models` off is not a reason: Curios relics fall back to plain boxes, armour relics to the painted armour. A missing model, a failed Curios read on this client, a late renderer and a stale cache are not reasons either; each has a fallback and says its cause once.

`WornVisibility.checkTable()` walks every combination (source x slot x eye x custom renderer x hideArmour x showWorn x view x distance x spectator x invisible, about six thousand, each also run through the plan for both values of "has a model" and "models on/off") and compares the contract with a plain list of the hide conditions and with `WornPlan` / `ArmorSlotPlan`, which are what the layers use, for both values of "has a model" and "models on/off". The layers cannot drift from the contract without `WornVisibilityTest` and the self-test failing. (Finding while writing it: the player's own body can never be "far" from its own camera; that combination is skipped.)

## Cost (no lag)

* Client, per frame per drawn player: one config read (`showWorn`), a loop over the player's worn relics (a handful) doing a set lookup for each fallback cause (allocation free after the first time), the same plan and draw as before. `WornVisibility.decide` allocates nothing (one result per reason) and is used by the debug report and the tests, not per frame. Pinned by a source scan.
* Client, per tick: one comparison and one volatile write. Per living entity that leaves the level: one map removal.
* Local player's look while a screen is open: at most 10 Curios reads per second (one wearer, only while a screen is open and for 1 s after). Other players keep the 10-tick look.
* Server: one new packet type, `RelicShowPayload` (one byte, only when a player runs `/multiverse relic show`, only to that player); no other new packet and no new per-tick work. Right-click equip reads the Curios inventory once per right-click of a Curios relic. A login, respawn or change of dimension makes one extra look at that player (one `scanExtra`) and at most one list packet to nearby players; the pending set is capped at 4096 and emptied at logout. The list is still sent only when it changed, only to players tracking the wearer (and the wearer), at most 40 stacks.
* Worst case on a 100-player server: the same packets as before plus at most 3 extra looks per player per session event; the table of causes is capped at 96 strings.

## Unverified (nothing visual or networked could be run)

* Whether Curios for NeoForge 1.21.1 ships `necklace`, `back`, `belt`, `hands`, `charm` itself, and exactly how it merges a second slot file with the same id from another namespace. The new files use the sizes and icons Curios is believed to use (hands 2 on purpose, so a merge can never leave fewer places than a Curios default of 2), and leave out `operation` and `replace`. If the log shows a Curios warning about these files, report the line; do not delete them.
* That the Curios icons `curios:slot/empty_<slot>_slot` exist for all five (the generic `empty_curio_slot` is the safe one; a missing icon is cosmetic only).
* That `RightClickItem` is fired for a relic item in the hand before `Item.use` and that cancelling it on the server is enough for the client (the client may play no arm swing on equip).
* That the packet reaches the client of the player who ran `/multiverse relic show` (it uses the same registrar as the other relic packets). The client-side `/nusmp relic show` and the key do not depend on it.
* Shift-click equip into Curios slots: Curios' container behaviour, not changed or testable here.
* That Tensura race forms or GeckoLib-wrapped players reach `RelicLayerGuard` (it needs `RenderLivingEvent.Pre` to fire for their renderer). A body form drawn without that event, or with a model that is not humanoid, cannot get the layer; the log names the renderer when the event is seen.
* The visual result on a real head, chest, feet, back, belt, hands, charm and neck, in third person, in the inventory preview and on the Curios paper doll.
* The real cause of "armour relics invisible" on the owner's setup (see "What was not found"); after this build, `latest.log` lines starting `[nusmp] worn relic:` and `/multiverse relic debug` name it.

## How each one LOOKS (what the models must be)

The items are plain gold. The old models in the mod (3.x and before) added lapis bands, pharaoh circlets, orbiting motes, halos, fate wheels and hieroglyph shafts that none of the seven have. The remake is
canon silhouette first, all gold, with the Eye of Wdjat (the Wedjat, a stylised eye with a brow line, a tear line and a curl) as the only recurring motif, and the glow limited to the eye itself.

| Item | Silhouette (from the wiki images and the anime) |
|---|---|
| Puzzle | An inverted golden pyramid (a flat square top, narrowing to a point at the bottom), a small round loop on the top corner/edge that carries a thin chain or cord, the Eye of Wdjat in relief in the centre of the front face, and engraved bands/ledges running along the faces and edges (the puzzle's interlocking panels), a spiral at the lower right of the face. No blue stone. |
| Eye | A flat round golden disc (like a coin or a lens) with a raised rim, a raised eyeball with a pupil in the centre, and a small eye-lash/brow flourish with the tear line; two small curls/loops on top. It is worn on the brow/forehead (HEAD slot): the disc sits on the forehead on a thin gold band that goes round the head. |
| Ring | A golden hoop (a ring about as wide as the hand) with a golden inverted-pyramid (triangle) fixed inside it, carrying the Eye of Wdjat on its face, a small loop on the top of the hoop for the cord, and FIVE long golden needle-spikes hanging from the lower part of the hoop (the dowsing needles: the centre one longest, the outer ones shorter, tapering to points). |
| Scale | A golden balance: a standing post on a flat base, a horizontal beam across the top (with a small hoop/eye ornament at the top of the post), two circular shallow dishes hanging from three short chains each, one at each end. No feather. |
| Key | A golden ankh (a loop on top of a cross) as a key: tall, thin, flat, with a plain loop, a long straight shaft, a short crossbar, and the Eye of Wdjat engraved near the top of the shaft; a small ring at the very top/back for the cord. Worn hung from the belt. |
| Rod | A long golden rod/staff. At the top: a crescent-shaped head with two curved horns/blades turned up and out (a lunate shape like a pair of horns or a winged crescent), the Eye of Wdjat in a round setting in the middle of the crescent, a short pointed finial above it; the shaft is slender with a ring/collar below the head; the bottom ends in a sharp spike. (Anime: the Eye sits in the crescent, with a spike on top.) |
| Necklace | A band of gold shaped like a collar-ring (a thin open torc or a wide collar) with a gold plate in the middle holding the Eye of Wdjat, two small outward-curled ends, and a thin chain behind the neck. No lapis, no beads. |

## What each one DOES in the mod (3.35.0)

Existing acts that match the wiki are kept (rule 2). New acts are added where the wiki names a power the items did not have. Acts that were invented (not in the wiki or the anime) are reworded to the
nearest canon power; none of the old ids were removed from the registry. The Millennium Items stay unattuned (all seven can be worn together) and the set bonus stays.

| Item | Wiki power | In the mod |
|---|---|---|
| Puzzle | Atem's soul; summons and controls Duel Monsters; mastery of games; power of darkness | Second soul takes a deadly blow (kept). Acts: Other Me (Atem takes the body, kept), Shadow Game (kept, the power of darkness), NEW Duel Monster (summons one spectral Duel Monster ally for a short time, bounded). |
| Eye | Reads minds; looks into a soul; seals souls in cards; visions of the dead | Mind Read (kept), Truth Gaze = looks into the soul (kept, reworded), NEW Seal in a Card (a foe is held in a soul card for a few seconds), NEW Sight of the Dead (the dead and the dying near you are shown; undead are marked). |
| Ring | Compass for what is sought; seals souls or soul fragments into objects; holds the souls of Zorc and Bakura | Dowsing Needles (kept, the compass), Draw Power (kept, reworded: draws the power of souls), NEW Seal a Soul (a weak mob's soul is sealed in the Ring: it is removed and its power feeds the wearer, bounded). |
| Rod | Mind control; seals spirit monsters in stone tablets | Mind Control, Pharaoh's Command (kept), Judgement Strike (kept), NEW Seal in a Tablet (a mob is sealed in stone for a while). |
| Key | Enters a person's mind; redecorates the soul to make a puppet | Open the Soul Room (kept: entering the mind), Mind Garden (kept), NEW Redecorate the Soul (one mob becomes the wielder's puppet for a long time, bounded). The invented fear/sleep immunity passive is reworded as the Key's guard of its own soul. |
| Scale | Weighs the darkness in a heart; fuses monsters | Weigh the Heart (kept, reworded: weighs darkness), Ammit's Verdict (kept), NEW Fuse Monsters (foes near are pulled together and fused: one blast, bounded). |
| Necklace | Sees the past and the future | Foresight (kept: the future), Prophecy (kept), NEW Sight of the Past (whoever last hurt you is shown and marked). |

Carriers are shown in each item's tooltip lore (old and recent, short).

## Cost

No new per-tick work. The new acts run once per use inside the shared `SafeCast` guard, take foes only from `Fx.foesAround` (radius clamped), cap every count (at most 5 foes, one summon per wearer,
one puppet per wearer), and use the existing VFX shapes and per-act cooldowns and daily-use limits. Models: each worn model stays within the Genesis visual-upgrade caps (the bake refuses
anything over); the remade models are SMALLER than the old ones (no orbiting motes or halos), so the draw cost goes down.

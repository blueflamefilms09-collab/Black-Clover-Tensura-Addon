# Relics of Many Worlds, second set: wave 12 (ten hand-held weapons)

All ten are `RelicSlot.MAIN_HAND` weapons (`RelicWeaponItem`), all real 3D models in hand (`model3d()`), none a Djinn vessel (none is LEGEND), all carry Tensura
engravings (`.engrave`). Attunement only on the Warmage's Longsword. The first act is the right-click, the second one sneak + right-click. The weapon's
printed die damage became the `.melee(damage, speed)` figure of the def (tooltip total, the player's own 1 included).
Code: `relics/wave/Wave12.java` (defs, `hooks()`), `Wave12Hooks` (the game-bus listeners), `Wave12Heavy` (Jawblade, Lumberjack), `Wave12Light` (towel, blades,
hook swords), `Wave12Magic` (Warmage, Crystal, Bottle, Stabshroom, Dagger), `Wave12Rules` (the pure, unit-tested numbers), `Wave12Data` (state). Item state
(missiles, crystal charges and night, the bottle's water) is in the stack's custom data under `nusmp_w12`; the wielder's short timers (jaw charges, wet towel,
crystal arm, climb counter, dagger blast) are in the player's persisted data under the same key. No static holds a level, entity or player. These are counters

## Tabletop rules that were replaced
- Ability checks (Arcana, Nature, History, Medicine, Religion, Bluff) -> the d4-1 is rolled for the pages read and decides the length and strength of a real buff; Bluff -> a d20+3 contest against each target's resolve (10 to 18 by max health).

- Hit points -> health points (0.4 per D&D point for Life's Emergency); AC -> durability (urn); 20 feet -> 12 blocks; "up to an hour" -> 5 minutes (relic allies are capped there).
- Pairs of items (Transferrence, Shared Pouches) -> relics are one of a kind (the Order), so the pair is one relic plus a linked companion; the second bag is reached with /pouch.
- Wild Magic Table -> an own d20 table. Cut-off cards (Crystal Dragon, Bag of Realistic Capacity): legible part played, remainder invented.

Classes: `relics/wave/Wave19` (defs + hooks), `Wave19Rules` (pure numbers and tables, tested), `Wave19Scrolls`, `Wave19Goods`, `Wave19Book`, `Wave19Surge` (the wild magic table), `Wave19Field` (charms and the crab), `Wave19Events` (handlers), `Wave19Util`. Icons: `tools/relics/wave19_icons.py`. Tests: `Wave19RulesTest`. Lang: `frag_relics2/relics_wave19.lang.json` (name, .desc, .card for each id).

Time scale: a long rest is a Minecraft day (24000 ticks), a tabletop hour is twenty minutes, a tabletop twenty minutes is about seven, a round is six seconds. A consumable that finds nothing to work on is handed back (the act refunds the stack). Fairness: nothing instantly kills, creative and spectator players are never targeted, damage on players goes through the relics' 30%/45% cap.

New stats: none. Magicules (Spellbook/Surge/Mug Wizard) run on Tensura's magicule storage (`TensuraStorages.getExistenceFrom`, `EnergyHelper.getMaxMagicule`); all skills are lent through `Fx.lendSkill(s)` (the MealSkills lease). Counters (uses per day, bookmark, mug title, facsimile generation) are plain stack data / persistent data.

Tensura ids used (all in clean.json): skills magic_bullet, magic_barrier, thought_acceleration, magic_sense, analytical_appraisal, magic_aura, thought_communication, sage, poison_resistance; effect fear; attribute max_magicule; entities undine, armorsaurus; enchantments holy_weapon, holy_coat; entities falmuth_knight, hinata_sakaguchi, lesser/greater/arch_daemon, zombie, skeleton, bone_golem. The damage type tensura:holy_damage is run-time gated (falls back to minecraft:magic).

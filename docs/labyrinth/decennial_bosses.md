# The 200 Decennial bosses (round 1.99.0)

## Why

The Mega-Dungeon puts a boss on every tenth floor. Until 1.98.0 all of them were "the Labyrinth Guardian" with a D&D profile of their tier. The owner asked for a boss per level of ten floors
on both roads (100 Holy at levels 1..100, 100 Evil at -1..-100; level L stands on floor 10*L), each with its own identity, phases, lair actions, loot and a thematic, hardcoded hall.
Everything reuses the Guardian framework (no new entity type, block, item or data component).

## Canon followed

Tree of Qliphoth: the Evil half is seven gate bands (gates at levels 14, 28, 42, 57, 71, 85, 97, halls `qliphoth_gate_1..7`, root-tendril nodes that spawn lesser devils, black-sap lair action),
ten named devils stationed across the bands (Walgner 14, Nahamah 28, Lilith 42, Astaroth 57, Beelzebub 71, Liebe 85, Ovrazel 97) and the three Supreme Devils on the `qliphoth_root_throne`
(Megicula -98, Zagred -99, Lucifero -100). After each gate the next ten floors wear a darker, rooted existing theme (`UnderworldRoot`, through `MegaDungeon.themeOverride`; a Crucible relic still wins).
Roster details and the 60+ archetype list: `docs/labyrinth/decennial_roster.md`.

## Numbers

Phases 2 to 4 (2 up to level 20, 3 up to 70, 4 above); 1 or 2 lair actions; shift = 2 s invulnerable; lair telegraph at least 16 ticks, share at most 0.6 of base attack, always capped by
`BalanceLaw.cap`; blindness at most 2 s; a root at most 1.5 s and halved when repeated; phase damage at most x1.6, speed x0.6..x1.35; adds at most 14 alive, 48 per fight, 6 per wave, with the share
the later phases need reserved. Loot grade by level: 1-12 special, 13-30 rare, 31-52 unique, 53-75 legend, 76-92 god, 93-100 genesis. NO hunger effect exists anywhere (the effect list is closed).

## How it works

* Floors: `DecennialArenaHook` (a `SectorListener`) plans the hall over the arena of a boss floor (same 45 footprint, turned to the Gatekeeper's side, doorways and altar column reserved) and
  `DecennialArenas.stepBuilds` writes it in steps (progress saved in `nusmp_decennial`). Standalone halls (41-61) come from `DecennialBosses.spawnBoss`.
* Elements: breakable only through `DungeonGuard.allowBreakWhere` (server set + client packet `nusmp:decennial_breakables`); tombstones/root nodes spawn adds from the budget, pillars topple on a
  strip, vessels shatter; reset restores them. The entry is sealed with iron bars while a party is inside; it opens on boss death, wipe (`wipeSeconds`), vanished boss or `sealMaxMinutes`.
* Loot: `DecennialLoot` ranks catalog relics of the level's grade (kind, weapon type, keywords); paid once per hall through the Order's ledger (owner-requested exception, config `bossRelic`).
  Admin-called bosses pay nothing.
* Facade: `com.newuniverse.nusmp.labyrinth.mega.decennial.DecennialBosses` (`spawnBoss`, `killBosses`, `describe`, `levels`).
* Previews: `python tools/arena_preview.py` -> `docs/previews/arenas/`.

## Not verified (honest list)

Nothing could be run in a game here. Verified: JUnit (roster, phase machine, 36 plans, frame, loot, themes), type-check against API stubs with a negative control. NOT verified in game:
the placer's output in a real floor (the self-test `boss.decennial_arena` is meant to prove it), template doorway positions assumed from the hall template (Gatekeeper north, entry south, altar at floor+3),
the fight feel and balance of lair actions, client-side crack start for elements, `tint` and `sound_set` are written (synced tint, `nusmp_sound_set` data) but no renderer/audio reads them yet,
Holy bosses use the existing abyssal looks. An arc boss that hosts a Guardian place (`ArcFloorBosses`) still replaces the Decennial boss on its floors.

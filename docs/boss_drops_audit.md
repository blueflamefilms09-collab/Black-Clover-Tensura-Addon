# Boss drops audit: every boss and what it drops today

Owner request: "all the bosses in my mod should actually have worthwhile boss drops, like custom drops, one-of-one drops, etc." This is step 1: what each boss drops BEFORE the worthwhile boss drops
(`docs/boss_drops_worthwhile.md`). Date of the audit: the state of the repository at the start of that change.

**How it was made.** By reading the code, the data files and the older docs (`docs/bosses/boss_drops.md`, `docs/boss_cores.md`, `docs/dungeon_bosses_audit.md`). Nothing was seen in a running game, so a drop that
is made in some code path I did not find is not in this list. No wiki was fetched (the bosses are the mod's own; trophy and signature names are invented from each character's canon).

**Where a boss's drops come from today** (the five things that exist):

1. The **Canon (Charter) Grimoire** (`charter/CharterEvents`): 30 bosses (the 7 older bosses, 22 arc bosses and Zagred). A first-ever kill, the boss's own chance (25 to 40 percent for the older bosses, 30 percent for Zagred), world-locked: one copy per world.
2. The **boss drop framework** (`bossdrops/`, docs/bosses/boss_drops.md): 23 bosses pay a generic trophy (1 to 2), Tensura silver coins and a first-kill gold coin; Lucifero also drops his armour pieces. Only the last-blow killer is paid.
3. The boss's **own code** (`dropCustomDeathLoot`): Goku, Kakashi, Light Yagami and the 16 tier-list bosses drop a few items; the Floor Warden has a small loot table.
4. The **dungeon** (`DecennialReward`, `BossCoreAward`, the coin loot of `LootVictims`): the Decennial and sector Guardians of the Mega-Dungeon.
5. The **arc story** (`ArcStory`, `ArcWorldCredit`): story credit and the saga reward (a book), not an item drop of the boss.

## Verdicts

* **NOTHING WORTHWHILE**: the boss drops nothing, or only currency / renamed vanilla food / a grimoire once. 28 bosses.
* **THIN**: it drops something, but nothing that belongs to the boss, nothing for a group and no one-of-one. 24 bosses.
* **OK**: it already has a real boss-specific drop (Lucifero's armour, the Decennial relics and cores). 2 bosses.

## What the audit found

* **28 of 54 bosses drop nothing worth a fight**: the six older Zogratis-family bosses with only a once-per-world grimoire (Dante, Zenon, Lucius, the Devil Avatar, Goliath, the Charybdis Spawn), Zagred (grimoire only), the old Zagred,
  Sinbad, the God of Order, the Floor Warden (emeralds and gold), Light Yagami (renamed potatoes) and the 16 tier-list bosses (a few Magic Stones).
* **24 are thin**: the 22 grimoire bosses that pay a generic trophy plus coins (Vanica among them), Goku and Kakashi (stones, coins and an existing charm).
* **Nobody has a one-of-one**, and a group fight pays one person (the last blow), not the people who did the work.
* Bosses that are not in the list because they are not bosses of this mod: Tensura and vanilla bosses, the Dark Disciples, the Gatekeeper, Blood Beasts and clones (followers). A boss made by a director on a Tensura body (a tagged mob,
  `BossIdentity`) is covered only by the `default` fallback of the worthwhile change.

## What could not be checked

* A drop produced by a code path I did not find (a loot modifier on another mod's tables, a quest reward fired on a boss kill) is not listed; the list is "what the code I read does".
* The Charter chances of the 22 arc bosses are each boss's own row in its roster; they are not repeated here.
* Nothing was run in a game; the CI self-tests of the older frameworks (`bossdrops.*`, `cores.*`, `arc.charter`) are what prove they work.

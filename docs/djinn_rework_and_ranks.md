# Djinn design audit and relic-grade ranks: what changed

Owner request: look through every Djinn design, rework any that is a copy of another, make look, abilities and weapons match the aspect; and show every rank or rarity with the relic grades (stars, grade, place), not custom text.
Full lists: `docs/djinn_design_audit.md` (generated by `python tools/djinn/design_audit.py`, `--check` fails on a duplicate) and `docs/rank_audit.md`.

## Part A: the Djinn design audit

### What was checked (all 36 Djinn, every layer)
Armour mesh sets (`u_<id>`, `d_<id>`, the shared fallback rig), armour skins and glow maps, rune, Lesser Summon bust (light and dark), dark art, weapon meshes and skins, seal and weapon icons, the tiny Djinn models,
palettes (in Lab colour space), the ambient particle rows, the element concept table, the Extreme Magic damage concept (`djinn_extreme_types.json`), and each asset's hue against the Djinn's element.

### Found
* **Duplicates, fixed:** 9 pairs of light busts that were the same picture (agares/balam, baal/haagenti, baal/vinea, bael/alloces, buer/furcas, focalor/amdusias, furfur/alloces, furfur/bael, vinea/haagenti); all 36 dark busts (one black figure, only the gem differed); tiny Vepar was a near copy of tiny Vinea (9 of 11 design slots equal) and tiny Barbatos of tiny Zagan (8 of 11).
* **Concept mismatch, fixed:** Focalor (Wind) had a "water" Extreme Magic damage concept; it is now "wind" (`djinn_extreme_types.json`, `docs/damage_scale_down.md` row 7).
* **Looked at and accepted, with the reason in the audit:** Camio and Kimaris skin hues (crow black, dark plum), Amdusias weapon skin (an ivory horn), Baal/Andrealphus and Paimon/Vepar palette twins (the element is in the motifs, VFX and weapon), four Extreme concepts (Camio, Kimaris, Haagenti, Vinea) that are right for the lore.
* **By design, not touched:** the shared fallback rig (`djinn/<id>`) is one template and is never drawn while `u_<id>` exists. The GUI medallion silhouette (horns, wings, ears) is shared by some Djinn; no two share horns, wings, tail and halo.
* Nothing duplicated in: the unique and dark armour mesh sets, weapon meshes, armour skins, seals, weapon skins and icons.

### Honest note on Blender (rule 14)
Blender is not installed in this environment, so no model was made there. The busts are 32x32 pixel art drawn by the existing Python generators, and the two tiny Djinn use the existing tiny-Djinn model generator (`tools/tiny_djinn`). The armour and weapon meshes were not remodelled: the audit found no duplicates in them.

## Part B: ranks as relic grades

Every rank, tier or rarity the player reads is listed in `docs/rank_audit.md` with file and line. The player now reads the relic card line (stars, grade, place, in the grade colour) in: the rank line of every ranked item,
Beast Will cores and the Beast Will panel, the Devil panel, the Saiyan panel, the Magic Knight status block, loot gear, Soul Forge aptitudes, Mana Cores. The mapping tables (beast SS is God Grade, and so on) and the list of what was
left alone (a state, a look, a balance letter that the player never reads) are in that file.

### Plain rules
1. A rank the player reads is stars, then the grade name, then the place, in the grade colour. Nothing is written as custom text.
2. A new rank or rarity is added to `rankings.json` (item, rule, or `legacy`), never as a text of its own.
3. The same letter is the same grade everywhere (beast S and Mana Core S are both Legend).

## Cost (rule 11)
Tooltips and panels only: no tick, no packet, no entity, no server state. The card text is a few string concatenations when a tooltip opens, or once a second / twice a second / per 10 ticks when a panel rebuilds its lines;
the draw per frame is one `drawString` of an already built string (the colour is built with the line). Textures: 43 busts of 32x32 and 8 small tiny-Djinn PNGs, loaded on first use like the old ones.

## Caveats
* Not compiled with Gradle and not seen in a game in this session. What did run: the audit script (0 duplicates, 0 mismatches), a stand-alone compile and run of the real `Rankings`, `Grade`, `WeaponGrades`, `GradeDamage` classes against the real `rankings.json` (all nine mapping tables resolve, in order), the tiny Djinn generator `--check`.
* The new JUnit tests (`RankCardsTest`, `DjinnDesignReworkTest`) and the whole test sweep (rule 12) have to run in CI.
* The panels (Devil, Saiyan, Beast Will) grew by one line when the card shows; the layout was not seen in the game.
* Decisions left: whether the two chat lines ("Promoted: ...", Saiyan command output) should also show the card; whether Mana Core letters should get eight separate grades (SS would be EX-Singularity).

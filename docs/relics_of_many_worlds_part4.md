## New stats
None. Every counter is a stack or persistent-data tag (day allowances, carnage stacks, hover/grapple timers); nothing is a new pool or meter.

## Rules honoured
No vanilla particles (only `RukhFx`, DARK for shovel/carnage/raven/dark pits, LIGHT otherwise); no Better Combat files (`.melee(...)` on every weapon, the lead runs the profile tools); the Labyrinth and Dungeon are never edited (Evil Gardening refuses there, block changes inside `DungeonGuard.internal`); creative/spectator are never targeted (`Fx.foesAround`/`Wave20Util.foesNear`); damage goes through `Fx.damage` (capped on players); nothing instantly kills; Soulgate and Deflection protect the wielder only; boss-sized foes (> 200 HP) are never pulled or rooted and always count as having saved against Toll the Dead; no regeneration on summons (none are summoned); no recipes or loot entries.

## Shared mechanics
* Charm (`Wave26Mind.charm`): the creature forgets its target, never targets the charmer (target-change event is cancelled), fights the charmer's other foes, and is released when the time is up, on logout or at server stop. Bosses (c:bosses or 200+ health) and pets are immune; a creature with more than 3x the caster's maximum EP saves; players are only frightened for 5 s. Nothing here kills or heals.
* Curse upkeep is staggered (once a second per player, carrying scan every 2 s) and leaves at once for players without any of the wave's flags. Per-session maps (carry mask, lens ledger, charm list, Frank's rabbit) are cleared on logout and server stop; every timer lives in the player's persistent data.
* No block is ever changed (the Goggles only read); safe in the Labyrinth. No vanilla particles: Rukh only.

## Tensura ids used (all from `clean.json`; skills and effects are lent or applied through the run-time registries, an absent id does nothing)
Skills: `tensura:eye_of_truth`, `farsight`, `danger_sense`, `poison_resistance`, `paralysis_resistance`, `spatial_motion`, `thought_communication`, `analytical_appraisal`.
Effects: `tensura:fear`, `frost`, `future_vision`, `presence_concealment`, `confusion` (the poison-family ids removed by the pair: `magicule_poison`, `fatal_poison`, `infection`, `paralysis`).
Attributes: `tensura:max_magicule`, `max_aura`, `magicule_regeneration_multiplier`, `height_multiplier`, `width_multiplier`. Engravings: stagnation, magic_interference, sealing (Paradox).
Items: `tensura:magic_stone` (Ruin). Verified in the id list; the in-game behaviour of `frost`, `fear`, `confusion`, `future_vision`, the size multipliers on mobs was not tested.

## New stats
None. Every pool the Stones touch is Tensura's (magicules, aura, regeneration); counters (charges, uses, souls, stars) are plain counters on the stack or in the player's persistent data. The Deck's Star gift is a small permanent attribute modifier re-applied from a saved counter.

## Not played / replaced by design (honest list)
Alignment; the Hand and Eye "removal results in death" and "destroyed only by the Sword of Kas" and the teleport to a vault; spell save DCs for unwilling Space Stone passengers (consent instead); free-text questions to the Mind Stone; polymorphing into another creature or object; the Soul Stone's resurrection of others; Paradox's sentience and vanishing; the Gauntlet's "no other attunement" (the standard cap of three stays); any Greater Wish beyond the five listed.

## Operator
`/vecna release <player>` (permission level 2) frees a bearer's grafted Hand and Eye (the curse of binding is stripped from the Eye). It is registered by `Wave30Bond` itself through `RegisterCommandsEvent`: nothing for the lead to add.

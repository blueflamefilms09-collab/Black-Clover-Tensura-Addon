## The shape of the whole thing, and why

**A Gate on the surface, a Spire in its own sky.** The Overworld only ever receives the *Gate*: a small Jigsaw structure (`nusmp:dungeon_gate`)
that the structure set `nusmp:dungeon_gates` scatters over surface biomes, pinned to the terrain by `project_start_to_heightmap: WORLD_SURFACE_WG`
and `terrain_adaptation: beard_thin`. Everything else lives in `nusmp:labyrinth`, a dimension whose generator is `minecraft:flat` with no layers
(a void) and `structure_overrides: ["nusmp:labyrinth_spires"]`. Why a void: a Jigsaw tower a hundred blocks tall has nothing to fight — no cave
to cut it, no mountain to bury its vault, no water to flood a trial — and the "sea of clouds" is the SAO look for free. Why its own dimension
rather than editing the legacy one: a world that already exists keeps its dimensions' generators in `level.dat`; a changed JSON would apply to new
worlds only, and the owner's rule 2 says the old content stays loadable. So the legacy dimension, its blocks, mobs and systems stay registered and
untouched; only the *routing* changes: Maws and `/multiverse dungeon enter` now lead to the Labyrinth while `labyrinth.engine = "labyrinth"`,
and `"legacy"` puts them back.

**Why everything in the Labyrinth is data.** Structures, pools, processor lists, loot tables, the biome palette, the boss roster of every altar,
the mob list of every trial: all of it is JSON or template NBT, generated by the Python tools in `tools/labyrinth/` and regenerated by one command.
A server owner or a pack author changes a weight, a mob list or a palette without touching Java; other mods' rooms snap in through the wildcard
pool. The Java is only what data cannot do: three blocks with behaviour (archway, altar, pedestal), one door, one key, one mini-boss, two
processors and the travel code.

**Why the progression cannot be skipped.** The whole dimension is under the same guard as the legacy dungeon (`DungeonGuard`, switch
`labyrinth.unbreakable`): no mining, no explosions, no pistons, no mob griefing. The Gatekeeper doors and the barriers are bedrock-hard on top of
that. The only ways up are the ones the designer left: clear the trial, kill the Warden, turn the key, kill the floor's master, open the vault.

**Why relics are exclusive now.** Every path that used to put a Relic of Many Worlds into the world outside a dungeon is gated by
`labyrinth.relicsExclusive` (default true): the global loot modifier only fires for the vault tables, the mimic drop only inside a dungeon
dimension, and a unit test fails the build if a recipe or any other loot table names a relic. The ledger (uniqueness) is untouched: the vault rolls
through the same `rollUnissuedAll` as before, so no relic can exist twice.

## The Mega-Dungeon (1.54.0) and its two roads (1.55.0)

The Spire above stays registered, but the default dungeon is now ONE persistent dimension, `nusmp:mega_dungeon`, built from vanilla blocks as a tightly packed tabletop-style layout of sectors that grows when a sector's Labyrinth Guardian is cleared. 1.55.0 puts Floor 0 (a safe, buildable Sanctuary Hub, its own dimension `nusmp:sanctuary`) in front of it and splits the growth into two roads: Holy floors +1, +2 ... and Abyssal floors -1, -2 ..., with a Directional Lock once a road is chosen.

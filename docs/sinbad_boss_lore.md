# Sinbad (Magi) - lore reference for a Minecraft boss

Source: `wiki/sinbad.json` (Magi Fandom pages Sinbad, Djinn, Djinn Equip, Household Vessel, Baal, Zepar, Focalor, Vepar, Crocell, Valefor, Furfur; plus omniversal-battlefield "Sinbad (Magi)"). A "Sitri" key exists but is empty. All text below is paraphrased. Spell names are kept exactly as the wiki spells them.

## 1. Sinbad himself

### Identity and titles
- Aliases listed: Sin, Sinbad of the Seven Seas, High King of the Seven Seas, Sinbad the Sailor, Lady Killer of the Seven Seas, First Class Singularity, World Innovator, King Vessel, plus joke names ("Stupid King", "Uncle Sinbad").
- Roles: former King of Sindria, former leader of the Seven Seas Alliance, CEO of Sindria Trading Company, Head of the International Alliance, and finally Guardian of the Sacred Palace (after he takes it over from Ugo).
- He was the first person to capture more than one Dungeon. With 7 djinn he has more than anyone else in the series. After the 7th, the djinn barred him and his Household from entering any more dungeons "for the sake of balance".
- He is "half fallen into depravity", so he can use both white and black Rukh/Magoi. He says he is not David's reincarnation, but David's will resonates with the black Rukh inside him, and he uses that link to pull knowledge from Alma Torran.
- Based on Sindbad the Sailor from One Thousand and One Nights.

### Look (boss model reference)
- Adult: 183 cm, 83 kg, tall, muscular, tan skin, golden eyes, very long purple hair in a ponytail, one long upright ahoge (cowlick).
- Floor-length purple and white robes, white turban with feathers, pointed red shoes, hoop earrings (worn since childhood).
- Lots of jewelry. Most of it is his Metal Vessels (see below). Easy, confident smile.
- In his youth he fought with a scimitar.
- Personality cues for boss lines: laid-back, flirtatious, charismatic. Underneath he is arrogant, calculating and manipulative, and believes he alone sees the "flow of destiny" and should hold the world's reins. He hates Al-Thamen and kills its members without remorse. Weakness listed in trivia: alcohol.

### Metal Vessels: which object holds which djinn
| Djinn | Vessel | Dungeon |
|---|---|---|
| Baal | Sword (inherited from his father Badr, handed to him by his mother Esra) | 1st |
| Valefor | Gold necklace with a red jewel at the center | 6th |
| Zepar | Ring on his (right) middle finger, with a small Seal of Solomon | 16th |
| Furfur | Silver necklace | 34th |
| Focalor | Silver bracelet, right arm | 41st |
| Vepar | Ring on his index finger | 42nd |
| Crocell | Gold bracelet, left arm | 49th |

That makes 1 sword + 2 bracelets + 2 rings + 2 necklaces (gold and silver). He can full Djinn Equip with all seven. Every Metal Vessel carries an eight-pointed star that glows when it is in use.

### Physical and combat abilities
- Extremely skilled swordsman, decent martial artist. Stats template on his page reads 5/5/4/5/5/4 (labels not given in the raw data).
- **Magoi Manipulation** (listed as his "special skill"): he uses the Magoi in his own body to cancel an opponent's Magoi, negating their magic to a degree. It wears his body down quickly, so it cannot be kept up for long. He learned it in one year of training with the Yambala Gladiators in the mountains. Shown when he cancels Kougyoku's magic and when Cassim fails to grab him.
- **Black Rukh**: being half-fallen lets him absorb black Rukh, e.g. soaking up Ithnan's curse magic. It also lets a non-Extreme spell of his counter Kougyoku's Extreme Magic. It comes "at a heavy price", and he dislikes using it.
- **Rukh affinity**: the Rukh "loves him more than anyone". As a child he could read the flow of the waves and steer boats out of killer storms, at age 3 guiding his father through one. As a newborn he guided his parents away from a volcanic eruption. At his birth, Yunan, Scheherazade, Mogamett and Arba all felt the Rukh shift, and Yunan called him a "miracle". As an adult he weaponizes his own Rukh (Foraz Zora is empowered by it, and Zepar's control works by pushing his Rukh into other people).
- **"Sinbad's eye / fate" trait**: the wiki never names an "eye" ability. What it describes is that he can foresee the flow of destiny, which feeds his arrogance and his belief that he should rewrite the world's Rukh System. Omniversal-battlefield calls this Fate Manipulation for his god-tier form. After seizing the Sacred Palace he can rewrite the Rukh and change his rank in the hierarchy relative to other gods. Boss idea: a "foresight" dodge, telegraph-reading or counter mechanic.

### The Household
- Seven of the Eight Generals are his Household Members:
  - Baal Household: Ja'far (Bararaq Sei, wires), Masrur (Bararaq Kauza, armor), Drakon (Bararaq Barasikh, earring), Mystras Leoxses (Bararaq Harba, spear, listed on Baal's page).
  - Valefor Household: Hinahoho.
  - Zepar Household: Pisti.
  - Focalor Household: Sharrkan.
- Yamraiha is a General but a magician, so she cannot be a Household member (magicians and djinn interfere with each other). Spartos is a General too, but his vessel is not given here.
- Chant for lending power to the Household (Baal): roughly, he commands the djinn and its Household to feed on his Magoi and empower his will, then calls "Come forth, Baal!" Boss idea: summon General minions that carry Baal, Valefor, Zepar or Focalor elements.

### Notable feats
- At 14 he conquered Baal's dungeon (the 1st), where his father had died. He then took Valefor's, founded the first Sindria, and went on to 7 dungeons.
- Finished off the Banker in Balbadd with a huge lightning attack in Baal equip.
- Ambushed Ithnan in the air with Focalor, absorbed his black Rukh, killed him in a single attack and crushed his egg.
- Beat Kougyoku. He put her to sleep with Zepar's scream and secretly mind-controlled her afterwards to spy on Kou.
- Against the Medium: led the Seven Seas Alliance in full Baal equip, used Bararaq Saiqa to help break its defensive wall, then fired Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa together with every other Dungeon Capturer present. The lightning pierced clean through the Medium.
- Regrew several lost limbs and recovered from crippling damage just by switching Djinn Equips.
- Final Arc: abolished slavery, pushed to ban conscription, unified world currency, then took control of the Sacred Palace and fought Ugo (he lost).
- Battle log on the wiki: won vs Drakon, Serendine, Darius, Mira, the Fog Troupe, Markkio, Ithnan and Kougyoku. Lost to child Masrur in the colosseum, was beaten in a joint fight against Cassim, and lost to Ugo.

### Weaknesses
- Alcohol (he passes out drunk and gets robbed of his vessels, as in Balbadd). If his vessels are taken, he cannot equip, and his Household cannot use their vessels either.
- Overconfidence and arrogance.
- Overusing Magoi drops him back to base form. Magoi negation strains his body.
- Black Rukh use has a heavy price.
- He can only use the magic of the djinn he currently has equipped (switching is fast, though).
- General Djinn Equip weak points: disrupting the "sash" hampers flight, and cutting the eight-pointed star during an equip breaks the vessel.
- A Magi's Borg stops him: Yunan's Borg made all his equipped attacks useless, and Aladdin's Borg saved him from Judar.
- Judar pinned him with ice spears when he was not equipped.

## 2. The Djinn system

- **Djinn**: magic beings made from Rukh that keep the memories and personalities of King Solomon's 72 Household retainers, who were human followers and chieftains of Alma Torran's tribes. They are typically blue-skinned and gigantic, and some can shrink (Valefor has a tiny "Miniform"). Each has a seal emblem and a tribe title (for example Baal's "Wrath & Heroes"), and each has an affinity for one of the eight magic types.
- **Dungeons**: the djinn stayed behind in their tribes' underground refuge cities in dying Alma Torran. Those cities became the Dungeons, which current Magi summon into the present world. A djinn sleeps in the treasure room, controls its dungeon's difficulty, and picks a King Vessel. When the holder dies, it returns to its dungeon with its memories intact.
- **Metal Vessel**: a metal item that the djinn lives in after its dungeon is captured. The first one is usually the conqueror's weapon, and later ones go into ornaments (necklaces, earrings, rings).
- **Weapon (Half) Djinn Equip**: the user wraps Magoi around the vessel and compresses it into the djinn's weapon. It is weaker than the full form. Sinbad teaches that compressing power is the right way to use it, rather than blasting it outward.
- **Full Djinn Equip** (Masō, "magic dress"): the user compresses the djinn's power into their own body and becomes a humanoid version of the djinn's race, without the blue skin. Common traits are a third-eye ornament on the forehead, racial jewelry or tattoos, and the djinn's weapon. It needs training. It grants a huge boost to strength and speed, plus two general abilities: fast flight (which depends on the sash) and some healing (inconsistent from user to user). One full equip is said to be able to level a small country.
  - Chant template: the user calls the spirit of the djinn's tribe title, orders it to clothe and dwell in their body and turn it into a great djinn, then shouts the djinn's name.
- **Extreme Magic**: an incantation-based ultimate move that only masters of Djinn Equip can use.
- **Magoi cost and limits**: the equip is powered by the user's own Magoi, and running out ends it (it "reverts" him, per omniversal-battlefield). Magicians cannot be King Vessels or Household members. When the Sacred Palace is suspended in the Final Arc, Metal Vessels stop working and Djinn Equip ceases to exist.
- **Multiple djinn**: a user may own several, but cannot use one djinn's magic while equipped as another. Switching is quick and can be used tactically, including feeding one djinn's magic into another's. Belial is the one exception. Sinbad holds 7, the series maximum, and the djinn refused to let him take more.
- **Household Vessels** (Kenzoku Ki): metal objects that matter to a King Vessel's followers. Those followers become Household Members. The djinn's Familiar appears to grant the power, and each member uses only one djinn. They need the master's Metal Vessel to be present.
  - Two stages: normal, and **Assimilation**. Assimilation fuses the member with the djinn's household spirit (the oath is "I consecrate my body to you"). It gives more power, and the ability seen so far is changing size. It costs humanity each time, risks the member's heart vanishing, and forcing it can be lethal. Most members stop at half assimilation.
- **Borg**: not covered on these pages beyond the fact that a Magi's Borg blocks a djinn user's attacks (Yunan's and Aladdin's Borgs).
- **Dark Djinn**: Al-Thamen's imitation. The user stabs themselves with a Dark Metal Vessel, which costs their life. It heals endlessly on black Rukh, looks more monstrous, and is weaker than a real djinn.

## 3. Sinbad's seven djinn

### Baal (1st Dungeon): Djinn of Wrath and Heroes, Lightning
- Vessel: Sinbad's sword. Baal's sword form is called Bararaq Saiqa.
- Djinn look: dragon-like, with bat wings, scales, horns, shaggy dark hair, a third eye and necklaces. Grim-faced but kind-hearted and strict.
- Djinn Equip: dragon motif. Two horns, a forehead jewel, an elaborate necklace, blue scale armor over parts of the body, and a long tail. The sword's guard looks like Baal's claw with blue scales. He flies.
- Spells:
  - **Bararaq** (Lightning): calls lightning, from the sky or from his sword, onto the enemy. It works in full or weapon equip and can wreck a whole mountain range in one strike. It is his basic attack.
  - **Bararaq Saiqa** (Lightning Sword): pulls lightning from the sky (or a summoned thundercloud) into the sword, stores it, then releases a huge blast that lights up the sky. Strong enough to hurt the Medium.
  - **Extreme Magic: Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa** (Lightning Sword of Extinction): a gigantic blade of concentrated lightning shaped like his sword drops from above and impales everything below. It pierced straight through the Medium.
- Household: Ja'far, Masrur, Drakon (and Mystras). Omniversal-battlefield calls Baal his strongest and most-used vessel.

### Valefor (6th Dungeon): Djinn of Falsehood and Confidence, Water/Ice
- Alternate translations: "falsehood and prestige", and in the official English, "falsehood and faith".
- Vessel: gold necklace with a red jewel.
- Djinn look: a towering beast form plus a tiny wolf "Miniform" (he insists he is a wolf, not a cat). Lazy, flashy and honest. He dislikes Baal.
- Djinn Equip: nine-tailed fox. White fur, long white hair, fox ears, and eyes on his palms that emit waves to use Valefor's true power.
- Abilities and spells:
  - **Stagnation**: halts molecular motion. On air moisture this makes ice. On a person it cuts their senses off from their surroundings and slows their body and even their thoughts, so they may not notice it is happening.
  - Mass freeze: freezes everything around him, people included, within seconds, and only hits those he considers enemies. Omniversal-battlefield cites an entire desert frozen instantly.
  - **Garufor Zairu** (Ice Beast Howl; the image caption spells it "Garefor Zairu"): fires many ice spikes formed from water. Stronger than it looks.
  - **Garufor Kirestal** (Ice Beast Crystal Formation): seals the target inside an ice stalactite or crystal, knocking them out.
  - No Extreme Magic named.
- Household: Hinahoho.

### Zepar (16th Dungeon): Djinn of Spirit and Puppetry, Sound
- Vessel: ring on his right middle finger. Zepar's first master was Serendine (in a sword hilt). Sinbad got Zepar by merging his Rukh with hers, and she died in the process.
- Djinn look: potbellied imp with horns, bat wings and a mischievous grin. He thinks kings end up lonely and dislikes rule by a single king.
- Djinn Equip: small imp with dragon wings, hooves, two pairs of horns, a furry tail, a big belly and a third eye. His voice turns childlike. Sinbad switched into it straight from Focalor.
- Abilities:
  - **Command scream**: a huge, loud shriek that "commands" minds. He used it to order Kougyoku's mind to sleep. He calls it tasteless because it affects everyone nearby to some degree.
  - **Mind control**: he pushes his own Rukh into a target with a special sound wave, then controls them long-term, even without equipping Zepar. Range appears unlimited. He did it to Kougyoku and two unnamed others, and used it to spy on the world.
  - **Hadeir** (Sound Magic): the name is listed with no description.
  - Not built for combat. No Extreme Magic named.
- Household: Pisti.
- Final Arc: Zepar's trial is the "Trial of Souls and Puppetry". Sinbad materializes a Serendine figure, and the two of them do a Djinn Equip together.

### Focalor (41st Dungeon): Djinn of Control and Obedience, Wind
- The Sinbad page translates it as "Rule and Submission".
- Vessel: silver bracelet, right arm.
- Djinn look: a handsome womanizer covered in feathers, with long hair, lots of jewelry and feather marks under his eyes. Designed after the wind god Fūjin.
- Djinn Equip: bird form. Dark feathers on parts of his body, long hair with feathered ends, a golden collar and a third eye. He spins tornadoes from his palms.
- Spells:
  - **Foraz Zora** (Cleaving Wind; omniversal spells it "Forzar Zoura"): a tornado around each hand, merged overhead into one enormous tornado thrown down hard enough to destroy a mountain. It can also be fired as two separate tornadoes. A smaller version, empowered by black Rukh, repelled Kougyoku's Extreme Magic.
  - No Extreme Magic named.
- Household: Sharrkan. A "cursed body" image shows him in Focalor equip while under Ithnan's curse.

### Furfur (34th Dungeon): Djinn of Ecstasy and Rending Darkness, Light (Darkness)
- Vessel: silver necklace.
- Djinn look: vertical antler-like horns on both sides of his head, dark skin, long messy black hair, jewelry.
- Djinn Equip: an image exists (shown briefly in the Final Arc). No description and no spells. **THIN.**

### Vepar (42nd Dungeon): Lightning (Magnetism)
- The only female among his djinn. Her tribe title is not given.
- Vessel: ring on his index finger.
- Djinn look: insect antennae, moth or butterfly wings, earrings, a necklace, dark skin, bare chest and midriff.
- Spell: **Vepar Istella** (Thousand Sword Shower): conjures hundreds of very sharp Vepar-style swords and rains them on targets. Equip appearance is not described. **THIN.**

### Crocell (49th Dungeon): Light
- Tribe title not given.
- Vessel: gold bracelet, left arm.
- Djinn look: a crown with a jutting horn, a forward jaw guard, two stripes on each cheek, furry ears.
- Djinn Equip: uses a saber as his main weapon. The general Djinn Equip page notes the form strays far from human, so tattoos and ornaments stand in for his race. No spells named. **VERY THIN.**

## 4. omniversal-battlefield "Sinbad (Magi)" (tiers skipped)
- Forms: Base, Full Djinn Equip Focalor, Full Djinn Equip Baal, Full Djinn Equip Zepar, and Guardian of the Sacred (Holy) Palace.
- Human form abilities: superhuman physical stats, flight, molecular immobilization (Valefor), immunity to curses, magic, lightning, wind and ice manipulation, mind control, Magoi manipulation and negation, Metal Vessel use.
- Guardian form abilities: soul, reality, life, energy, space, time, plot, void and fate manipulation; high regeneration; higher-dimensional and non-corporeal existence. Likely near-omniscient because the Golden Palace lets him watch all worlds at once.
- Concrete feats: froze an entire desert instantly (Valefor). Harmed Ugo and took hits from him.
- Stats: genius intellect, superhuman stamina.
- Weaknesses: a bit overconfident; overusing Magoi reverts him to base.
- Equipment: the Baal, Focalor, Zepar and Valefor vessels, 3 more vessels, and a normal sword.
- Techniques:
  - Black Rukh Magoi boost (used to counter Kougyoku's Extreme Magic).
  - Baal: B Bararaq, Bararaq Saiqa (summoned thundercloud absorbed into the sword, then released), Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa (a giant lightning sword impaling the foe).
  - Valefor: Stagnation, Garufor Zairu (spears of frozen air moisture), Garufor Kirestal (ice crystal prison).
  - Focalor: Forzar Zoura (winds on both hands).
  - Zepar: Commands (a wide-radius screech that forces obedience), Sleep, and Mind Control (persists without Zepar active, no known range limit).

## 5. Multi-djinn equips and combination attacks
- **No simultaneous multi-equip.** He cannot use one djinn's magic while equipped with another (only Belial breaks this rule).
- **Rapid switching** is his trademark. He switches for tactics, can feed one djinn's magic into another's attack, and heals lost limbs by switching equips. Example: Focalor straight into Zepar.
- **Combination attack**: at the Medium, every assembled Dungeon Capturer fired Extreme Magic together alongside his Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa.
- **Final Arc**: he briefly shows the last three equips (Furfur, Vepar, Crocell), and in Zepar's trial he does a joint Djinn Equip with a materialized Serendine.
- Boss design implication: a phase per djinn (switching forms, each with its own model), healing on each switch, and a finale of Baal's Extreme Magic, possibly with Household or "allied capturer" minions joining in.

## Pasted summary vs wiki
- **Baal**: lightning, held in the sword, and grants flight: confirmed. **Wrong**: "Valalark Saika" is not his Extreme Magic. The wiki spells the move **Bararaq Saiqa** (Lightning Sword), and that is the thundercloud-absorbed-into-the-sword move. "Balalark Saika" appears only as an image filename. The Extreme Magic is **Bararaq Inqerad-Saiqa** (a giant falling lightning sword).
- **Focalor**: wind, silver bracelet (right arm), flight and tornadoes: confirmed. **Wrong**: "Forzar Zoura" is not an Extreme Magic. That is omniversal-battlefield's spelling of **Foraz Zora** (Cleaving Wind), a normal spell with twin tornadoes merged into one. No Focalor Extreme Magic is named.
- **Zepar**: sound, mind control, middle-finger ring, makes targets obey or sleep: confirmed. "Zepar's Command" is not a wiki name. Omniversal-battlefield lists "Commands" and "Sleep"; the Fandom page names only **Hadeir**.
- **Valefor**: ice and a necklace (gold, with a red jewel) confirmed. Nine-tailed fox Djinn Equip confirmed ("Masō" means Djinn Equip).
- **Furfur**: "dark energy" is roughly supported (Light magic of the Darkness subtype, Djinn of Ecstasy and Rending Darkness). "Manga only" is unsupported as stated. The wiki only notes he is not seen in the anime's djinn silhouettes and that his equip appears in the Final Arc.
- **Vepar**: "Vepar Istella" and the index-finger ring confirmed. "Rain of Swords" is a paraphrase; the wiki's translation is "Thousand Sword Shower". The summary leaves out her magic type, which is Lightning (Magnetism).
- **Crocell**: "unknown or minor" is broadly fair. The wiki gives Light magic, a gold left-arm bracelet and a saber weapon, and no spells.
- **Magoi manipulation**: cancelling enemy magic is confirmed, with the limit that it strains his body. "Transforms it" is unsupported. The closest thing is absorbing black Rukh from Ithnan's magic through his fallen half.
- **Rukh manipulation**: "remove or control the Rukh within him" is unsupported as phrased. The wiki says the Rukh guides him, that he weaponizes his own Rukh and pushes it into others to control them, and that he can absorb black Rukh. Once he holds the Sacred Palace, he can rewrite the Rukh.
- **Destruction**: "destroyed mountain ranges" is partly supported. Bararaq can destroy a mountain chain in one strike and Foraz Zora can destroy a mountain, but no specific youth feat is recorded. "City-level" is unsupported. The wiki says a single Djinn Equip user can destroy a small country; omniversal-battlefield's own figure is a desert frozen instantly.

# Black Clover scene prompt (from the art pack)

Built from `Black Clover Art.zip`: the grimoire tables (Clover / Spade / Heart kingdom books), the cover designs (blue three-leaf with gold filigree, green four-leaf, royal-blue five-leaf, black spear-spade, arcane-wheel spade, purple cloud swirls, sea-serpent teal, crystal diamond, black-and-blood, gold sunburst), the Grimoire Tower (exterior, the old tower keeper with the glowing green stone, the manga ceremony page), the anime ceremony frame of books flying down from the shelves, the squad cloaks and the Minecraft character skins.

## Stylistic choices

- **Ceremony in the tower:** the art pack centres on the Grimoire Tower (exterior shot, the keeper, the manga ceremony page, the anime frame of books flying down), so the scene is the moment a grimoire chooses its mage. That is the signature Black Clover image.
- **Low angle, strong diagonal:** looking up the spiral shelves makes the tower feel endless and the hero heroic. The diagonal from the raised sword to the floating grimoire gives the frame its shonen energy.
- **Anti-magic against colourful magic:** the black and crimson aura that tears particles apart contrasts with the coloured glows of the other grimoires. This is the series' core visual conflict.
- **Grimoires from the cover sheets:**
  - The double gold frames, filigree corners and clover medallions come straight from the reference covers.
  - The other knights' books come from the reference covers: the green four-leaf, the royal-blue book and the spear-crowned spade. Each glows in its magic colour, as in the wiki tables.
  - The hero's open V pose with the spine toward the viewer matches how the books float in the anime.
- **Manga finish:** heavy ink outlines, speed lines and screentone keep it reading as manga, not a painting. Rim lighting from the magic auras supplies the drama without flattening the line work.
- **Attire:** a short squad mantle with a clasp, a dark tunic and bandaged arms match the cloak sheet and the character skins in the pack, so the result also works as a reference for the mod's knight robes.

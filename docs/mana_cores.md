# Rank Mana Cores (3.51.0)

Inspired by *The Beginning After the End*: mana cores and beast cores ranked **E, D, C, B, A, AA, S, SS**. Each rank has three variants (24 items): **standard** (a human-style core), **beast** (taken from a mana beast) and **ancient** (crossover relic). Each core has one passive and two actives. **Right-click** is the first active, **sneak + right-click** the second. The passive works while the core is held in either hand or worn in a Curios slot (one core at a time: main hand, off hand, then Curios).

Status: written, unit tested and typechecked. **Nothing has been seen in a game.** Sources and caveats are at the end. The sector-key `nusmp:mana_core` (Labyrinth Guardians) is a different item and is untouched; the new items are `nusmp:core_<rank>_<variant>` (`core_e_standard` ... `core_ss_ancient`).

## Beast Will hookup (A, AA, S, SS beast cores)

The spec's Acquire / Integrate phases already exist as the Beast Will system (3.45.0, `docs/beastwill.md`), so nothing was duplicated:

- Shadow Panther Core -> `shadow_panther_will`, Elderwood Guardian Core -> `elderwood_guardian_will`, Nihil Dragon Core -> `dragon_will` (all three existed).
- **New:** `thunder_fang_chimera_will` (data file `data/nusmp/beast_will/thunder_fang_chimera_will.json`, rank A, 30,000 EP, built on the lion will's two abilities) for the AA Chimera core.
- **Sneak + right-click** on such a core: with no will of that kind, it opens the normal Beast Will preview (nothing is taken or given until the player says yes); with the will active, it starts or ends the Integrate form through `BeastWillManager` (assimilation, mastery, strain and the form's energy are the Beast Will system's). If another will is active the player is told to swap first.
- The **Acquire phase** is the Beast Will system's own (its key); the core's passive only adds a small carried bonus (night vision and speed for the panther, regeneration for the guardian, lightning resistance and thorns for the chimera, a quarter-health heal per kill for the dragon).
- The core is not consumed. The ledger of the Beast Will system still decides that one will binds once per player.

## Where they come from

- **Standard / beast:** a wild (not tamed, not owned), at least one minute old creature killed by a player. Its rank comes from its Existence Points (E 100, D 300, C 1,000, B 5,000, A 20,000, AA 50,000, S 100,000, SS 500,000; a creature Tensura does not know counts 100 EP per health point). Chance per kill by rank: E 3%, D 2%, C 1.5%, B 1%, A 0.6%, AA 0.4%, S 0.2%, SS 0.1% (times `dropChanceScale`); a non-humanoid creature gives a beast core half of the time but at half the chance. One drop per player per `dropGapMinutes` (20), one core per kill, no despawn timer.
- **Ancient:** only from a boss kill (double chance) or a **cleared pocket rift** (3% per player, rank C or better the player's Existence Points allow), and from `/manacore give`.

## Changes against the owner's text (and why)

| Spec text | What it does | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Overclock Pulse drains 10 HP per second | costs 2 hearts once, refused at or under 4 health | 10 HP/s kills in two seconds |
| Mana Leak loses 2% of stored MP | leaks 0.03% of max MP per second | 2% in total over a minute, net of regeneration |
| Fragile Resonance lowers maximum MP | not done (only the +5% attack speed) | Tensura's maximum MP has no safe hook |
| Stable Channeling lowers all spell costs; Mana Sovereign lowers all cooldowns | applies to this core's own actives only | other mods' spells are not ours to change |
| Mana Core Condensation triples max MP | fast MP regeneration instead | same |
| Spell Matrix (3 stored charges) | every third active of this core is free and nearly instant | simplest correct form |
| Infinite Horizon: limitless MP in combat | +3% of max MP per second while hurt in the last 10 s | bounded |
| Radiant Aura: light level 10 | night vision | no cheap moving light source |
| Cosmic Infusion (ignores resistance and defence) | not done | no safe hook, and it would break the damage balance |
| World-Eater's Maw: full heal on any kill | 25% of max health per kill | bounded |
| Venom Spit leaves a lingering pool | poisons the foes it hits | no spawned entity |
| Micro-Singularity pulls for 3 s then collapses | one strong pull and damage | no spawned entity |
| Chrono-Lock / Crystalline Prison: invulnerable | a real time stop on the target, broken by 4 hearts of damage | the mod's time stop law |
| World of Still Seconds freezes players for 10 s | mobs, projectiles and falling blocks 10 s; a **player 3 s, a boss 1 s** (the shared hard-control caps) | PvP and boss balance |
| Last Second of Creation | 30 s countdown: everything in a 24-block zone floats and glows, the user is quickened, then one damage burst, no block damage | server safety |

## Bounds and worst-case cost

- Server work per tick: nothing for a player without a core (one map read). With a core: once a second (staggered) a few attribute writes, at most one 12-block entity query with 6 hits (Void Step), at most 16 item pulls (Gravity Well). A busy player (runes, a delayed burst or the countdown) adds a tick-level check; the countdown does one query of at most 24 creatures every half second for 30 s.
- One press: one entity query (radius at most 30), at most 24 targets (the world freeze 32), no entity spawned, no block touched, particles at most 24 per call through `Fx`'s own budgets, one chat line.
- State: one small object per player with a core, dropped on logout, death, respawn, dimension change, server stop and when the core leaves the hands; runes at most 3, delayed bursts at most 2, one countdown.
- Network: no new packet. Hard control obeys the 12 s shared gate and the 3 s / 5 s / 1 s caps (players / mobs / bosses), except the SS world freeze for mobs, projectiles and blocks (see above).
- Server safe: no client class in the package (`ManaCoreRulesTest` scans it); every press is validated on the server (rank gate, cooldown, cost, protection, teams, PvP setting).

## Verified and not verified

Verified: the pure table (bounds, uniqueness, the owner's numbers), every lang key, the package scan, typecheck of the package, `manacore.table` and `manacore.player` (written for the CI smoke test). **Not verified in a game:** how any active looks or feels, balance of the numbers, the Curios worn path, and the Beast Will confirm screen opened from a core.

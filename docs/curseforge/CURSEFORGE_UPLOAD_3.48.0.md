## 5. Check these BEFORE you publish (real risks, not boilerplate)
1. **Voice lines (ElevenLabs).** About 290 spoken lines (system, bosses, NPCs, Djinn, Shishou, spirits) were generated with ElevenLabs library voices. Our own notes say commercial use and library-voice terms were **never cleared** (`docs/audio/spoken_voices.md`). CurseForge pays creators, which counts as commercial, and library voices carry their own conditions. Check your ElevenLabs plan and each voice's licence (David, Justin, Final Boss, Obsidian, Jay and the others in `tools/audio/voice_lines.json`) first. If unsure, ship without them (set the voice option off by default, or remove the `sounds/voice` and `sounds/gnome` ogg files) rather than guess.
2. **AI content disclosure.** Voices (and parts of the art) are AI-generated. CurseForge's rules change; read their current "AI generated content" rule and tick any disclosure box. The description already says voices are AI-generated.
3. **Fan IP.** The mod uses names and ideas from many series (Black Clover, DanMachi, Jujutsu Kaisen, JoJo, Hunter x Hunter, Fate, Dragon Ball ...). CurseForge allows fan content but can remove a project after a rights-holder complaint. The description has a fan-made notice; keep it. Avoid anime screenshots or official art on the page.
4. **Third-party assets.** I cannot prove every texture and sound in the jar is yours or licensed; the models and icons are mostly generated by the project's own scripts. If any came from outside sources, credit them in the description.
5. **Not seen in a game.** 3.48.0 compiles, passes ~10,970 tests and boots on a dedicated server in CI, but wings, horns, markings and the balance of numbers were never seen in a running game. That is why the type is Beta.
6. **File size.** The jar is large (hundreds of models packed). CurseForge accepts files well above the typical size, but if the upload is rejected for size, tell me and I will trim it.
7. **Review time.** New projects and first files go through CurseForge approval (often a day or more). The file is not public until approved.

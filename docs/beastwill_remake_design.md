**Canon basis:** the TBATE wiki *Beast Will* page and the two webcomic panels you sent, plus your lore pastes. A canon label means a fact is on those pages; everything else is our own design built on the canon rules.

**Rules every will follows:** A-class beast or higher; Acquire = human body + motion streaks + ONE equipped piece (borrowed strength); Integrate = whole-body beast-humanoid in a full armoured form with a signature weapon (its price is mana drain and strain); an untrained holder is hurt, and a badly controlled will can kill; no summoned hordes; one will per beast; each will its own colour.

**Style idea used for the transformation:** partial piece in Acquire, full armoured form in Integrate (no copied names from any other series).

## Dragon Will: Mana Dragon (Sylvia, who gave her will to Arthur)  [SS / legendary, CANON-BASE]
*Sylvia's Dragon Will is the will of an Asuran dragon of the Indrath line, handed to Arthur. Acquire keeps you human with lavender afterimages and one equipped piece, a single aether-rune gauntlet, so you can step through aether and briefly freeze time (Static Void). Integrate (Realmheart) makes you a white-maned dragon-humanoid in gold-runed aether plate that sees mana and bends space; it burns the highest drain in the roster and is painful until mastered.*

- **Lore:** The Dragon's Will, an Asuran dragon's will of the Indrath line (Aether) that Sylvia gave to Arthur. A holder first borrows its grip on aether (Static Void); only a trained one becomes the Realmheart dragon-humanoid.
- **Source:** CANON (wiki page): a mana beast of A Class or higher can pass on its will; Sylvia gave hers to Arthur; Arthur as a child felt pain from it so it must be trained; Acquire borrows strengths (+ common markings), Integrate truly uses the abilities with a real body transformation. CANON ADDENDUM 2 (owner): it is the Dragon's Will, the will of an Asuran entity, given by Sylvia to Arthur; Arthur also has an Equals-Contract bond with her daughter Sylvie (a separate mechanic, not modelled here). SNIPPET (search digests, medium confidence): Acquire = Static Void (aether freezes time, short and costly), Integrate = Realmheart (white hair, lavender eyes, gold runes on arms and under the eyes, sees mana and aether, gravity/space). DESIGN: the lavender-aether palette, the dragon-humanoid body, every number, the breath pulse and the skill moves. Style idea: partial piece in Acquire -> full armoured form in Integrate (design only, not TBATE canon; owner request).
- **Untrained holder:** Canon: Arthur felt pain from Sylvia's will. DESIGN: the highest strain of any will (acquire 0.28/s, integrate 1.4/s); an untrained holder's screen shudders, aether backlash burns mana and hearts whenever Static Void or the breath is cast past the trained level. CANON ADDENDUM 2: the will lies dormant in the holder's mana core until it is forcefully awakened (a flame sparked in the core); if the holder cannot suppress and balance it, the clashing energies wreck body and core and can kill (design: strain climbs fast, backlash damage, forced cancel at the cap); mastery means training both the holder's own core and the beast core, best taught by the beast itself. This will summons no beasts or hordes.
- **Core / spirit colours:** core #B9A4FF (accent #E3B341); spirit #B9A4FF, #F2F0FF, #E3B341
- **Core look:** A faceted lavender-aether orb with a slit gold pupil, two slow-turning gold rune rings and a faint folded-wing glimmer inside.
- **ACQUIRE** (drain 0.8%, strain 0.24/s) , one equipped piece: One lavender aether-rune gauntlet on the casting forearm (gold runes glow when Static Void fires); body stays human.
  - Look: The human player, body unchanged, blurring in long pale-lavender afterimages with a faint wing-shaped streak; ghost time-stutter frames during Static Void. ONE equipped piece: a lavender aether-rune gauntlet on one forearm (slit lavender-gold pupils are not added).
  - **Static Void** (time_exclusion, cost 12%, cd 60s, strain 14, power 1, range 0, radius 6, targets 3)
  - **Aether Step** (shadow_dash, cost 5%, cd 8s, strain 6, power 1, range 10, radius 0, targets 1)
- **INTEGRATE** (drain 2.0%, strain 1.35/s)
  - Body: Full armoured form Realmheart dragon-humanoid: white-orange-tipped mane over swept horns, glowing lavender eyes with gold under-eye runes, gold runes down both arms, ornate white-lavender dragon plate on shoulders and spine, digitigrade clawed greaved legs, a long spined tail, two membrane wings and an aether-fang blade grown from the forearm.
  - Weapon: A long aether-glass dragon-fang blade grown from the right forearm, gold runes down the flat. | Armour: Ornate white-and-lavender dragon plate: scaled pauldrons, spine ridge plates, runed bracers and greaves, a folded two-wing membrane cape. | Hair/eyes: Hair turns white with orange tips and sweeps back over two horns; eyes glow lavender, gold runes under the eyes. | Aura: Lavender-gold aether aura with drifting rune motes and a faint space-warp shimmer.
  - Silhouette echo: A tall dragon-humanoid in white and lavender aether plate: swept-back horned head, white mane, slit lavender-gold eyes, scaled digitigrade legs, thick spined tail, two membrane wings, clawed hands, aether-fang blade.
  - **Realmheart Sight** (dragon_sight, cost 6%, cd 20s, strain 10, power 2, range 24, radius 24, targets 8)
  - **Aether Breath Pulse** (draconic_pulse, cost 14%, cd 10s, strain 16, power 5, range 8, radius 8, targets 6)
- **Unique -> Ultimate skill:** Wyrmheart -> Dragon Sovereign (Wrath of the Mana Dragon), scale 1.0, finisher fx G:SPACE_TEAR
  - Passives: Draconic Hide (ARMOR); Dragon Sight (SENSE); Draconic Vigor (REGEN)
  - Actives: Dragon Breath (BEAM): A long beam of aether-fire from the horned jaws; armour-breaking, reaches far.; Aether Stasis (Static Void) (STOP): The holder freezes the aether around a circle of enemies for a heartbeat: they stand still while the holder moves; costly and short.
  - Mastery finisher: **Dragon King's Annihilation** (BEAM): The dragon rears and breathes a screen-wide aether beam that ignores defences and stuns what survives; gravity presses the target flat.  Incantation: "Realmheart, my aether, answer: break the realm in two."

## Elderwood Guardian Will: Elderwood Guardian (corrupted S-class plant beast, centaur-like body of entangled vines)  [S, CANON-BASE]
*The Elderwood Guardian was a Vritra-corrupted S-class plant beast of entangled vines (a Hamadryad-like grove), and Tessia holds its will. Acquire keeps you human in a thin emerald second-skin glow with one equipped piece, a single bark-vine bracer, to lash and snare with vines. Integrate (Full armoured form) makes you a green-haired, turquoise-eyed treant-beastman in thorned bark armour with twin aura blades; it has the hardest control in the roster, no healing, and will overwhelm an untrained holder.*

- **Lore:** The will of a Vritra-corrupted, Hamadryad-like grove guardian. It lends vines and emerald aura, but it pushes back: a holder who is not trained enough is overwhelmed.
- **Source:** CANON (wiki page): Tessia Eralith holds the corrupted Elderwood Guardian's will; even trained she needed more training to master it or it would overwhelm her; Acquire borrows strengths, Integrate is a true transformation. SNIPPET (search digests, medium confidence): the Guardian is S-class (so minRank S); forged tamer (core absorbed); Acquire = thin emerald aura like a second skin, bursting out under the feet through the grass, summoning and controlling many vines; Integrate = thick coiling emerald aura, pale skin, forest-green hair, glowing turquoise eyes with markings, aura shaped into dual blades; Vritra-corrupted, half power at first; no heal or ward is attested. DESIGN (ours): the treant-beastman body, every number and move name. CANON ADDENDUM 2 applied: only an A-class or higher beast leaves an intact will (rare); the will lies dormant in the holder's core and must be forcefully awakened (a flame sparked in the host's core); mastery = train the holder's own core AND the inherited beast core, best teacher is the beast itself; Beast Tamers command no armies (no summoning of several beasts). Style idea: partial piece in Acquire -> full armoured form in Integrate (design only, not TBATE canon; owner request).
- **Untrained holder:** Hardest will to control in the roster. Canon: even a trained holder must train more or the corrupted will overwhelms them. DESIGN: highest strain of every will (Acquire 0.28, Integrate 1.4); past a strain threshold black blight veins climb the body, abilities cost extra strain and the vines turn on the holder (a short self-root); the awakening needs an outside flame in the core and a forced or neglected one can kill the host.
- **Core / spirit colours:** core #1FBF5A (accent #2FD8C8); spirit #1FBF5A, #0F2A1A, #35E0D0
- **Core look:** A knotted heartwood orb with a cracked bark husk showing emerald glow, black blight veins crawling over it and a turquoise spark in the crack.
- **ACQUIRE** (drain 0.7%, strain 0.3/s) , one equipped piece: One bark-and-vine bracer on a forearm with a single black blight vein under it; thin emerald second-skin glow, grass bursts under the feet.
  - Look: The human player, body unchanged, in a thin emerald sheen like a second skin; a ring of grass and green motes bursts out from under the feet, soft green afterimages on each dash. ONE equipped piece: a bark-and-vine bracer on one forearm.
  - **Emerald Vine Lash** (vine_lash, cost 3%, cd 5s, strain 5, power 2.5, range 8, radius 6, targets 2)
  - **Grass Burst Snare** (restraining_vines, cost 6%, cd 30s, strain 8, power 2, range 10, radius 5, targets 3)
- **INTEGRATE** (drain 1.9%, strain 1.4/s)
  - Body: Full armoured form treant-beastman: antler-branch crown, forest-green hair, hollow turquoise eyes, thorned bark armour on shoulders and back, root-toed digitigrade clawed greaves, vine-wrapped arms ending in two aura blades, a trailing vine tail and black blight veins over the chest.
  - Weapon: Twin emerald aura blades coiled from the vine-wrapped forearms (the Guardian's vine lance is the Mastery move). | Armour: Thorned bark armour: antler-branch crown, bark plates on shoulders and back, vine-bound bracers and root-toed greaves, black blight veins over the chest. | Hair/eyes: Hair turns forest-green, pale skin, hollow glowing turquoise eyes with markings. | Aura: Thick coiling emerald aura with black blight motes and falling leaves.
  - Silhouette echo: A tall treant-beastman in thorned bark armour: antler-branch crowned head with forest-green hair and hollow turquoise eyes, bark-plated shoulders, vine-bound arms ending in twin aura blades, root-toed digitigrade legs, a trailing vine tail, black blight veins, a thick emerald aura.
  - **Aura Twin Blades** (ambush, cost 10%, cd 12s, strain 18, power 5, range 10, radius 0, targets 1)
  - **Corrupted Coil Burst** (draconic_pulse, cost 14%, cd 10s, strain 28, power 5, range 8, radius 8, targets 6)
- **Unique -> Ultimate skill:** Corrupted Grove -> Elder Grove Sovereign (Heart of the Corrupted Grove), scale 0.7, finisher fx G:TREE_ROOTS
  - Passives: Bark Hide (ARMOR); Creeping Blight (BLIGHT); Grove Sense (SENSE)
  - Actives: Vine Lance (BEAM): Vines twist into a giant lance, the Guardian's own weapon, and pierce a line of enemies.; Restraining Roots (SEAL): Roots burst from the ground and bind one target; heavy bosses resist.
  - Mastery finisher: **Heart of the Elder Grove** (BLAST): The corrupted heartwood opens: a wave of roots, blades and blight erupts around the holder, pinning and hurling enemies away, then the holder is left strained.  Incantation: "Roots of the elder grove, rise through the rot: Heartwood Lance!"

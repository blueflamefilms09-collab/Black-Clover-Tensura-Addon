# Spike Magic rebuild (1.93.0): armour, constructs, effects and spells

The `Spike` wiki attribute used to be one of the 30 generic wiki magics: a bolt, a line, a field, a domain and a guard drawn by the shared wiki layer
(`WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`), with no constructs. It is now rebuilt in the five parts of the rebuild brief (`docs/rebuild/magic_rebuild_brief.md`).

## 1. NEW / REPLACED / KEPT

| part | status | what |
|---|---|---|

| the temporary armour of the Guard page (5 s, `Aura.WIKI` style 22, a shell and spikes) | REPLACED | by `Aura.SPIKE`: a real set of pieces (section 3), forged in and shattered out by `EquipAnimation` like the Djinn full equip. `WikiAura` is untouched and still serves the other 25 wiki magics |

## 2. Canon

No wiki text for Spike Magic exists in the repo and the Black Clover wiki could **not** be reached from the build session (no network). The magic is built from what it is called: spikes thrust out of the ground and thrown from the body, in the earth family of the book (its `MagicType` is an EARTH magic with the PIERCE hit). No named user is claimed; every spell is the nearest honest version, not a 1:1 reproduction of a canon spell.

## 3. The temporary armour (`client/aura/SpikeAura.java`)

Brushed gun-metal plate bristling with spikes: a helm with a crown of five long spikes, a brow spike and cheek thorns; a ridged cuirass with four forward spikes; a belt with hip spikes; on each shoulder a plate and three long swept spikes (the silhouette of the set); bracers with a spike blade; gauntlets with four knuckle spikes; greaves with a knee spike and a heel spike; on the back a fan of seven long spines. Every tip glows ice white (the glow map). Accents: a seal under the feet, nine spikes thrusting out of the ground round the feet in turn and glinting shards.

Every piece is a cube set of `geo/entity/spike_armor.geo.json` (box UV, pivots, inflate of 0.45 px or more over the skin, a `spike_armor_glow.png` emissive map)
drawn by `ShakudoPieceKit`: anchored on the model part it was built for, so it follows crouching, swimming, swinging and the head; arms are scaled to 80 percent
across for a slim skin. Stagger and fly-in are the Djinn's numbers through `EquipAnimation`: each piece forges in over 8 ticks (fade up, 85 to 100 percent size,
flying in from its side) and shatters out over the last 18 ticks; the spell keeps the aura at least 40 ticks.

| piece (root bone) | anchored on | stagger (ticks) | flies in from (model px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| helm | head | 0 | above and in front (0, -14, -4) |
| chest | body | 2 | in front (0, 0, -12) |
| waist | body | 4 | below and in front (0, 8, -6) |
| pauldron_r / pauldron_l | right / left arm | 3 | from its own side (-14, -4, 0) / (14, -4, 0) |
| vambrace_r / vambrace_l | right / left arm | 5 | its side and forward (-12, 2, -6) / (12, 2, -6) |
| gauntlet_r / gauntlet_l | right / left arm | 6 | its side, low (-10, 8, -6) / (10, 8, -6) |
| greave_r / greave_l | right / left leg | 7 | from below and in front (-3, 14, -6) / (3, 14, -6) |
| back | body | 10 | from behind (0, -2, 12) |


Clips (`spike_armor.animation.json`): `idle` and `move` (played while the player walks or runs). The seven spines of the back flex in a wave (slow in idle, fast while moving).

## 4. Constructs

| model (prop kind) | bones | clips | what it is | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `spike_pillar` (`SPIKE_1`) | root, base, core, main, ring0 to ring5 (10 bones, 24 cubes) | idle, rise, retract | a cluster of one long spike ringed by six on a rocky base | Strike: a cracked warning seal for 8 ticks, then the spikes thrust up under whoever stands there (1.1 block ring, damage 8 x scale, Slowness) and keep pricking every 10 ticks for 3 x scale; sinks in its last 10 ticks |
| `spike_urchin` (`SPIKE_2`) | root, ball, u0 to u17 (20 bones, 39 cubes) | idle (slow turn), roll, burst | a dark ball with 18 spikes pointing every way and a glowing seam | Missile: homes in (12 percent a tick), bursts on the first contact in a 3 block ring (damage 6 x scale, Slowness) |

All behaviours come from `prop/ShakudoPropKit` (Companion / Guard / Strike / Missile) with the numbers in `prop/SpikeProps`; ticks run inside `SafeCast.tick`, damage goes
through `BalanceLaw.damage` (bosses are capped, control is shortened by `BalanceLaw.controlTicks`). The synced word `param` is laid out by `ShakudoPropState`
(state, the tick the state began, a variant), so the painters play one-shot clips from their first frame.

## 5. VFX (`vfx/client/layer/SpikeLayer.java`)

| shape | meaning of from / to / power | look |
|---|---|---|
| `SPIKE_FX1` | cast | a charge ring, then a big spike rockets to the target on a streak with two ghosts and shed shards, bursting into a star of spikes and a ring |
| `SPIKE_FX2` | zone | a seal and cracks in a rim ring, spikes thrust up at staggered moments all over the field and round the rim, shards float up, a pulse runs out |
| `SPIKE_FX3` | impact | a flash, a star of ten spikes thrown out every way (they hang, then sink), a ring and cracks, shards in arcs |

All three stay under the 400 vertex budget (the preview reports the maximum: cast 76, zone 196, impact 96). Sprites are generated by
`tools/gen_spike_textures.py` from `tools/shakudo_vfx_kit.py` (a sigil of the magic's motif, a ring, a flare, a streak, a glow, a crack, a mote and the specific sprites).
The Time Magic VFX files were not touched. Preview: `python3 -B tools/vfx_preview/preview.py spike_cast spike_field spike_impact`.

## 6. Spells (`book/SpikeSpells.java`, appended after the generic five)

| mode | id | name | tier / cost | what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `spike_eruption` | Spike Eruption | starter | six pillars in a line, 1.8 blocks apart, two ticks apart |
| 6 | `spike_wall` | Spike Wall | mid | seven pillars across the way for 8 s |
| 7 | `spike_urchin` | Spike Urchin | mid | a homing spiked ball (80 ticks) that bursts in a 3 block ring |
| 8 | `spike_armor` | Spike Armor | mid | the armour for 20 s: 20 percent of every blow, attackers cut for half of what they dealt (capped at 14 + thorns), Resistance 0 |
| 9 | `spike_impaling_field` | Impaling Field | signature, 90 s | for 5 s a pillar every half second under each of the three nearest foes within 7 blocks |

Costs are the usual tiers (starter 8 percent / floor 150, mid 12 / 300, zone 20 / 600, signature 35 / 1200 of the magicule pool, scaled by the cover and gear as for every book);
damage is `GrimoireBook.hurt` (the balance law, EP scale through `EnergyBridge.scale`), control sits behind `GrimoireBook.control`, summons and zones run through `SpellRuntime`,
all casts run inside `SafeCast` (the grimoire already wraps them). A summon lasts up to 50 percent longer for a mighty caster. The armour page uses `ShakudoKit.guard` (cut 0.20, thorns 3 + 0.4 x mighty and half of the damage taken (capped), no Resistance).
The family now has 10 pages (limit 24). Appended family pages move the universal pages (Devil Union, Summon Grimoire ...) up by five modes; those are found by id, not by number, and
carry no unlock bit, so no save is affected.

## 7. Render layers (every pass)

| what | render type | order and scale | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each armour piece, fully forged | `AuraRender.cutout` (entityCutoutNoCull) | every piece's cubes inflated 0.45 px or more over the skin, drawn first | solid, never coplanar with the body |
| each armour piece while forging in or shattering out | `AuraRender.translucent` (entityTranslucent) | same, alpha = forge progress | the Djinn's fade up and out |
| each piece's glow map | `AuraRender.additive` (eyes), full bright | after every skin pass, alpha pulses | emissive, never z-fights |
| armour accents (seal, motes, orbiting sprites) | `AuraRender.additive`, full bright | last | light |
| construct model | cutout, glow map additive and full bright (`GeoDraw.paint`) | model first | solid body, glow on top |
| construct shadow | translucent, 2 cm over the ground | before the model | soft contact shadow |
| construct ground glyph | additive, 3 cm over the ground | after the model | seal / warning |
| effect sprites | `VfxBlend.ALPHA` for the dark solid sprites, `ADD` for glows | alpha first, add last (the vertex buffer sorts by blend order) | back to front |

## 9. How to test in game

`/give` yourself the grimoire (`nusmp:book_spike` skill), summon it, open the pages with `/nusmp grimoire unlock` or by mastery. Cast `attribute_guard` for the armour at once; cast page 5 to 9 for the new spells.
The CI server smoke test runs `magic.spike_rebuild` (`/multiverse mega selftest magic.spike_rebuild`).

## 10. NOT verified (honest)

* Nothing was run in the game. The type check (real javac against API stubs) reports no error in the new or edited files except the known stub artefacts (`MobEffectInstance is abstract`, `deserializeNBT`); a deliberate wrong call was caught.
* Every picture comes from the preview tools, which are re-implementations of the render path (no real lighting, no world depth, an approximate entity shader): proportions, colours, animation and the forge / shatter staging were looked at in them, the in-game look was not.
* The armour sits over the skin with 0.45 px or more of inflate; slim arms are handled by an 80 percent horizontal scale of the arm pieces. A modded player model or a very different skin may show gaps.
* The Mojang `PlayerSkin.model()` check for slim arms and `ModelPart.getInitialPose()` are real API (confirmed in the API dump) but are not exercised by the preview stubs of `PlayerSkin`.
* The balance numbers of the spells and constructs (damage, cost tier, durations) were set by reading the other books, not by play testing.
* The construct hit boxes are estimates from the model sizes; the golem and serpent walk with the simple `walk` of the Bronze constructs and can get stuck on odd terrain.
* No canon text for this magic could be fetched (the network is closed to the wiki); see section 2.

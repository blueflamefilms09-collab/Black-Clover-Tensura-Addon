# Scale Magic rebuild (1.93.0): armour, constructs, effects and spells

The `Scale` wiki attribute used to be one of the 30 generic wiki magics: a bolt, a line, a field, a domain and a guard drawn by the shared wiki layer
(`WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`), with no constructs. It is now rebuilt in the five parts of the rebuild brief (`docs/rebuild/magic_rebuild_brief.md`).

## 1. NEW / REPLACED / KEPT

| part | status | what |
|---|---|---|
| the five generic pages `attribute_bolt`, `attribute_cut`, `attribute_field`, `attribute_domain`, `attribute_guard` (modes 0 to 4, their mastery bits) | KEPT | same ids, order, costs and numbers; their effects are now this magic's own (see below) |
| the temporary armour of the Guard page (5 s, `Aura.WIKI` style 13, a shell and spikes) | REPLACED | by `Aura.SCALE`: a real set of pieces (section 3), forged in and shattered out by `EquipAnimation` like the Djinn full equip. `WikiAura` is untouched and still serves the other 25 wiki magics |
| the shared wiki effects (`WIKI_MAGIC_CAST / FIELD / BURST`) for this magic | REPLACED | by `SCALE_FX1 / FX2 / FX3` drawn by `ScaleLayer` (section 5). `ElementBook.vfx` maps the three wiki shapes onto them, so the five generic pages need no edit of their own |
| five spells: Scale Volley, Scale Serpent, Scale Shield, Scale Mail, Shedding Tide | NEW | pages appended as modes 5 to 9 through `ExclusiveExt` (section 6) |

## 2. Canon

No wiki text for Scale Magic exists in the repo (`.github/wiki` has no page for it) and the Black Clover wiki could **not** be reached from the build session (no network). The magic is therefore built from what scales are, in the spirit of the other body-and-material magics of the book: overlapping teal fish / dragon scales that turn blows, shed and regrow. No named user is claimed. Every spell is the nearest honest version of "a scale magic", not a 1:1 reproduction of a canon spell.

## 3. The temporary armour (`client/aura/ScaleAura.java`)

A scaled hood with swept ear fins and a crest fin; a scale cuirass of overlapping rows over a pale belly plate with a pearl and a frill collar; scale lames at the waist; three scale cowls and two fins on each shoulder; sleeves with a blade fin; gauntlets with three claws; scaled greaves with calf fins; a dorsal ridge of fin plates on the back and a tail of seven segments. Material: teal scales with a pale shimmer (the glow map). Accents: ripple rings running out from the feet, eight scales orbiting in a spiral and gleams.

Every piece is a cube set of `geo/entity/scale_armor.geo.json` (box UV, pivots, inflate of 0.45 px or more over the skin, a `scale_armor_glow.png` emissive map)
drawn by `ShakudoPieceKit`: anchored on the model part it was built for, so it follows crouching, swimming, swinging and the head; arms are scaled to 80 percent
across for a slim skin. Stagger and fly-in are the Djinn's numbers through `EquipAnimation`: each piece forges in over 8 ticks (fade up, 85 to 100 percent size,
flying in from its side) and shatters out over the last 18 ticks; the spell keeps the aura at least 40 ticks.

| piece (root bone) | anchored on | stagger (ticks) | flies in from (model px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| helm | head | 0 | above and in front (0, -14, -4) |
| chest | body | 2 | in front (0, 0, -12) |
| waist | body | 4 | below and in front (0, 8, -6) |
| pauldron_r / pauldron_l | right / left arm | 3 | from its own side (-14, -4, 0) / (14, -4, 0) |
| vambrace_r / vambrace_l | right / left arm | 5 | its side and forward (-12, 2, -6) / (12, 2, -6) |
| gauntlet_r / gauntlet_l | right / left arm | 6 | its side, low (-10, 8, -6) / (10, 8, -6) |
| greave_r / greave_l | right / left leg | 7 | from below and in front (-3, 14, -6) / (3, 14, -6) |
| tail | body | 12 | from behind and low (0, 6, 14) |
| back | body | 10 | from behind (0, -2, 12) |


Clips (`scale_armor.animation.json`): `idle` and `move` (played while the player walks or runs). The tail waves down its seven segments (a travelling sine, faster while moving) and the dorsal ridge flexes.

## 4. Constructs

| model (prop kind) | bones | clips | what it is | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `scale_serpent` (`SCALE_1`) | root, s0 to s9, head (12 bones, 40 cubes) | idle (slither), strike (lunge and bite), coil | a wyrm of ten scaled segments with a fanged head, belly plates and a dorsal fin | Companion: follows, slithers to the nearest hostile, bites after 6 ticks (1.7 block ring, damage 6 x scale, Weakness and Slowness) |
| `scale_shield` (`SCALE_2`) | root, plate (2 bones, 9 cubes) | idle (bob and tilt), block | a kite shield of overlapping scales with a pale rim and a glowing boss | Guard: hovers 1.4 blocks in front of its owner, turns projectiles back in the owner's name (the shield flashes) |

All behaviours come from `prop/ShakudoPropKit` (Companion / Guard / Strike / Missile) with the numbers in `prop/ScaleProps`; ticks run inside `SafeCast.tick`, damage goes
through `BalanceLaw.damage` (bosses are capped, control is shortened by `BalanceLaw.controlTicks`). The synced word `param` is laid out by `ShakudoPropState`
(state, the tick the state began, a variant), so the painters play one-shot clips from their first frame.

## 5. VFX (`vfx/client/layer/ScaleLayer.java`)

| shape | meaning of from / to / power | look |
|---|---|---|
| `SCALE_FX1` | cast | five scales on a helix round a streak of water-light, each with a gleam, bursting into a ripple ring and gleams at the target |
| `SCALE_FX2` | zone | a sigil and three ripples running out over the ground in a rim ring, fins swaying round the rim, a ring of scales turning at chest height, gleams drifting up |
| `SCALE_FX3` | impact | a flash, two ripple rings, a crown of fins thrown up, scales thrown out in arcs that settle |

All three stay under the 400 vertex budget (the preview reports the maximum: cast 84, zone 204, impact 100). Sprites are generated by
`tools/gen_scale_textures.py` from `tools/shakudo_vfx_kit.py` (a sigil of the magic's motif, a ring, a flare, a streak, a glow, a crack, a mote and the specific sprites).
The Time Magic VFX files were not touched. Preview: `python3 -B tools/vfx_preview/preview.py scale_cast scale_field scale_impact`.

## 6. Spells (`book/ScaleSpells.java`, appended after the generic five)

| mode | id | name | tier / cost | what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `scale_volley` | Scale Volley | starter | five scales in a fan (small piercing bolts, 5 x EP scale, Weakness) |
| 6 | `scale_serpent` | Scale Serpent | zone | a serpent (two for EP scale 5 or more) for 25 s |
| 7 | `scale_shield` | Scale Shield | mid | a hovering shield for 15 s that turns every projectile back |
| 8 | `scale_mail` | Scale Mail | mid | the armour for 20 s: 25 percent of every blow, 20 percent more against projectiles, Resistance II |
| 9 | `scale_tide` | Shedding Tide | signature, 90 s | a wave of 12 x EP scale damage within 6 blocks with knock-back, the caster sheds every harmful effect and heals 15 percent |

Costs are the usual tiers (starter 8 percent / floor 150, mid 12 / 300, zone 20 / 600, signature 35 / 1200 of the magicule pool, scaled by the cover and gear as for every book);
damage is `GrimoireBook.hurt` (the balance law, EP scale through `EnergyBridge.scale`), control sits behind `GrimoireBook.control`, summons and zones run through `SpellRuntime`,
all casts run inside `SafeCast` (the grimoire already wraps them). A summon lasts up to 50 percent longer for a mighty caster. The armour page uses `ShakudoKit.guard` (cut 0.25, no thorns, +0.20 against projectiles, Resistance II).
The family now has 10 pages (limit 24). Appended family pages move the universal pages (Devil Union, Summon Grimoire ...) up by five modes; those are found by id, not by number, and
carry no unlock bit, so no save is affected.

## 7. Render layers (every pass)

| what | render type | order and scale | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each armour piece, fully forged | `AuraRender.cutout` (entityCutoutNoCull) | every piece's cubes inflated 0.45 px or more over the skin, drawn first | solid, never coplanar with the body |
| each armour piece while forging in or shattering out | `AuraRender.translucent` (entityTranslucent) | same, alpha = forge progress | the Djinn's fade up and out |
| each piece's glow map | `AuraRender.additive` (eyes), full bright | after every skin pass, alpha pulses | emissive, never z-fights |
| armour accents (seal, motes, orbiting sprites) | `AuraRender.additive`, full bright | last | light |
| construct model | cutout, glow map additive and full bright (`GeoDraw.paint`) | model first | solid body, glow on top |
| construct shadow | translucent, 2 cm over the ground | before the model | soft contact shadow |
| construct ground glyph | additive, 3 cm over the ground | after the model | seal / warning |
| effect sprites | `VfxBlend.ALPHA` for the dark solid sprites, `ADD` for glows | alpha first, add last (the vertex buffer sorts by blend order) | back to front |

## 9. How to test in game

`/give` yourself the grimoire (`nusmp:book_scale` skill), summon it, open the pages with `/nusmp grimoire unlock` or by mastery. Cast `attribute_guard` for the armour at once; cast page 5 to 9 for the new spells.
The CI server smoke test runs `magic.scale_rebuild` (`/multiverse mega selftest magic.scale_rebuild`).

## 10. NOT verified (honest)

* Nothing was run in the game. The type check (real javac against API stubs) reports no error in the new or edited files except the known stub artefacts (`MobEffectInstance is abstract`, `deserializeNBT`); a deliberate wrong call was caught.
* Every picture comes from the preview tools, which are re-implementations of the render path (no real lighting, no world depth, an approximate entity shader): proportions, colours, animation and the forge / shatter staging were looked at in them, the in-game look was not.
* The armour sits over the skin with 0.45 px or more of inflate; slim arms are handled by an 80 percent horizontal scale of the arm pieces. A modded player model or a very different skin may show gaps.
* The Mojang `PlayerSkin.model()` check for slim arms and `ModelPart.getInitialPose()` are real API (confirmed in the API dump) but are not exercised by the preview stubs of `PlayerSkin`.
* The balance numbers of the spells and constructs (damage, cost tier, durations) were set by reading the other books, not by play testing.
* The construct hit boxes are estimates from the model sizes; the golem and serpent walk with the simple `walk` of the Bronze constructs and can get stuck on odd terrain.
* No canon text for this magic could be fetched (the network is closed to the wiki); see section 2.

## 1.99.0 armour policy

Scale Magic has no armour in the anime, so since 1.99.0 **no cast puts the temporary armour of this document on the caster** (policy kind NONE). Everything described above as the armour (the render layer, its painter, geo models, textures and generator) is KEPT on disk and registered in `AuraRegistry`, so old saves and tests still resolve; it is simply never started by a page. What the pages did besides the armour (damage cut, thorns, resistance, constructs, effects) is unchanged. Where the armour carried the magic's look, every cast of a family page now sends a small `SCALE_FX2` glow that follows the caster instead (no armour piece is drawn). See `docs/rebuild/armour_policy.md` for the table and the canon reasons; the code is `book.ArmourPolicy` (data: `data/nusmp/armour_policy.json`).

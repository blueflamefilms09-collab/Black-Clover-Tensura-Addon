# Hair Magic rebuild (1.93.0): armour, constructs, effects and spells

The `Hair` wiki attribute used to be one of the 30 generic wiki magics: a bolt, a line, a field, a domain and a guard drawn by the shared wiki layer
(`WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`), with no constructs. It is now rebuilt in the five parts of the rebuild brief (`docs/rebuild/magic_rebuild_brief.md`).

## 1. NEW / REPLACED / KEPT

| part | status | what |
|---|---|---|
| the five generic pages `attribute_bolt`, `attribute_cut`, `attribute_field`, `attribute_domain`, `attribute_guard` (modes 0 to 4, their mastery bits) | KEPT | same ids, order, costs and numbers; their effects are now this magic's own (see below) |
| the temporary armour of the Guard page (5 s, `Aura.WIKI` style 1, a shell and spikes) | REPLACED | by `Aura.HAIR`: a real set of pieces (section 3), forged in and shattered out by `EquipAnimation` like the Djinn full equip. `WikiAura` is untouched and still serves the other 25 wiki magics |
| the shared wiki effects (`WIKI_MAGIC_CAST / FIELD / BURST`) for this magic | REPLACED | by `HAIR_FX1 / FX2 / FX3` drawn by `HairLayer` (section 5). `ElementBook.vfx` maps the three wiki shapes onto them, so the five generic pages need no edit of their own |
| five spells: Hair Lash, Hair Bind, Hair Cocoon, Hair Weave, Mane of a Hundred Strands | NEW | pages appended as modes 5 to 9 through `ExclusiveExt` (section 6) |
| constructs: `hair_tendril`, `hair_cocoon` | NEW | two geo models with animations and glow maps, drawn through the prop system (`PropKind.HAIR_1 / _2`) |
| the armour model `hair_armor` | NEW | one geo model, one root bone per piece |
| particle sprites `hair_*` | NEW | generated by `tools/gen_hair_textures.py` |

| `MagicType.HAIR`, the `WikiStyles` table, `WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`, `WikiBooks` | KEPT | untouched |

## 2. Canon

No wiki text for Hair Magic exists in the repo and the Black Clover wiki could **not** be reached from the build session (no network). The magic is built from what it is called: hair grown, hardened, wound into constructs and thrown (the book's flavour is BODY, its `MagicType` has the SLOW hit). No named user is claimed; every spell is the nearest honest version, not a 1:1 reproduction of a canon spell.

## 3. The temporary armour (`client/aura/HairAura.java`)

A woven armour of pink hair: a mane (crown volume, a fringe, two side locks to the shoulders and a long back mass, swaying), a bodice of braided bands with crossed lacing and a pearl, a sash with hanging strands, braid coils and locks on the shoulders, hair wound round the forearms with tassels, mitts with fringes, wrapped leggings with trailing locks, and on the back a three-panel cloak that sways and four living tendrils of five segments that wave over the shoulders. Strand tips glow (the glow map). Accents: a curling seal under the feet and seven long locks drifting in loops.

Every piece is a cube set of `geo/entity/hair_armor.geo.json` (box UV, pivots, inflate of 0.45 px or more over the skin, a `hair_armor_glow.png` emissive map)
drawn by `ShakudoPieceKit`: anchored on the model part it was built for, so it follows crouching, swimming, swinging and the head; arms are scaled to 80 percent
across for a slim skin. Stagger and fly-in are the Djinn's numbers through `EquipAnimation`: each piece forges in over 8 ticks (fade up, 85 to 100 percent size,
flying in from its side) and shatters out over the last 18 ticks; the spell keeps the aura at least 40 ticks.

| piece (root bone) | anchored on | stagger (ticks) | flies in from (model px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| helm | head | 0 | above and in front (0, -14, -4) |
| chest | body | 2 | in front (0, 0, -12) |
| waist | body | 4 | below and in front (0, 8, -6) |
| pauldron_r / pauldron_l | right / left arm | 3 | from its own side (-14, -4, 0) / (14, -4, 0) |
| vambrace_r / vambrace_l | right / left arm | 5 | its side and forward (-12, 2, -6) / (12, 2, -6) |
| gauntlet_r / gauntlet_l | right / left arm | 6 | its side, low (-10, 8, -6) / (10, 8, -6) |
| greave_r / greave_l | right / left leg | 7 | from below and in front (-3, 14, -6) / (3, 14, -6) |
| back | body | 10 | from behind (0, -2, 12) |


Clips (`hair_armor.animation.json`): `idle` and `move` (played while the player walks or runs). The cloak panels and the mane sway (slowly in idle, streaming while moving) and each tendril waves as a travelling wave down its segments.

## 4. Constructs

| model (prop kind) | bones | clips | what it is | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hair_tendril` (`HAIR_1`) | root, h0 to h9 (11 bones, 12 cubes) | idle, lash, grab, rise | a grasping lock of ten segments ending in a tuft with a pearl | Strike: rises under its target (follows it), lashes after 10 ticks (1.3 block ring, damage 5 x scale, Slowness III) and holds it up to 50 ticks (shortened for bosses), curled round it in the `grab` clip |
| `hair_cocoon` (`HAIR_2`) | root, egg, p0 to p5 (9 bones, 14 cubes) | idle (pulse), open | an egg of woven hair strands with a glowing seam and six petals | Guard: stays on its owner, destroys projectiles within 1.8 blocks, mends 1.5 hit points a second (x EP scale) and bursts into three times that when it ends (opens in its last 16 ticks) |

All behaviours come from `prop/ShakudoPropKit` (Companion / Guard / Strike / Missile) with the numbers in `prop/HairProps`; ticks run inside `SafeCast.tick`, damage goes
through `BalanceLaw.damage` (bosses are capped, control is shortened by `BalanceLaw.controlTicks`). The synced word `param` is laid out by `ShakudoPropState`
(state, the tick the state began, a variant), so the painters play one-shot clips from their first frame.

## 5. VFX (`vfx/client/layer/HairLayer.java`)

| shape | meaning of from / to / power | look |
|---|---|---|
| `HAIR_FX1` | cast | three strands of hair twist along curving arcs from the hand to the target, each ending in a tuft, and wrap round it in a flash and a curl ring |
| `HAIR_FX2` | zone | a curling seal in a rim ring, long locks grow out of the ground all over the field and sway, tufts rise, a pulse runs out |
| `HAIR_FX3` | impact | locks spiral inward round the point and tighten, a pink flash and a curl ring, tufts puff out and settle |

All three stay under the 400 vertex budget (the preview reports the maximum: cast 156, zone 224, impact 76). Sprites are generated by
`tools/gen_hair_textures.py` from `tools/shakudo_vfx_kit.py` (a sigil of the magic's motif, a ring, a flare, a streak, a glow, a crack, a mote and the specific sprites).
The Time Magic VFX files were not touched. Preview: `python3 -B tools/vfx_preview/preview.py hair_cast hair_field hair_impact`.

## 6. Spells (`book/HairSpells.java`, appended after the generic five)

| mode | id | name | tier / cost | what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `hair_lash` | Hair Lash | starter | a tendril under the foe in the crosshair (16 blocks): 5 x scale damage, held briefly |
| 6 | `hair_bind` | Hair Bind | mid, control gate | the tendril kept 80 ticks: the foe is held up to 50 ticks (shortened for bosses) |
| 7 | `hair_cocoon` | Hair Cocoon | mid | the cocoon for 7 s (x up to 1.5 with EP) and Absorption (II, III from EP scale 5) |
| 8 | `hair_weave` | Hair Weave | mid | the armour for 20 s: 20 percent of every blow, attackers bound (Slowness II), a regeneration pulse of 1.5 x EP scale every two seconds |
| 9 | `hair_mane` | Mane of a Hundred Strands | signature, 90 s | for 5 s each second a tendril on each of the three nearest foes within 7 blocks and a 2 percent draw of their magicules (0.5 percent from bosses) to the caster |

Costs are the usual tiers (starter 8 percent / floor 150, mid 12 / 300, zone 20 / 600, signature 35 / 1200 of the magicule pool, scaled by the cover and gear as for every book);
damage is `GrimoireBook.hurt` (the balance law, EP scale through `EnergyBridge.scale`), control sits behind `GrimoireBook.control`, summons and zones run through `SpellRuntime`,
all casts run inside `SafeCast` (the grimoire already wraps them). A summon lasts up to 50 percent longer for a mighty caster. The armour page uses `ShakudoKit.guard` (cut 0.20, no thorns, Slowness II on the attacker, a regeneration pulse every 2 s).
The family now has 10 pages (limit 24). Appended family pages move the universal pages (Devil Union, Summon Grimoire ...) up by five modes; those are found by id, not by number, and
carry no unlock bit, so no save is affected.

## 7. Render layers (every pass)

| what | render type | order and scale | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each armour piece, fully forged | `AuraRender.cutout` (entityCutoutNoCull) | every piece's cubes inflated 0.45 px or more over the skin, drawn first | solid, never coplanar with the body |
| each armour piece while forging in or shattering out | `AuraRender.translucent` (entityTranslucent) | same, alpha = forge progress | the Djinn's fade up and out |
| each piece's glow map | `AuraRender.additive` (eyes), full bright | after every skin pass, alpha pulses | emissive, never z-fights |
| armour accents (seal, motes, orbiting sprites) | `AuraRender.additive`, full bright | last | light |
| construct model | cutout, glow map additive and full bright (`GeoDraw.paint`) | model first | solid body, glow on top |
| construct shadow | translucent, 2 cm over the ground | before the model | soft contact shadow |
| construct ground glyph | additive, 3 cm over the ground | after the model | seal / warning |
| effect sprites | `VfxBlend.ALPHA` for the dark solid sprites, `ADD` for glows | alpha first, add last (the vertex buffer sorts by blend order) | back to front |

## 9. How to test in game

`/give` yourself the grimoire (`nusmp:book_hair` skill), summon it, open the pages with `/nusmp grimoire unlock` or by mastery. Cast `attribute_guard` for the armour at once; cast page 5 to 9 for the new spells.
The CI server smoke test runs `magic.hair_rebuild` (`/multiverse mega selftest magic.hair_rebuild`).

## 10. NOT verified (honest)

* Nothing was run in the game. The type check (real javac against API stubs) reports no error in the new or edited files except the known stub artefacts (`MobEffectInstance is abstract`, `deserializeNBT`); a deliberate wrong call was caught.
* Every picture comes from the preview tools, which are re-implementations of the render path (no real lighting, no world depth, an approximate entity shader): proportions, colours, animation and the forge / shatter staging were looked at in them, the in-game look was not.
* The armour sits over the skin with 0.45 px or more of inflate; slim arms are handled by an 80 percent horizontal scale of the arm pieces. A modded player model or a very different skin may show gaps.
* The Mojang `PlayerSkin.model()` check for slim arms and `ModelPart.getInitialPose()` are real API (confirmed in the API dump) but are not exercised by the preview stubs of `PlayerSkin`.
* The balance numbers of the spells and constructs (damage, cost tier, durations) were set by reading the other books, not by play testing.
* The construct hit boxes are estimates from the model sizes; the golem and serpent walk with the simple `walk` of the Bronze constructs and can get stuck on odd terrain.
* No canon text for this magic could be fetched (the network is closed to the wiki); see section 2.

## 1.99.0 armour policy

Hair Magic has no armour in the anime, so since 1.99.0 **no cast puts the temporary armour of this document on the caster** (policy kind NONE). Everything described above as the armour (the render layer, its painter, geo models, textures and generator) is KEPT on disk and registered in `AuraRegistry`, so old saves and tests still resolve; it is simply never started by a page. What the pages did besides the armour (damage cut, thorns, resistance, constructs, effects) is unchanged. Where the armour carried the magic's look, every cast of a family page now sends a small `HAIR_FX2` glow that follows the caster instead (no armour piece is drawn). See `docs/rebuild/armour_policy.md` for the table and the canon reasons; the code is `book.ArmourPolicy` (data: `data/nusmp/armour_policy.json`).

# Art references from the owner (anime stills)

Targets for the VFX, aura, prop and model work of these attributes. Match the SHAPES and COLOURS below, not pixels.

| Attribute | File | What is in it |
|---|---|---|
| BEAST (upgrade) | `beast.png` | A raised, clawed hand and forearm wrapped in a translucent orange beast-fire: a streaming flame-shaped aura that trails off the arm in ragged licks, hot yellow-orange core, darker red-orange edges, drawn like cel-shaded flame. Reads as an animal's spirit (claws, fur-like licks) rather than fire. |
| BLACK_OIL (new) | `black_oil.webp` | A huge circular pool of glowing magenta-red liquid inside a thick, ragged black tar rim (an oily ring with torn, dripping edges). Four small white candle flames float above the pool with soft white halos. The whole room is lit magenta/pink. The pool surface is churned, like cooled lava or curdled blood-oil, with lighter pink ridges. |
| BLOOD (upgrade) | `blood.webp` | A giant translucent red sphere with a pink glowing rim; inside it is full of white, fibre-like veins and threads (like a cell full of capillaries), a few dark clots. Dozens of thin dark-red threads arc from the caster on the ground up to the sphere and form a web / cage, drawn as long curved lines that cross each other. Black ink-like tendrils pool at the caster's feet. |
| BONE (upgrade) | `bone.webp` | Dozens of huge white skeletal arms and fingers radiating from the caster in all directions (like a spider's legs or a sunburst). Every bone is long and segmented with ring-shaped joints, ending in a long curved claw. Bone is warm white with a light grey outline; the grimoire in the middle glows pale violet-white. |
| BRIAR (new) | `briar.webp` | A towering tangled mass of pale-yellow ribbon-like vines, each a flat strap with small triangular thorns along it, coiling and looping around a captured figure. Small blue roses grow out of the tangle. The caster's grimoire glows cyan-blue in front of it. |

Palette hints from the stills: Beast orange `#ffa030` / `#ff5a14`; Black Oil magenta `#ff2d9a` over black `#1a0a14` with white flames; Blood `#ff3b3b` with white fibre and dark `#7a0c14` threads; Bone `#f4f1e6` with grey `#9a968a`, violet `#c8b8ff` glow; Briar `#e8e2a0` with blue `#4aa3ff` flowers and cyan `#3ee6ff` book glow.

# Shakudo Magic rebuild (1.93.0): armour, constructs, effects and spells

The `Shakudo` wiki attribute used to be one of the 30 generic wiki magics: a bolt, a line, a field, a domain and a guard drawn by the shared wiki layer
(`WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`), with no constructs. It is now rebuilt in the five parts of the rebuild brief (`docs/rebuild/magic_rebuild_brief.md`).

## 2. Canon

The repo's own wiki extract (`.github/wiki/copper_magic.txt`, `bronze_magic.txt`) says "For the magic used by Cesc, see Shakudo Magic": Shakudo is a metal magic of the copper / bronze family used by Cesc. The wiki (blackclover.fandom.com) could **not** be reached from the build session (no network), so no spell list was read; what follows is the nearest honest version, built from what Shakudo is (the Japanese black-purple patinated alloy of copper with a little gold, used on sword fittings and finished with fine gold inlay) and from the metal-construct family of the book (Bronze, Copper, Iron). The look follows the material: a lacquered black-violet that takes gold lines. Where the anime has a named user it is Cesc; no spell of his is reproduced 1:1.

## 3. The temporary armour (`client/aura/ShakudoAura.java`)

A kabuto-style full armour in black-violet shakudo with gold inlay (the inlay is the glow map): a helm with a gilt brow band, a gem, cheek guards, three neck lames and a gilt crescent crest; a lamellar do with gilt bands and a crest disc; tassets at the waist; three layered sode plates on each shoulder; kote; gauntlets with knuckle plates; haidate, a gilt knee, shin plates and boots; on the back a gilt ring with a glowing rune on a pole (it turns) and two sash tails (they sway). Accents: a spinning gold seal under the feet and drifting gold leaf.

Every piece is a cube set of `geo/entity/shakudo_armor.geo.json` (box UV, pivots, inflate of 0.45 px or more over the skin, a `shakudo_armor_glow.png` emissive map)
drawn by `ShakudoPieceKit`: anchored on the model part it was built for, so it follows crouching, swimming, swinging and the head; arms are scaled to 80 percent
across for a slim skin. Stagger and fly-in are the Djinn's numbers through `EquipAnimation`: each piece forges in over 8 ticks (fade up, 85 to 100 percent size,
flying in from its side) and shatters out over the last 18 ticks; the spell keeps the aura at least 40 ticks.

| piece (root bone) | anchored on | stagger (ticks) | flies in from (model px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| helm | head | 0 | above and in front (0, -14, -4) |
| chest | body | 2 | in front (0, 0, -12) |
| waist | body | 4 | below and in front (0, 8, -6) |
| pauldron_r / pauldron_l | right / left arm | 3 | from its own side (-14, -4, 0) / (14, -4, 0) |
| vambrace_r / vambrace_l | right / left arm | 5 | its side and forward (-12, 2, -6) / (12, 2, -6) |
| gauntlet_r / gauntlet_l | right / left arm | 6 | its side, low (-10, 8, -6) / (10, 8, -6) |
| greave_r / greave_l | right / left leg | 7 | from below and in front (-3, 14, -6) / (3, 14, -6) |
| back | body | 10 | from behind (0, -2, 12) |


Clips (`shakudo_armor.animation.json`): `idle` and `move` (played while the player walks or runs). The ring turns (12 s a turn, twice as fast while moving) and the two sash tails sway.

## 4. Constructs

| model (prop kind) | bones | clips | what it is | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `shakudo_golem` (`SHAKUDO_1`) | root, hips, torso, head, arm_r/l + fore_r/l, leg_r/l + shin_r/l, sash (13 bones, 42 cubes) | idle, walk, strike (overhead double fist), crumble | a 2.3 block barrel-chested alloy golem with a glowing gilt core, a kabuto head with one eye slit, heavy sode, big fists | Companion: follows, chases the nearest hostile within 12 blocks, strikes after 11 ticks (2 block ring, damage 9 x scale, knock-back); crumbles over its last 26 ticks |
| `shakudo_bulwark` (`SHAKUDO_2`) | root, slab, seal (3 bones, 9 cubes) | idle (the seal breathes), rise | a slab of shakudo in a gilt frame with a glowing seal | Guard: solid, health 60 x EP, destroys projectiles within 2.4 blocks (the seal flashes), sinks in its last 10 ticks |

All behaviours come from `prop/ShakudoPropKit` (Companion / Guard / Strike / Missile) with the numbers in `prop/ShakudoProps`; ticks run inside `SafeCast.tick`, damage goes
through `BalanceLaw.damage` (bosses are capped, control is shortened by `BalanceLaw.controlTicks`). The synced word `param` is laid out by `ShakudoPropState`
(state, the tick the state began, a variant), so the painters play one-shot clips from their first frame.

## 5. VFX (`vfx/client/layer/ShakudoLayer.java`)

| shape | meaning of from / to / power | look |
|---|---|---|
| `SHAKUDO_FX1` | cast: from = hand, to = target, power = size | a gold seal contracts on the hand, a dark alloy lance with a glowing inlay line and three trailing plates flies to the target shedding gold leaf, ends in a flash, a ring and chips |
| `SHAKUDO_FX2` | zone: from = centre, power = radius | a gold seal turns on cracked ground in a rim ring, six inlaid slabs stand round the rim with gold running up them, a ring turns overhead, leaf rises, a shock ring pulses |
| `SHAKUDO_FX3` | impact: from = centre, to - from = direction hint, power = scale | a giant dark slab drops onto the point, gold flash, two shock rings, cracks with gold in them, chips and leaf fly out |

All three stay under the 400 vertex budget (the preview reports the maximum: cast 80, zone 236, impact 96). Sprites are generated by
`tools/gen_shakudo_textures.py` from `tools/shakudo_vfx_kit.py` (a sigil of the magic's motif, a ring, a flare, a streak, a glow, a crack, a mote and the specific sprites).
The Time Magic VFX files were not touched. Preview: `python3 -B tools/vfx_preview/preview.py shakudo_cast shakudo_field shakudo_impact`.

## 6. Spells (`book/ShakudoSpells.java`, appended after the generic five)

| mode | id | name | tier / cost | what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `shakudo_lance` | Gilded Inlay Lance | starter | a lance of inlaid alloy along 14 blocks: every target on the line (up to 12) takes 11 x EP scale, is slowed and thrown back |
| 6 | `shakudo_golem` | Shakudo Golem | zone | a golem for 25 s (x up to 1.5 with EP), one at a time |
| 7 | `shakudo_bulwark` | Shakudo Bulwark | mid | a solid slab 3 blocks ahead for 10 s, one at a time |
| 8 | `shakudo_plate_armor` | Shakudo Plate Armor | mid | the armour for 20 s: 30 percent of every blow (35 percent melee) softened, melee attackers staggered and cut for 2.5 + 0.4 x EP scale, Resistance II |
| 9 | `shakudo_colossus` | Shakudo Colossus | signature, 120 s | a golem of scale 1.7 (heavier fist: damage x scale) for 20 s |

Costs are the usual tiers (starter 8 percent / floor 150, mid 12 / 300, zone 20 / 600, signature 35 / 1200 of the magicule pool, scaled by the cover and gear as for every book);
damage is `GrimoireBook.hurt` (the balance law, EP scale through `EnergyBridge.scale`), control sits behind `GrimoireBook.control`, summons and zones run through `SpellRuntime`,
all casts run inside `SafeCast` (the grimoire already wraps them). A summon lasts up to 50 percent longer for a mighty caster. The armour page uses `ShakudoKit.guard` (cut 0.30, thorns 2.5 + 0.4 x mighty, Resistance II, the Shakudo answer staggers the attacker).
The family now has 10 pages (limit 24). Appended family pages move the universal pages (Devil Union, Summon Grimoire ...) up by five modes; those are found by id, not by number, and
carry no unlock bit, so no save is affected.

## 7. Render layers (every pass)

| what | render type | order and scale | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each armour piece, fully forged | `AuraRender.cutout` (entityCutoutNoCull) | every piece's cubes inflated 0.45 px or more over the skin, drawn first | solid, never coplanar with the body |
| each armour piece while forging in or shattering out | `AuraRender.translucent` (entityTranslucent) | same, alpha = forge progress | the Djinn's fade up and out |
| each piece's glow map | `AuraRender.additive` (eyes), full bright | after every skin pass, alpha pulses | emissive, never z-fights |
| armour accents (seal, motes, orbiting sprites) | `AuraRender.additive`, full bright | last | light |
| construct model | cutout, glow map additive and full bright (`GeoDraw.paint`) | model first | solid body, glow on top |
| construct shadow | translucent, 2 cm over the ground | before the model | soft contact shadow |
| construct ground glyph | additive, 3 cm over the ground | after the model | seal / warning |
| effect sprites | `VfxBlend.ALPHA` for the dark solid sprites, `ADD` for glows | alpha first, add last (the vertex buffer sorts by blend order) | back to front |

## 9. How to test in game

`/give` yourself the grimoire (`nusmp:book_shakudo` skill), summon it, open the pages with `/nusmp grimoire unlock` or by mastery. Cast `attribute_guard` for the armour at once; cast page 5 to 9 for the new spells.
The CI server smoke test runs `magic.shakudo_rebuild` (`/multiverse mega selftest magic.shakudo_rebuild`).

## 10. NOT verified (honest)

* Nothing was run in the game. The type check (real javac against API stubs) reports no error in the new or edited files except the known stub artefacts (`MobEffectInstance is abstract`, `deserializeNBT`); a deliberate wrong call was caught.
* Every picture comes from the preview tools, which are re-implementations of the render path (no real lighting, no world depth, an approximate entity shader): proportions, colours, animation and the forge / shatter staging were looked at in them, the in-game look was not.
* The armour sits over the skin with 0.45 px or more of inflate; slim arms are handled by an 80 percent horizontal scale of the arm pieces. A modded player model or a very different skin may show gaps.
* The Mojang `PlayerSkin.model()` check for slim arms and `ModelPart.getInitialPose()` are real API (confirmed in the API dump) but are not exercised by the preview stubs of `PlayerSkin`.
* The balance numbers of the spells and constructs (damage, cost tier, durations) were set by reading the other books, not by play testing.
* The construct hit boxes are estimates from the model sizes; the golem and serpent walk with the simple `walk` of the Bronze constructs and can get stuck on odd terrain.
* No canon text for this magic could be fetched (the network is closed to the wiki); see section 2.

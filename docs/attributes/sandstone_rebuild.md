# Sandstone Magic: the 1.93.0 rebuild

Sandstone Magic is layered stone that turns to sand and sets hard again. It is a wiki attribute (`MagicType.SANDSTONE`, colour `0xFFD5A76C`); its grimoire used to be the generic five-page wiki book. The rebuild gives it its own armour, constructs, effects and spells. The family shares code with Rock, Red Ochre, Stone, Earth and Mineral (`EarthKit`, `EarthArmorKit`, `EarthFamilyLayer`, `EarthPropPainter`); each magic has its own models, palette, silhouette and spells.

## 2. Canon

Only one spell of Sandstone Magic is named on the Black Clover wiki: **Variable Hardness Prison** ("With an open grimoire, the user generates a large block of sandstone and moves toward the target. The block dissolves into sand and reforms around the target. Once hardened, the target is left to suffocate."). The wiki could be reached only through a search summary in the build session (the fandom pages themselves were not reachable), so no user, captain or look beyond that text could be checked. The prison is built after it: block leaves the grimoire (cast effect), cage forms round the target (the cage's `form` clip, walls closing from four sides and the roof dropping), the target is held and pressed (damage ticks, rooted), the cage crumbles. Everything else (bolt, slabs, sphinx, sandstorm) is the nearest honest version of "sandstone that is sand and stone at will" and is not claimed as canon. The look (tan strata, a dune collar, an hourglass) is invented from the name.

## 3. Armour: the Dune Plate

Fourteen pieces on the player's model, each anchored on the part it was built for and driven by `EquipAnimation` (staggered 0 to 9 ticks, flying in from its own side, fading up and growing 85 to 100 percent over 8 ticks, shattering out over the last 18; the same as the Djinn full equip). Slim and wide arms both fit (the arm pieces are inflated past the wide arm).

| Piece | Anchor | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| helm | head | a stack of strata slabs with a brim, a stepped crest, a neck flap |
| chest | body | five strata plates (alternating light and dark sand) with an hourglass in a gold-lit window |
| belt | body | a wrap of layered tassets |
| mantle | body | the dune collar (two flaring rings) and strata draped down the back |
| pauldron_r / _l | arms | three overlapping slabs and a spill of grains |
| vambrace_r / _l | arms | stacked rings with sand-gold seams |
| gauntlet_r / _l | arms | a block fist in two strata |
| greave_r / _l | legs | stacked boot slabs, a knee wedge |
| back | body | the monolith: a tall strata slab with a glowing hourglass hovers behind the back (sway and bob), two slabs orbit it |
| extra | head | a halo of eight slabs turning over the head |

Files: `geo/entity/sandstone_<piece>.geo.json` (+ animation for `back`, `extra`), `textures/entity/sandstone_<piece>.png` + `_glow.png`; generated by `tools/gen_earth_family_armor.py` (set `sandstone_set`) with painters in `tools/earth_family_lib.py` (material `sandstone`: strata bands, grain, glowing seams). Glow colour `0xFFD98A`. The armour is style 12 on `Aura.WIKI` (its `WikiStyles` index, so the old guard page already sent the right number); `EarthAura` answers it and hands every other style to `WikiAura`.

## 5. Constructs

- `sandstone_sphinx`: 27 cubes, bones body / head / four legs / two tail segments / jaw; clips idle (breath, tail sway), walk (diagonal gait), attack (pounce with jaw). 
- `sandstone_cage`: 17 cubes, four walls, a roof and a floor sigil as bones; clips form (walls slide in, roof drops, 0.7 s), idle, crumble (walls sink, 0.9 s). The painter pours sand from the roof and turns the hourglass sigil under it.

Both ride `PropKind.EARTH_CONSTRUCT` (one appended line in `PropKind`); behaviour in `prop/EarthConstructProps`, look in `client/prop/EarthPropPainter`.

## 6. Effects and render layers

`EarthFamilyLayer` (read from the colour `0xFFD5A76C`, `vfx/EarthFxStyle`): CAST is a swirling cloud of grains with a sand streak; ZONE a stained floor with the hourglass sigil and curtains of sand pouring from the rim; BURST grains blown up in a spiral. Sprites `earthfam_sandstone_*` and the shared `earthfam_all_trail` come from `tools/gen_earth_family_textures.py`.

| Pass | Render type | Why |
|---|---|---|
| armour piece, fully forged | `entityCutoutNoCull` (GeoDraw CUTOUT) | solid, drawn first |
| armour piece forging or shattering | `entityTranslucent` | a cutout pass cannot fade |
| piece glow map | additive (`RenderType.eyes`), full bright | the hourglass and seams shine |
| construct model | CUTOUT; glow additive | as above |
| shadow, dust, sand streams | translucent sprite 2 cm over the floor | no coplanar surfaces |
| sigil, glints | additive | light goes last |

## 7. Self-tests and checks

`magic.earth_family_rebuild.books` and `magic.earth_family_rebuild.constructs` (registered from `EarthConstructProps.init`), `EarthFamilyRebuildTest` (pure: param word, palettes, style numbers against `WikiStyles`, page ids and order read from the sources, every asset present). Offline: `tools/geo_check.py` on all models, `tools/render_preview` scenes `ef_sandstone_close`, `efm_sandstone_*`, `efp_sphinx`, `efp_cage`, `tools/vfx_preview` scenes `efx_sandstone_*` (vertex budget at most 256 of 400).

## 8. NOT verified

Nothing was run in the game. The render and effect previews are re-implementations against stubs; lighting, the exact vertex order of the armour on a real skin, slim-arm fit, first-person hiding of the helm, the cage's hold on real mobs and the pounce's physics are unchecked. Page balance numbers are first estimates. The Black Clover wiki itself was not reachable, so the canon section rests on one search summary and on memory.

## 1.99.0 armour policy

Sandstone Magic has no armour in the anime, so since 1.99.0 **no cast puts the temporary armour of this document on the caster** (policy kind NONE). Everything described above as the armour (the render layer, its painter, geo models, textures and generator) is KEPT on disk and registered in `AuraRegistry`, so old saves and tests still resolve; it is simply never started by a page. What the pages did besides the armour (damage cut, thorns, resistance, constructs, effects) is unchanged. Where the armour carried the magic's look, every cast of a family page now sends a small `EARTHFAM_ZONE` glow that follows the caster instead (no armour piece is drawn). See `docs/rebuild/armour_policy.md` for the table and the canon reasons; the code is `book.ArmourPolicy` (data: `data/nusmp/armour_policy.json`).

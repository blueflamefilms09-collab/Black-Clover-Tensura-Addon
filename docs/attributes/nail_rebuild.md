# Nail Magic rebuild (1.93.0): armour, constructs, effects and spells

The `Nail` wiki attribute used to be one of the 30 generic wiki magics: a bolt, a line, a field, a domain and a guard drawn by the shared wiki layer
(`WikiMagicLayer`, `WikiAura`), with no constructs. It is now rebuilt in the five parts of the rebuild brief (`docs/rebuild/magic_rebuild_brief.md`).

| part | status | what |

## 2. Canon

No wiki text for Nail Magic exists in the repo (`.github/wiki/spell_curse_filled_nails.txt` is a Curse Magic spell) and the Black Clover wiki could **not** be reached from the build session (no network). The magic is built from what it is called: iron nails driven through things and fingernails grown into claws (the book's flavour is BODY with METAL). No named user is claimed; every spell is the nearest honest version, not a 1:1 reproduction of a canon spell.

## 3. The temporary armour (`client/aura/NailAura.java`)

Dark studded iron with rusted patches and ember-hot heads: a cap with a crown of eight long nails; a brigandine with three nails driven into the sternum; nail-head tassets with two hip nails; on each shoulder two studded plates and four nails; bracers with three nails driven through; gauntlets with five long pale nail-claws; studded greaves with knee and shin nails; on the back the nail wheel, a hub with ten long nails that turns. Accents: a seal of nails under the feet, ten small nails orbiting the waist, ember sparks.

Every piece is a cube set of `geo/entity/nail_armor.geo.json` (box UV, pivots, inflate of 0.45 px or more over the skin, a `nail_armor_glow.png` emissive map)
drawn by `ShakudoPieceKit`: anchored on the model part it was built for, so it follows crouching, swimming, swinging and the head; arms are scaled to 80 percent
across for a slim skin. Stagger and fly-in are the Djinn's numbers through `EquipAnimation`: each piece forges in over 8 ticks (fade up, 85 to 100 percent size,
flying in from its side) and shatters out over the last 18 ticks; the spell keeps the aura at least 40 ticks.

| piece (root bone) | anchored on | stagger (ticks) | flies in from (model px) |
|---|---|---|---|
| helm | head | 0 | above and in front (0, -14, -4) |
| chest | body | 2 | in front (0, 0, -12) |
| waist | body | 4 | below and in front (0, 8, -6) |
| pauldron_r / pauldron_l | right / left arm | 3 | from its own side (-14, -4, 0) / (14, -4, 0) |
| vambrace_r / vambrace_l | right / left arm | 5 | its side and forward (-12, 2, -6) / (12, 2, -6) |
| gauntlet_r / gauntlet_l | right / left arm | 6 | its side, low (-10, 8, -6) / (10, 8, -6) |
| greave_r / greave_l | right / left leg | 7 | from below and in front (-3, 14, -6) / (3, 14, -6) |
| back | body | 10 | from behind (0, -2, 12) |


Clips (`nail_armor.animation.json`): `idle` and `move` (played while the player walks or runs). The nail wheel turns (10 s a turn, 2.5 times as fast while moving).

## 4. Constructs

| model (prop kind) | bones | clips | what it is | behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `nail_bolt` (`NAIL_1`) | root, nail (2 bones, 7 cubes) | idle, fly (spinning) | a giant driven nail: a round head, a long tapered steel shaft, a hot band | Missile: flies straight (1.6 blocks a tick, yaw and pitch from the flight), hits the first foe (damage 6 x scale, Slowness III) or sticks in the wall until its life ends |
| `nail_hammer` (`NAIL_2`) | root, haft, head (3 bones, 9 cubes) | idle, slam | a heavy iron war hammer with glowing faces on a long haft | Strike: hangs head down over the target with a seal warning for 14 ticks, falls and strikes (2.2 block ring, damage 12 x scale, Slowness IV) and holds the target for 36 ticks (shortened for bosses) |

All behaviours come from `prop/ShakudoPropKit` (Companion / Guard / Strike / Missile) with the numbers in `prop/NailProps`; ticks run inside `SafeCast.tick`, damage goes
through `BalanceLaw.damage` (bosses are capped, control is shortened by `BalanceLaw.controlTicks`). The synced word `param` is laid out by `ShakudoPropState`
(state, the tick the state began, a variant), so the painters play one-shot clips from their first frame.

## 5. VFX (`vfx/client/layer/NailLayer.java`)

| shape | meaning of from / to / power | look |
|---|---|---|
| `NAIL_FX1` | cast | a seal of nails contracts on the hand, three nails fly point first with hot heads and spark trails and are driven in with a flash and a ring |
| `NAIL_FX2` | zone | a seal of nails in a rim ring, nails fall out of the sky over the field and stay driven in the ground, sparks rise, a pulse runs out |
| `NAIL_FX3` | impact | a giant hammer drops onto the point, flash, two shock rings, cracks with ember in them, nails shoot out in a star and stand for a moment, sparks fly |

All three stay under the 400 vertex budget (the preview reports the maximum: cast 80, zone 192, impact 116). Sprites are generated by
`tools/gen_nail_textures.py` from `tools/shakudo_vfx_kit.py` (a sigil of the magic's motif, a ring, a flare, a streak, a glow, a crack, a mote and the specific sprites).
The Time Magic VFX files were not touched. Preview: `python3 -B tools/vfx_preview/preview.py nail_cast nail_field nail_impact`.

## 6. Spells (`book/NailSpells.java`, appended after the generic five)

| mode | id | name | tier / cost | what |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | `nail_barrage` | Nail Barrage | starter | three nails (five from EP scale 4), damage 6 x scale each, Slowness III, stay in walls |
| 6 | `nail_driver` | Nail Driver | mid, control gate | the hammer on the foe in the crosshair (18 blocks), pins it for 36 ticks |
| 7 | `nail_mail` | Nail Mail | mid | the armour for 20 s: 25 percent of every blow, attackers pricked for 2 + 0.3 x EP scale and slowed, Resistance II |
| 8 | `nail_pin` | Pinning Nails | mid, control gate | three nails round the foe's feet: Slowness VII and no jumping for 60 ticks (shortened for bosses) |
| 9 | `nail_storm` | Nail Storm | signature, 90 s | for 4 s a nail every 4 ticks in a 6 block ring round the crosshair, 4 x EP scale each, Slowness III |

Costs are the usual tiers (starter 8 percent / floor 150, mid 12 / 300, zone 20 / 600, signature 35 / 1200 of the magicule pool, scaled by the cover and gear as for every book);
damage is `GrimoireBook.hurt` (the balance law, EP scale through `EnergyBridge.scale`), control sits behind `GrimoireBook.control`, summons and zones run through `SpellRuntime`,
all casts run inside `SafeCast` (the grimoire already wraps them). A summon lasts up to 50 percent longer for a mighty caster. The armour page uses `ShakudoKit.guard` (cut 0.25, thorns 2 + 0.3 x mighty and Slowness III on the attacker, Resistance II).
The family now has 10 pages (limit 24). Appended family pages move the universal pages (Devil Union, Summon Grimoire ...) up by five modes; those are found by id, not by number, and
carry no unlock bit, so no save is affected.

## 7. Render layers (every pass)

| what | render type | order and scale | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| each armour piece, fully forged | `AuraRender.cutout` (entityCutoutNoCull) | every piece's cubes inflated 0.45 px or more over the skin, drawn first | solid, never coplanar with the body |
| each armour piece while forging in or shattering out | `AuraRender.translucent` (entityTranslucent) | same, alpha = forge progress | the Djinn's fade up and out |
| each piece's glow map | `AuraRender.additive` (eyes), full bright | after every skin pass, alpha pulses | emissive, never z-fights |
| armour accents (seal, motes, orbiting sprites) | `AuraRender.additive`, full bright | last | light |
| construct model | cutout, glow map additive and full bright (`GeoDraw.paint`) | model first | solid body, glow on top |
| construct shadow | translucent, 2 cm over the ground | before the model | soft contact shadow |
| construct ground glyph | additive, 3 cm over the ground | after the model | seal / warning |
| effect sprites | `VfxBlend.ALPHA` for the dark solid sprites, `ADD` for glows | alpha first, add last (the vertex buffer sorts by blend order) | back to front |

## 9. How to test in game

`/give` yourself the grimoire (`nusmp:book_nail` skill), summon it, open the pages with `/nusmp grimoire unlock` or by mastery. Cast `attribute_guard` for the armour at once; cast page 5 to 9 for the new spells.
The CI server smoke test runs `magic.nail_rebuild` (`/multiverse mega selftest magic.nail_rebuild`).

## 10. NOT verified (honest)

* Nothing was run in the game. The type check (real javac against API stubs) reports no error in the new or edited files except the known stub artefacts (`MobEffectInstance is abstract`, `deserializeNBT`); a deliberate wrong call was caught.
* Every picture comes from the preview tools, which are re-implementations of the render path (no real lighting, no world depth, an approximate entity shader): proportions, colours, animation and the forge / shatter staging were looked at in them, the in-game look was not.
* The armour sits over the skin with 0.45 px or more of inflate; slim arms are handled by an 80 percent horizontal scale of the arm pieces. A modded player model or a very different skin may show gaps.
* The Mojang `PlayerSkin.model()` check for slim arms and `ModelPart.getInitialPose()` are real API (confirmed in the API dump) but are not exercised by the preview stubs of `PlayerSkin`.
* The balance numbers of the spells and constructs (damage, cost tier, durations) were set by reading the other books, not by play testing.
* The construct hit boxes are estimates from the model sizes; the golem and serpent walk with the simple `walk` of the Bronze constructs and can get stuck on odd terrain.
* No canon text for this magic could be fetched (the network is closed to the wiki); see section 2.

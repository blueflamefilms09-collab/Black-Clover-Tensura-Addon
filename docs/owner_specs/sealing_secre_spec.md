# Sealing Magic: basic random combo + Secre Swallowtail's exclusive magic (owner spec)

## Part 1: Basic Sealing Magic and the random combo
Visuals: dynamic magic circles from a pool of designs (fantasy, sci-fi / cosmic, gothic, anime / manga) with distinct inner runes and outer rings; glowing orbs orbiting the circle or the target.
Random combo (data driven): when a generic Sealing Magic user casts a basic seal, the spell rolls:
1. Base shape: Hexagram (binds movement), Concentric rings (silences magic), Star (drains mana).
2. Elemental tint: Red (burns while sealed), Blue (slows), Purple (gravity crush).
3. Orb count 1 to 5 (more orbs = longer seal duration or higher spell tier).

## Part 2: Secre Swallowtail's exclusive magic (only with Secre's grimoire; light-blue / cyan neon aesthetic)
1. **Seal Magic: Branching Array (Mana Method)**: bright glowing blue core from the hands; sharp geometric blue lines and square-edged branches extend rapidly in a 3D web. AoE lock-down or unlocking spell (opening sealed dungeons / chests).
2. **Seal Magic: Eternal Prison**: the target is instantly encased in a massive translucent glowing blue 3D cube; fast white speed lines / sparks shoot off the cube's edges. Hard CC: the trapped entity cannot move, take damage or deal damage until the cube breaks.
3. **Seal Magic: Wound Sealing**: Secre's grimoire floats open emitting a blue aura; the caster points at an ally and a stream of gentle blue energy seals their wounds (restores HP, removes debuffs).
4. **Seal Magic: Orbital Bind**: swirling high-speed blue rings / lines orbit continuously around an ally in a spherical pattern; a lingering buff (magic resistance, anti-magic synergy).

Animation: Secre casting Eternal Prison: both arms thrust forward, hands open.
Rendering: translucent glowing cube over the entity; rotating VfxShape meshes or particles for the rings and the 3D branching lines.

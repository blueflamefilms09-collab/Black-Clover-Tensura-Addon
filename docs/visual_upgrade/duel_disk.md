# Duel disk look for the deck relics (slice `duel_disk`, release 1.110.0)

Owner request: "for any deck called or deck-based relics, use the Yu-Gi-Oh duel disk as a base for how it should look on a player when worn in the corresponding Curios slot."

**Honest notes first.** The duel disk is a third-party design (the arm mounted card game device of a well known series). It is used as a **visual base only**: a curved plate strapped on the
forearm, a fan of five card zones, a deck holder with a stack, a life-point strip. No asset of the series was used; every shape, texture and colour is generated by the scripts under
`tools/visual_upgrade/duel_disk/` and `tools/deck_summon/`. **Nothing here has been seen in a game.** Everything was measured from the files and drawn with a software rasteriser
(`docs/previews/duel_disk/`): orthographic, no light map, no bloom, not a screenshot.

## Which relics are deck relics

Searched: every `RelicDef` (`relics/wave/**`), `docs/relic_*.md` / `docs/relics_of_many_worlds_part4.md`, the lang descriptions and the worn / hand catalogs for "deck", "card", "tarot", "duel".

| relic | id | Curios slot | grade | worn as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Deck of Many Things | `deck_of_many_things` | charm | GOD | duel disk, left forearm, theme black enamel + gold, "XXII FATE", star inlay, floating fate cards |
| Pact of the Deck | `pact_of_the_deck` | charm | SPECIAL | duel disk, left forearm, theme ivory + gold + red wax, the four suits and a joker on the five zones, orbiting playing cards |

Not covered, on purpose: the hero weapon *book* page decks (`heroweapon/weapon/book/PageDeck`, `BookDecks`) are weapon data, not relics; the Archivist's Starbound Codex, Kazuma's Adventurer
Card and the Adventurer's Guild Card are a book and ID cards, not decks. There is no Deck Case, Elemental Deck or "deck of other anime" relic in the mod today.

Both are charms (Curios slot `charm`, no slot change: a relic keeps its slot). The disk is anchored to the **left arm** (`anchor larm`), as in the series.

## What was built

* **The disk** (`duel_disk_<id>_worn`, `_hand`, each with a `_lod`): a C shaped plate (the curved shell, five segments) round the outer, front and back of the left forearm, two straps with
  buckles, a hub with a gem, **five blades** fanned on the hub (the card zone fan, a `swing` group that breathes), a monster zone on each blade (a thin dark plate with a glowing frame and
  the emblem painted on: the suits for the pact, a star for the deck) and a spell zone nearer the hub, a **life-point strip** along the rim of the fan and the lower edge of the plate, a
  **deck holder** (back plate, two side walls, floor) with a **visible stack of nine cards** (card back painted: lattice and border) and a top card that bobs out of the stack.
* **Grade changes the finish**: GOD (Deck of Many Things): 512 skin, glow map, the **nebula** inlay round the hub (STAR quads), the **halo** shapes (a seal ring in front of the stack, light shafts
  above it, a lens flare), obsidian and gold with filigree. SPECIAL (Pact of the Deck): 256 skin, glow map for the life points and the frames, only the small lens flare; silver, gold, leather,
  ordinary finish. Both get the grade passes of the library (rim, shimmer, motes) like every worn relic.
* **The floating cards of the Deck of Many Things are kept**, copied **quad for quad** (uv, normals, flags, animation groups `fate_cards` and `fate_cards_far`, their motion numbers) from the
  older model into `deck_of_many_things_cards_worn` / `_cards_hand`; only moved by one constant offset, now anchored on the same arm so they hover **beside and above the disk**
  (centre 9.5 px out, 2.6 px down the arm) and never cross the plate, even when the arm swings. The pact has its own ambient group `pact_cards` (seven playing cards on a spin).
  The ambient layers of the older fx file (`card fan`, `fate cards`, `far fate cards`, `starry sky`, `fate seal`, `lens flare`, `light shafts`, the gold dust and glints) still find a
  group each: the disk carries groups of the same names for the fan, sky, seal, flare and shafts. The pact got `fx/pact_of_the_deck.json`.
* **Placement**: the disk is written at 1/0.85 because the worn library draws a relic piece at `WornPlacer.BODY_SCALE` about its anchor; the final numbers are in the arm frame of the
  player model (arm box x -1..3, y -2..10, the second skin layer box x -1.25..3.25): every static vertex stands outside that layer box (a test checks it, and that `WornMesh.clearOf`
  returns the model unchanged), the plate lies on the forearm (y 1.7 to 10.0), the holder reaches 6.1 px out. Slim arms: the library already moves arm pieces 0.5 px toward the body.
  First person: an arm anchored piece goes through the same draw path as the other arm anchored worn relics (89 library models are on arms), nothing special was added.
* **Held** (`relic_hand`): the same disk, centred, with its card stack, plus (Deck of Many Things) the floating cards exactly as they were; `style=palm`.
* **Behind the flag**: everything is behind the existing `visual.upgrade` client option; off, the 1.109 looks come back (the Pact of the Deck its library charm, the Deck its 1.102 model).

## Placement among other charms (a hook in the worn code)

`WornPlacer.place(slot, index)` puts the 2nd, 3rd ... charm on the hips and the back. A disk on the arm must not be moved there when another charm is worn, so `WornPlacer.armMounted(model)` and
`place(slot, index, model)` (pure, new, tested) return no offset for the disk models and the floating cards. **One line of `WornLibDraw` changed** (it passes the model name to the placer);
nothing else of the draw code. If both deck relics are worn at once their disks stand on the same place (one arm): documented, not solved.

## The card summon (owner's follow up) and the removed flat picture

* **REMOVED (owner's order, rule 2 exception)**: the flat floating picture of the drawn card. It was the `SPELL_CARD` effect of the act effect tables (`act_vfx.txt`, drawn by `AuraLayer.card`: a camera
  facing picture of the grimoire icon in a gold frame). Removed: `AuraLayer.card()` and its icon / badge caches, the `SPELL_CARD` case and shape registration, `VfxTextures.CARD_FRAME`, the texture
  `textures/particle/card_frame.png`, and every use in data (`act_vfx.txt` x5 lines, `hand_of_vecna.json`, the two generators). The `VfxShape.SPELL_CARD` constant stays (ordinals are saved); nothing
  draws it. The chat lines ("Card 1 of 22: ...") and the card effects are untouched. Guard: `NoFlatCardPictureTest` (source scan: no client billboard / plane in a card or deck method, no
  `CARD_FRAME`, no `SPELL_CARD` in code or data, the summon layer draws a box).
* **NEW**: the summon (`vfx/client/layer/DeckSummonLayer`, shape `DECK_SUMMON` appended last): about two seconds (40 ticks): SLIDE (the top card leaves the deck slot of the disk), FLASH (ten short radial
  light streaks), SLAP (the card lands on a zone with a shockwave ring), FLIP (a face-up turn, 180 degrees), COLUMN (a hologram column of light in the alignment colour: good green and gold, neutral aqua,
  bad red and purple), DISSOLVE (the card fades, sparks of the mod's own particles, `Fx.burst` with `FxThemes.RELIC_WAVE_30`, through the client particle budget). The card is a **thin box** (back, face,
  gold rim strips) on the atlas `textures/particle/deck_summon.png` (22 faces drawn from the alignment colours and a motif, a back, a rim, a streak, a ring, a column), never a picture floating in the air.
  The server only sends the drawn card number, its zone and alignment in the seed of **one effect packet per card** through `VfxSpawn` (the per-tick effect budget, range 96). Option
  `deck.summonEffect` in `nusmp-deck-summon-client.toml` (client, default true). Clock and geometry are pure (`vfx/deck/DeckSummonTimeline`, `DeckSummonGeometry`, `DeckSummonTimelineTest`),
  vertex budget 240 per summon. Self-test `visual.deck_summon`.

## What could NOT be verified

* Nothing was run in a game: how the disk sits on the arm in motion (swing, sneak, attack animations, first person, slim arms), whether the blades clip the other arm or the sleeve of a mod armour, the
  look of the glow and nebula passes, the real size of the summon card, the timing feel. The previews are a software rasteriser.
* The disk is a fixed model on the arm (an `arm` piece): it does not retract. "Retractable arm" is represented by the blade fan breathing (`swing`), not by an animation.
* The summon is placed from the player's body yaw and a fixed arm pose; while the arm swings the effect stays where the arm rests.
* Two deck relics worn together draw their disks on the same place.
* Gradle was not available: Java was type-checked with the session's stub setup and the JUnit tests ran in the shim (21 new tests pass); the full build and the CI self-tests (`visual.duel_disk`,
  `visual.deck_summon`) have not run.

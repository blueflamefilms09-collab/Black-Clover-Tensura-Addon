Sandalphon, Lord of Judgment
Information
Type: Ultimate Skill
Modes: 10

Passive
[Passive - Toggle]

[Passive - Toggle]

[Active - Crouch + Press]
Condemnation:

[Active - Press]
Supreme Edict:
Cancels all active magical effects, buffs, and domains within a 30-block radius.
Costs 5.5K MP, 4.0K on Mastery.

[Active - Crouch + Press]

A devastating, short-range melee sweep that deals true damage and applies Future Rejection to anyone hit.
Costs 3.8K MP, 2.5K on Mastery.

[Active - Press]
Verdict of Ruin:
Spawns a massive AoE field of true damage that follows the target. 
Enemies inside are silenced and cannot use spatial abilities.
Costs 8.0K MP, 5.5K on Mastery.

[Mastery, Active - Held]
Final Judgment:
Locks onto a single target, freezing them in time. Calls down a world-ending cross of light that detonates all Temporal Debt at a 5x multiplier. 
Costs 45K MP. Cooldown 120s.

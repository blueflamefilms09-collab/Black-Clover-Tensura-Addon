# Armour policy (1.99.0): casting never equips; only explicit armour pages and canon forms do

Owner request: "The recently remade Black Clover magics don't match the anime. Casting a spell automatically equips an armour, and too many magics have armour.
(a) Adjust the magics so NOT ALL of them grant or require armour (true to Black Clover lore). (b) For the magics that DO use armour, decouple equipping the armour from
casting: casting a spell must NOT instantly auto-equip the armour."

Code: `book/ArmourPolicy` (pure, data driven), `book/ArmourRules` (pure rules), `book/ArmourState` (server state), `book/ArmourCast` (the one meeting point with a page cast),
`book/SpiritDiveForm` (Spirit Dive), `book/DevilFormLimit` (Devil Union), data `src/main/resources/data/nusmp/armour_policy.json` (generated by `tools/gen_armour_policy.py`
from the table below), self-test `magic.armour_policy`, tests `ArmourPolicyTest`. Lang fragment `tools/lang/armour_policy.json`.

## 1. What changed, in one paragraph

In the 1.93.0 rebuild every page of 41 magics put a temporary armour (the render layer `aura.Aura`, drawn by `client/aura/<Name>Aura` through `EquipAnimation`, forged in like
the Djinn full equip) on the caster for 5 seconds, through helpers such as `WaterArmour.wrap`, `SpatialArmourCast.armoured`, `SongRebuildKit.armoured`, `TreeKit.armour`,
`ShakudoKit.guard` and direct `PlayerAuras.set` calls (37 direct `set` / `clear` calls and 75 wrapped page casts in the kits, 53 changed files in all). Now **no cast puts an armour on**. Every magic is classified in one data file: `NONE` (no armour
exists: 94 magics), `TOGGLE` (5), `FORM` (6). The magics that have an armour get it only from their own explicit page (or from their canon form page), through one server
state (`ArmourState`): the armour forges in with `EquipAnimation`, stays until the same page is cast again, the mana runs out, the wearer is knocked out or dies, gives the
stats of the policy table while it is on, and shatters out with the same animation. 11 of 105 magics (10.5 percent; the test caps it at 40 percent and the aim was a third at most)
have an armour at all.

## 2. How a cast meets the armour (the rule that cannot be bypassed by accident)

`GrimoireBook.castPage` has two lines:

1. before the cooldown check: `ArmourCast.beforeCast(...)`: if the page is an **armour page** and that exact armour is worn, the cast only takes it off (free, no mana, no
   cooldown, no spell);
2. after a successful cast: `ArmourCast.afterCast(...)`: asks the pure `ArmourPolicy.forCast(magic, pageId)`. The answer is `NONE` for every page that is not an armour or form
   page, and for every page of a `NONE` magic. Only `ARMOUR` makes `ArmourState.putOn`. So casting a bolt, a zone, a summon or even another magic's page **never equips and never
   resets the timer** of an armour that is already on. (A copied page, `Copy Magic`, calls the page's cast directly and can never equip.)

The old helpers keep their names and signatures (the page lists and the spells that call them are unchanged) but do nothing: `WaterArmour.wrap` returns the page, `WaterArmour.put`,
`SpatialArmourCast.on`, `SongRebuildKit.armour`, `EarthKit.flash` and the `shortArmour` helpers of Mercury, Recombination, Modification and Transmutation are empty, `TreeKit.armour`
and `ShakudoKit.guard` keep the passive and the resistance but no longer grow the render layer. The `WaterArmour.wrap(...)` and `SongRebuildKit.armoured(...)` wrappers were taken out
of the page lists of Water, Dark, Lightning, Flame, Song, Sound, Soul, Soul Corpse and Memory (the pages are the same pages). The remaining `SpatialArmourCast.armoured(...)` /
local `armoured(...)` wrappers of Spatial, Gravity, Mirror, Permeation and Slash return the cast unchanged.

## 3. The state (`ArmourState`), server authoritative

* **Where.** The player's persistent data, key `nusmp_armour` {magic, style, form flag, layer, stage, since}. One armour per player: putting another on takes the first off.
* **Sync.** The look is the existing `Aura` slot of the magic, driven by `PlayerAuras`: a worn armour has no end, so the server keeps a **lease**: it tells the clients "200 more
  ticks" every 100 ticks (the client keeps the start of a refreshed armour of the same style, so nothing is forged twice) and, when the armour ends, "20 more ticks", which is inside
  the 18 tick shatter window of `EquipAnimation`, so the pieces shatter out exactly as they forged in. Viewers that come into range get it from `PlayerAuras.onStartTracking`.
* **Events.** Login: a toggled armour is forged in again for the player and everyone who sees them (a form is dropped: it does not outlive the session). Respawn: cleared (the death
  hook already cleared it and persistent data is not copied to the new body). Dimension change: re-sent to the clients. Logout: the transient attribute modifiers are removed.
* **Ends** (`ArmourRules.drop`): toggled off (free); the mana of a second's upkeep cannot be paid (`Your mana gives out`); the wearer is held in stopped time (knocked out:
  `TimeStop.isFrozen`); the wearer dies (at once, no animation); the wearer no longer owns the magic's book.
* **Stats while on** (`ArmourPolicy.Bonus`, numbers in the table of section 6): a share of every blow cut (never above 60 percent), immunity to the magic's own element (Flame: fire,
  Lightning: lightning), attack and speed multipliers and knockback resistance as transient attribute modifiers (removed when it ends), hidden night vision for forms. Half of the
  attack bonus also strengthens the spells of the armour's own magic (`ArmourState.spellBoost`, used by `GrimoireBook.hurtAs`): an ordinary cast may use the armour's boost, it
  never puts the armour on.
* **Upkeep.** A toggled armour costs 0.4 to 0.5 percent of the magicule pool a second (`armour.upkeepScale` in the config scales it; 0 = free); the Silver Guardian also costs half
  a hit point a second (the mercury is poison), never below six hit points and never in creative. **Nothing touches food or hunger** (a test greps the new classes for it).

## 4. Spirit Dive: the FORM of Fire, Water, Wind and Earth

Canon (the owner's text): an advanced Spirit Magic spell; the mage merges with their elemental spirit; a big boost to magic power, speed, physical attributes and senses; the user passively
absorbs ambient natural mana to fuel spells and the internal reserve drains slower; the body is cloaked in dense elemental mana. Yuno (Sylph, wind): the first dive transforms only the
left half (one wing, half a crown, a ring round the wrist), the full dive has two wings and a full crown. Noelle (Undine, water): the Saint Valkyrie Dress, her usual water armour plus
feathered wing-like parts and a halo. Fuegoleon (Salamander, fire): arms cloaked in fire mana with draconic scales, claws and fiery wings. The earth spirit exists but is never shown
(generic).

The page already existed (`spirit_dive`, universal, Spirit Lord gate and the spirit's trust): it is the explicit form page. 1.99.0 changes what the dive is, not who may enter it:

| Part | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Duration | fixed 8 seconds | held while the upkeep is paid: 2 percent of the pool a second (as before), **minus** the spirit's ambient intake (1.2 percent a second, `armour.diveAbsorbScale`), so a full pool lasts about two minutes; the dive is renewed every second it holds (`SpiritDiveForm.second`) |
| Spirit lock | 60 s cooldown started with the dive (so it could not be left early) | 3 s after the start (so a recast can end it), the 60 s lock starts when the dive ends (recast or the upkeep failing) |
| Stats | spells 20 percent bigger | the same, plus the form bonus: speed, attack, a share of blows cut, knockback resistance, hidden night vision (senses); the first three dives of an unmastered book are **half dives** at 60 percent strength |
| Look | Fire: Salamander overlay, Wind: Sylph overlay (`ModeArmor`, 160 ticks); Water: none; Earth: none | the overlays are renewed with the dive; Water now shows the Valkyrie overlay (Noelle's plates and avian water wings); Earth shows the Gnome Plate through the armour layer |
| Armour already on | n/a | any other armour gives way to the dive (the pieces shatter out), the dive's look takes over |
| Ends | the 8 seconds | recast, the upkeep failing, death, knock-out; the overlay and the stats go with it |

## 5. Devil Union (Anti-Magic) and Devil Unite (Shadow): FORM, canon behind the policy

The mod already has both forms and they are left as they were, with their own overlays and gauges (`blackclover.ModeArmor`, `shadow.UniteState`, `book.AntiMagicBook` Black Form with the
Aura gauge of `antimagic.aura`). Canon (the owner's text): Asta and Liebe: no mana; Anti-Magic coats the body and erases, cuts or repels any magic it touches; horns, tail, slit pupils,
pitch-black jagged armour. It needs a Devil-Binding Ritual contract; Asta's is an **Equal Contract**: the human and the devil must synchronise thoughts, pain and motivation or the union
fails. A strict time limit of five minutes, then the form shatters and the user is incapacitated by intense muscle pain for about thirty minutes before fighting again.

Built now (`book/DevilFormLimit`, hooked into `AntiMagicBook.setForm` / `upkeepForm` / `blackForm`): the Black Form of a Lord ends at the limit (`armour.devilFormLimitSeconds`,
default 300 s, minimum 20 s), the form switches off with its overlay, and the price is paid: **slowness III, weakness II and mining fatigue III for `armour.devilFormAftermathSeconds`
(default 1800 s)**, and the next union is refused with a message until the aftermath ends. Ending the form on purpose, or the Aura running dry (as before), costs nothing extra. No hunger
effect of any kind. The stored times are two numbers in the persistent data (cleared by death).

**Not built (follow-up):** the Devil-Binding Ritual itself. Canon: it needs a relic tied to the devil and a summoning circle that pulls the devil out of the underworld while suppressing
it; if the relic is destroyed the spell ends; then a duel to the defeat (a loss: the host is taken over and warped into a mindless monster; a win: a collar binds the devil and the host
declares the terms): **Master-Servant** (subjugation, easy to unite and to dominate) or **Equal Contract** (matching marks on both wrists, the union is very hard and needs perfect
sync). The code only has the five-leaf cover / devil state gating (`GrimoireBook.devilUnion`, `ForbiddenMagic`); nothing like a ritual, relic, duel, collar or contract kind exists, and
this task did not build one.

## 8. What could not be verified

* Nothing was run in a game. Gradle is not available offline: the changed files were type-checked against the API stubs (0 real errors, the negative control fails as it should) and the
  pure rules run as JUnit in the shim (`ArmourPolicyTest`: 19 tests). `MagicEnumAuditTest` and the other tests that need game classes cannot run in the shim.
* The self-test `magic.armour_policy` is registered for the CI server smoke test; it was not run (it needs the real registries). It uses a connection-less fake player, so the clients' side
  of the sync (the payload, the lease, the shatter window) is checked by reading `PlayerAuraClient.receive` and `EquipAnimation`, not by seeing it.
* The wiki host was unreachable; canon is from memory and from the repository's copy of the wiki text.
* The balance numbers (bonuses, 0.4 percent upkeep, 1.2 percent intake) are design values, untested in play. They are data: change the file, or the three config scales.
* The look of a toggled armour that stays for minutes (the lease keeps the same painter running; painters were written for 5 to 30 seconds) is unseen; none of them reads the total length.
* The mod-bus ordering of the new login / respawn / dimension handlers against the other session handlers was reasoned, not observed; the armour re-sends itself every 100 ticks anyway, so
  a missed packet heals within five seconds.

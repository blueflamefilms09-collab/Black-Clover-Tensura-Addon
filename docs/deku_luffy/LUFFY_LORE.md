### Base look (for the model)
- Slim but well-muscled; shaggy black hair with a small widow's peak; round black eyes; stitched
  scar under his left eye; large X-shaped scar across the chest (from Akainu at Marineford).
- Signature straw hat: yellow straw, red band, string strap so it can hang on his back.
- Classic post-timeskip outfit: open red short-sleeve (3/4) cardigan with four gold buttons, blue
  denim shorts, yellow sash at the waist, sandals.
- Wano-exit / Egghead fighting outfit: yellow shorts, purple sash, sandals (he shed the Egghead
  red coat to fight Kizaru).
- Elbaph outfit (latest): shirtless; long red fur cape; dark brown leather shorts under a three-
  strip light-brown leather skirt (yellow dots on the front flap); wide yellow leather belt with a
  silver buckle and black feather trim; long brown fur-lined boots; huge black-and-gold Viking
  helmet with long curved horns; battle axe on his back on an orange strap; straw hat hanging behind.

### Gear 5 look (the boss skin)
- Hair, shirt and shorts turn pure WHITE. Hair becomes a billowing, flame-like mane.
- Sash / belt turns PURPLE.
- Eyes: large red irises with white ring-shaped pupils, often drawn glowing bright red (pink in anime).
- Eyebrows become curled spirals / billowing.
- A collar of white CLOUDS drapes around his neck, over his shoulders and under his arms (the Gear 4
  "steam scarf" replaced by clouds).
- Gear 5 tends to swap whatever he is wearing back to his own shorts + sandals (like a Zoan
  transformation shedding clothes); the outfit returns when he powers down.
- Headwear merges into the hair: wearing the Elbaph Viking helmet, his Gear 5 hair grows HORNS.
  (Good option for an "Elbaph Gear 5" variant: white flame hair with two horns.)
- Huge, constant grin and laughter. Cartoon eyes pop out, jaw drops, etc.
- Wanted poster #7 uses the Gear 5 image.
- Latest state (Ch. 1198, vs Imu): "monkey form" - the Zoan's animal instinct takes over: pure
  BLACK body, sharp fangs, Gomu-fruit swirls on shoulders and upper back, white cloud rings round
  the wrists, monkey-like behaviour and a monkey laugh, a different heartbeat. (Possible phase-3.)

## 2. The Devil Fruit

### 2a. Base rubber body (always on, even under seastone/water)
- Whole body (organs too) is rubber: stretch, bounce, twist, inflate. Max stretch per Oda's first
  SBS: "72 Gomu Gomus"; beyond that he bleeds and risks tearing.
- IMMUNE to lightning/electricity (rubber insulates): Enel's Goro Goro no Mi, Mink Electro, the
  homie Zeus. Can hit Enel without Haki. Survived a lightning strike on Buggy's execution platform.
- Effectively IMMUNE to blunt force not coated in Armament Haki: punches, clubs, being stepped on by
  giants, falls from great heights. Bullets and cannonballs bounce back at the shooter.
- Fusen (balloon belly) boosts bounce-back and keeps him afloat.
- Twisting limbs then releasing (Rifle) or stretching back (Bullet/Pistol) stores spring energy.
- Can pull himself across distances (Rocket) and pseudo-move in mid-air by grabbing things.
- Poison resistance (Magellan aftermath; shrugged off Caesar's gas; still hurt by extreme poisons).
- Recovers stamina and wounds by eating huge amounts of food and sleeping.

### 2b. Awakening = Gear 5 (Hito Hito no Mi, Model: Nika)
- First awakened in centuries (previous user: Joy Boy, ~900 years ago). The Five Elders call it
  "the most ridiculous power in the world"; Imu fears it.
- Theme: FREEDOM. Luffy can fight any way he can imagine. Road To Laugh Tale Vol. 3 says the true
  power is turning imagination into reality, limited only by his imagination.
- Drums of Liberation: in Gear 5 his heartbeat plays a musical rhythm, "dondottotto" (Zunesha named
  it). He activates the form by forcing his heart into that rhythm. -> Boss music / heartbeat SFX.
- Laughter: he laughs constantly, even in dead-serious moments, feels light and creative, and his
  joy is contagious - enemies start to feel the fun of the fight.
- Zoan perks: big boosts to strength, speed, durability; fast involuntary recovery (healed while
  unconscious after Kaidou "killed" him).
- Can inflate limbs (Gear 3 style) and muscles (Gear 4 style, anime "Muscle-Muscle Power") instantly,
  without blowing air in. Can inflate his WHOLE body to giant size (Gomu Gomu no Gigant).
- Can use Gear 4 tricks inside Gear 5 (Boundman limb compression, Tankman counter-bounce).
- Cartoon physics (rubber-hose / Tom and Jerry inspired):
  - Head wraps around Kaidou's spiked kanabo; body gets squashed flat and pops back.
  - Charred black by Bolo Breath but basically fine.
  - Swallowed one of Kizaru's lasers and let it flash harmlessly through his body.
  - Hit by Warcury's Supreme-King-infused roar: his clothes, eyes, cloud collar and scars were
    literally blown off his body, then he stuck them back on.
  - Eyes pop out of his head in shock (and other characters' too).
  - Spins legs so fast he RUNS ON AIR, leaving fire from friction; spins like a top and DRILLS
    into the ground; can FLOAT in mid-air (but needs to run on air or grab things to travel far).
  - Props: shapes his hair into goggles mid-move, chews a tree into a baseball bat and adds a
    painted bat + baseball helmet for show. Props are cosmetic and vanish in seconds.
- Environment rubberization (Paramecia-like awakening, Kaidou noted it has traits of both types):
  - Turns floors, walls, roofs into rubber: cushion falls, trampoline off them, stretch them to
    bounce attacks back (sent Kaidou's Bolo Breath back off a rubberized piece of the Skull Dome
    roof that did not even melt).
  - Works on intangibles: lightning (grabbed and threw it), air, clouds.
  - Works on living flesh: opponents stretch around his fists (but still take the full force);
    flattened Kizaru and Saturn into discs; inflated the MMA Zaza until she burst like a balloon.
  - All changes are temporary: terrain and people snap back to their original shape.

### 2c. Gear 5 drawbacks
- Huge stamina drain due to inexperience. On powering down he can look aged by decades (wrinkled,
  skinny, white-faced; anime mutes his colours) and is exhausted. It wears off with time.
- Eating a massive amount of food restores him almost immediately (Egghead: re-entered Gear 5 right
  after stuffing himself). He can also push through on willpower, at the price of worse collapse.
- Zoan awakening risk: the animal nature (the Sun God Nika) can swallow his mind - this seems to be
  what happens in the Ch. 1198 black monkey form.
- Still vulnerable to cutting attacks; big Haki-backed hits still hurt through the squash.

### 2d. General Devil Fruit / rubber weaknesses
- Cannot swim. Sea water and SEASTONE drain his strength and switch off the power (he stays rubbery
  in shape, but loses the blunt immunity; others can stretch him).
- Blades and slashes cut him normally, even when he is coated in Haki.
- Stretched limbs take a moment to snap back (an opening, exploited by Buggy and Kuro).
- Armament Haki bypasses the blunt immunity (it does not turn him back to flesh). Shockwave styles
  (Fish-Man Karate, Rokuogan) also bypass it.
- Yami Yami no Mi contact (Blackbeard) nullifies the fruit completely.
- Armament-hardened body parts stop being rubber (this is why a Haki-coated Thor Elephant Gun can
  carry electricity). Gear 4 gets around this by fusing rubber and Haki ("iron balloon").
- Gag: Garp's "Fist of Love" and the crew's angry punches hurt him anyway.

## 3. Haki

### 3a. Armament Haki (Busoshoku; Wano name "Ryuo" = "Flowing Cherry Blossom")
- Learned from Rayleigh in the timeskip. Lets him hit Logia users (Caesar) and Katakuri.
- Hardening (Koka): limbs turn glossy BLACK. Luffy's coating has a CRIMSON/red tint, strongest in
  Gear 4. Very dense coating gets flame-shaped edges (Luffy's Gear 4 "tattoos").
  - Atama Buso ("Head Armament"): hardened forehead (shattered Rebecca's sword, Gomu Gomu no Kane).
  - Gear 2 Buso ("Gear 2 Armament"): hardened fist in Gear 2 that ignites -> Red Hawk.
- Emission (advanced): pushes Haki out of the fist to hit without contact - a short invisible burst
  with the force of a huge punch. Learned under Hyougoro in Udon after hardened hits did nothing to
  Kaidou. Practised by punching steel blocks; blasted a hole out the far side of a tree.
- Internal Destruction (higher grade): Haki flows INTO the target and destroys it from inside
  (first used to rip Queen's explosive collars off; later Yamato's cuffs; a tree burst from inside
  when he only held his fist toward it). Only Rayleigh and Luffy shown using it.
- With advanced Armament his hardened Gear 3 (Red Roc) finally hurt Kaidou, and he could block
  Kaidou's kanabo in base form (arms hurt afterwards).
- Depletion: heavy coating drains it; Gear 4 used to empty it completely (10-minute Haki lockout).
- VFX: anime shows a red aura at full power; in Wano, advanced Armament gives Luffy an ORANGE-and-RED
  flowing aura with cherry-blossom-shaped particles and glowing vein-like lines spreading over him.
  Combined with advanced Supreme King: a GOLDEN aura round his whole body.

### 3b. Observation Haki (Kenbunshoku)
- Learned in the timeskip (blindfold training with Rayleigh). Senses presence, emotion, intent;
  dodges bullets, Pacifista lasers, Hody's water-bullet barrage, thrown-weapon volleys.
  Rayleigh says he is especially attuned to sensing living things' emotions.
- Future Vision (advanced): unlocked mid-fight with Katakuri after 9+ hours; sees a precise image
  of the next few seconds (and hears what will be said). By Udon he uses it as fluidly as Katakuri
  (coached Hyougoro through dodges seconds ahead; stopped Babanuki's Excite Shot because he "saw a
  terrible future"). Only Katakuri, Luffy, Kaidou, Shanks and Scopper Gaban shown with it.
- Used offensively: Snakeman's semi-homing punches and Hydra's 360-degree barrage rely on it.
- Limits: needs focus; can't help if he is restrained or the attack is simply too fast; doesn't
  tell him HOW to dodge unknown mechanics (Apoo's sound attacks hit him).
- VFX: little visible in manga; anime shows eyes/pupils glowing RED when he senses or foresees.

### 3c. Supreme King / Conqueror's Haki (Haoshoku) - his speciality
- Latent until Amazon Lily / Impel Down / Marineford; Rayleigh gave him control.
- Pressure + Incapacitation: knocks out weak-willed people instantly, selectively (spared civilians
  around the Fake Straw Hats); knocked out 50,000 New Fish-Man Pirates at once; used it while
  UNCONSCIOUS on Beasts Pirates who tried to carry him off; cowes animals (Kraken obeyed him).
- Supreme King Clash: when two users clash, crackling BLACK LIGHTNING (thin black-and-red streaks,
  visible across the island) plus shockwaves that blow people back. Luffy has clashed with Chinjao,
  Doflamingo, Katakuri, Kaidou.
- Splitting the Heavens: his clash with Kaidou split the clouds/sky in two - previously only seen
  between Emperors or with Roger.
- Infusion ("coating"/"wearing" it, advanced): discovered mid-fight with Kaidou. He clads arms,
  legs and body in Conqueror's like Armament; black lightning crackles off the limbs. First crude
  and clumsy (Kaidou said so), then mastered. This is what made his blows deal real damage to Kaidou
  and let him kick the kanabo aside.
- Elbaph: Scopper Gaban taught him that infused Supreme King Haki suppresses the Knights of God's
  immortality; he then blew Shepherd Sommers apart in one attack and cancelled his fruit.
  Supreme King Haki generally negates Imu's Akuma no Mi applications.
- VFX: black-and-red lightning arcs; ripple/shockwave ring; anime: dark-blue tint over the affected
  area, a dark purple aura (Ep. 723), Luffy's expanding wave coloured RED (Stampede), Wano-era gold
  sparkle effect.

## 4. Gears and key techniques

Naming rules worth reproducing:
- Gear 2 = "Jet"; Gear 2 + Armament = "Hawk" / "Eagle" / "Red Hawk".
- Gear 3 = "Gigant"; Gear 3 + Armament = "Elephant" / "Grizzly"; + Supreme King = "Roc".
- Gear 4 = big mammals + guns ("Kong", "Rhino", "Leo"); Python-redirected = snakes.
- Gear 5 + Haki = "Dawn" (written with the kanji for "White").

### Base (no Gear) staples, for reference
Gomu Gomu no Pistol (stretch punch), Bullet (close stretch-back punch), Rifle (twisted spinning
punch), Bazooka (double palm), Gatling (barrage), Whip / Muchi (sweeping kick), Stamp (stretched
sole kick), Ono / Axe (overhead heel drop), Rocket (grab-and-slingshot), Fusen (balloon belly).

### 4a. Gear 2 ("Gear Second")
- What: pumps blood at high pressure (rubber vessels survive), flooding body with oxygen and
  nutrients - like doping. Huge speed and power, beats Soru users and smashes Tekkai.
- Look: body steams (sweat vaporising); anime gives pinkish, shiny skin. Pre-timeskip stance:
  crouch with one hand on the ground pumping the legs. Post-timeskip: instant activation in any
  single limb, used in short bursts.
- Drawbacks: burns food/energy fast, heavy panting, paralysis after long use; Lucci warned it
  shortens his lifespan. Much reduced after the timeskip.
- Key techniques:
  - Soru (Shave): blink-speed movement, better than Blueno's.
  - Gomu Gomu no Jet Pistol (Jet Pistol/Gun): invisible-speed straight punch, other palm aimed at
    the target; hits like a hand cannon. With Haki it one-shots a Pacifista.
  - Gomu Gomu no Jet Gatling (Jet Gun Battering): invisible barrage. A Gear 5 version exists too.
  - Gomu Gomu no Jet Bazooka, Jet Whip, Jet Stamp, Jet Bullet, Twin Jet Pistol, Jet Rocket,
    Jet Hammer, Jet Mace, Jet Spear.
  - Gomu Gomu no Red Hawk (Fire Pistol / Fire Fist Gun): Gear 2 + hardened arm stretched back like
    Bullet; the arm IGNITES and the punch lands as a fiery explosion (works even underwater). Nod to
    Ace's Hiken. Strong enough by Wano that Kaidou chose to dodge it.
  - Gomu Gomu no Hawk Gatling (Hawk Gun Battering): hardened Jet Gatling. Also Hawk Rifle, Hawk
    Whip, Hawk Stamp, Eagle Bazooka (hardened double palm).
  - Combos with Gear 3: Gomu Gomu no Gigant Jet Shell (Giant Jet Shell; inflated body rams like a
    cannonball), Red Roc, Roc Gatling (see Gear 3).

### 4b. Gear 3 ("Gear Third")
- What: bites thumb and blows air into his BONES (Hone Fusen, "Bone Balloon"), inflating a limb to
  giant size - hard as a car tyre, a giant mace.
- Pre-timeskip: whole limb balloons, slow; afterwards he SHRANK to a chibi child form for as long as
  he had been inflated.
- Post-timeskip look: only the FIST becomes giant with a big round air-sphere at the wrist
  ("elephantine"), arm stays thin so he can stretch it back; almost always hardened BLACK with
  Armament (shiny). No more shrinking; size is controllable; can be used as a hardened shield.
  After Udon he inflates instantly without blowing.
- Key techniques:
  - Gomu Gomu no Gigant Pistol (Giant's Pistol/Gun), Gigant Axe, Gigant Whip, Gigant Bazooka,
    Gigant Rifle, Gigant Stamp, Gigant Fusen, Gigant Tsuppari (Giant's Thrust).
  - Gomu Gomu no Elephant Gun (Elephant Gun): giant black hardened fist on a stretched arm; one of
    his most-used post-timeskip moves; a visually identical version exists in Gear 5.
  - Gomu Gomu no Elephant Gatling (Elephant Gun Battering): barrage of giant hardened fists (used
    on the ark Noah).
  - Gomu Gomu no Grizzly Magnum (Grizzly/Ash Bear Gun): both arms inflated, runs, leaps, hardens
    mid-air and fires both - Elephant Gun + Bazooka.
  - Gomu Gomu no Thor Elephant Gun (Thunder General Elephant Gun): Elephant Gun wreathed in
    electric sparks (Chinjao).
  - Gomu Gomu no Gigant Thor Axe (Giant Thunder Axe): giant leg raised into a thundercloud, brought
    down charged with lightning.
  - Gomu Gomu no Red Roc (Inferno Pistol/Fist Gun): Gear 3 fist + Gear 2 + advanced Armament,
    ignited into a huge FLAMING black fist. First thing that knocked down, burned and injured Kaidou.
  - Gomu Gomu no Roc Gun (Monster Bird Gun): Elephant Gun hardened + Supreme King infused, arm
    stretched far behind while running then snapped forward.
  - Gomu Gomu no Roc Gatling (Monster Bird Gun Battering): Elephant Gatling + Supreme King +
    Gear 2; matched Kaidou's Human-Beast Form attacks.

### 4c. Gear 4 ("Gear Fourth")
- What: coats arm in Armament, bites forearm and blows air into his MUSCLES (Kin'niku Fusen,
  "Muscle Balloon"), spreading it over the upper body. Rubber + Haki fused = "iron balloon".
  Focuses on COMPRESSION (retracting fists into forearms like springs) rather than stretching.
  Python lets him bend punches mid-flight (Kaidou says rubber shouldn't be able to do that).
- Common look: grows to ~4 m; black Armament coating on arms, legs and much of the torso with
  flame-shaped tattoo-like edges and CRIMSON accents; dark rings/shading around the eyes; brown
  irises; a scarf of steam over the shoulders (like a Nio guardian statue's ribbon - Gear 4 is
  based on the Nio temple guardians). Anime: rougher, deeper voice, kabuki head-rolls.
- Drawbacks: time limit, then it auto-cancels leaving him barely able to move; 10 minutes with NO
  Haki; heavy metabolism burn (burned off a bellyful of biscuits in minutes); a second use in a row
  once knocked him out for 3 days; prone to falling asleep. Much reduced later: after Udon he
  enters it instantly (no blowing), switches between forms, can cancel manually and keep Haki
  (Egghead), and by Elbaph uses "Mini Gear 4" on a single limb.
- Kin'niku Fusen (Muscle Balloon): the activation; Doflamingo's Haki kick bounced off.
- Python (Great Snake/Serpent): bend/redirect a punch mid-strike; semi-homing.

Boundman (Bound-Man / "bouncing man") - balanced form
- Look: spherical inflated torso, huge arms matching the torso, Haki swirls on the upper chest like
  the fruit's pattern, hair standing on end, black eye lining, brown irises, taller and more
  muscular. Steam scarf (purple hue on Vol. 79 cover; red/orange on Ch. 821 cover).
- Constantly bounces in place; flies by kicking off the air (Geppo-like) at Soru-like speed; lands
  as an uncontrolled bouncing ball. Most hits bounce off; can even resist cuts head-on (but strong
  Haki blades cut him, and Kaidou's kanabo one-shot him in their first fight).
- Techniques:
  - Gomu Gomu no Kong Gun (Monkey King Gun): fist compressed into the cannon-like forearm, flies in
    and "fires" it. Sent Doflamingo from the palace into the city; shattered Cracker's shields.
  - Gomu Gomu no King Kong Gun (Great Monkey King Gun): Kong Gun plus air pumped into the arm so it
    becomes GIANT before firing. Broke Doflamingo's Kumo no Sugaki and God Thread at once and
    flattened a town, folding the ground in on itself. Back Haki patterns thicken across the whole
    back. Wano version: no inflation, Haki projected from the fist (shattered a steel block).
  - Gomu Gomu no Over Kong Gun (Supreme Monkey King Gun): King Kong Gun infused with advanced
    Supreme King Haki, no air-blowing needed (vs Kaidou).
  - Gomu Gomu no Rhino Schneider (Rhinoceros Howitzer): legs compressed into the body, flying
    dropkick that sends the target through dozens of buildings.
  - Gomu Gomu no Culverin (Great Serpent Cannon): straight stretch punch that keeps momentum and,
    with Python, chases the target if it misses. Double Culverin = both arms.
  - Gomu Gomu no Leo Bazooka (Lion Bazooka): both fists compressed, double open-palm spring blast
    in a Kamehameha-like stance; sent Doflamingo into a mountain leaving a crater.
  - Gomu Gomu no Kong Organ (Monkey King Murder of Crows Cannon): wide barrage of Kong Guns with
    afterimages of six arms.
  - Gomu Gomu no Kong Rifle (Monkey King Rotating Bullet): twisted compressed punch.
  - Gomu Gomu no Kong Gatling (Monkey King Gun Battering): tighter, more focused Kong Organ.

Tankman (Tank-Man) - defence/power form; only seen as "Manpuku Version" ("Full Stomach Version")
- Look: sumo-like; enormous perfectly round Haki-coated belly (still muscular up top), arms and legs
  only slightly inflated, flame-like Haki over lower torso, shoulders, arms; big height gain.
- Cracker's sword could not pierce the belly. The belly is a launcher.
- Gomu Gomu no Cannonball: sucks the enemy into his compressed stomach and blasts them away (beat
  Cracker through his Biscuit Soldiers).

Snakeman (Snake-Man) - speed form
- Look: barely inflated; Haki only on forearms/legs, ending at the biceps; long flame-shaped
  upswept hair; scarf of steam mixed with FIRE (orange hue, Vol. 89 cover); knuckles extended
  like a leopard fist.
- Punches keep ACCELERATING the longer they travel; semi-homing via Python + Observation Haki.
  Worse defence (takes blunt hits normally). Good jumper, poor flyer (switched to Boundman to fly).
- Techniques:
  - Gomu Gomu no Jet Culverin (Jet Great Serpent Cannon): ever-accelerating homing punch.
  - Gomu Gomu no Black Mamba (Black Snake Swarm): homing barrage with afterimages.
  - Gomu Gomu no King Cobra (King Snake): arm loops around the arena building speed, then the fist
    enlarges and slams in. Finished Katakuri. Anime: pink and blue steam snakes merge into a golden
    king cobra round the arm.
  - Gomu Gomu no Hydra (Nine-Headed Snake): Supreme King infused, advanced-Armament (contactless)
    barrage guided by advanced Observation, from every direction including behind - a 360-degree
    assault (vs Kaidou).

Mini Gear 4 (Elbaph): only one limb in Gear 4 so he can stretch-and-launch again; arm-only Kong Gun
destroyed a large wall in the Land of Gods.

### 4d. Gear 5 ("Gear Fifth") - the peak
(Abilities and look: see section 2b. Haki-boosted moves get "Dawn".)
- Gomu Gomu no Fusen (Balloon): body becomes helium-light and floats (used inside Kaidou).
- Gomu Gomu no Gigant (Giant): whole body inflates to an actual GIANT's size.
- Gomu Gomu no Nawatobi (Skipping Rope): as a giant, uses Kaidou's dragon body as a jump rope.
- Gomu Gomu no Dasshutsu Rocket (Escape Rocket): from inside Kaidou, stretches arms out of his eyes,
  grabs his nose and slingshots out.
- Gomu Gomu no Kaminari (Lightning): rubberizes a lightning bolt, GRABS it and throws it (anime:
  several bolts at once). Signature boss move.
- Gomu Gomu no Bajrang Gun (Monkey God Gun): Gear 5 Over Kong Gun; fist inflated to nearly the size
  of ONIGASHIMA, advanced Armament + Supreme King infused, Haki emitted so it hits without contact.
  His most powerful attack; defeated Kaidou. ("Bajrang" = Hanuman.)
- Gomu Gomu no Star Gun (White Star Gun): inflated muscles, arm with Armament + Supreme King, spins in
  place then a giant stretched hook to the head; cartoon STARS burst out on impact. Vs Kizaru it was
  wreathed in flames (anime: multicolour lights) and left the Admiral immobile.
- Gomu Gomu no Mogura Pistol (Mole Gun): punches the floor; a nearby piece of floor stretches into a
  tendril and hits the enemy from the side. (Great "ground eruption" boss attack.)
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Whip (White Whip): twists body, hardens and stretches a leg, kicks the face and
  lets the body untwist for extra force.
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Rocket (White Rocket): throws himself into the scenery, bends it like a
  slingshot, makes GOGGLES from his hair, launches with a whip-spinning fist into the gut; the fist
  stretches cartoonishly through the enemy and sends them flying. Finished Lucci.
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Stamp (White Stamp): lines enemies up and hits them all with one hardened
  stretched leg (vs Kizaru's light clones).
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Gatling (White Gun Battering): Elephant Gatling with no wind-up lag - fists grow
  and harden mid-barrage; also usable at normal fist size (vs Imu).
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Cymbal (White Vise): giant hands clap the enemy flat into a disk, then fling it
  (Kizaru and Saturn).
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Balloon (White Balloon): inflated, hardened body pulls the enemy into its belly;
  allies hit his back, compressing him, and the force launches the enemy far away (vs Mars).
- Gomu Gomu no Dawn Pistol (White Gun): stretched punch with Armament + Supreme King (vs
  Hilmungardr, Elbaph).
- Gomu Gomu no Hara Senbei (Tummy Cracker): inflates and spreads over the ground as a giant cushion
  (caught falling giant children).
- Gomu Gomu no Jet Gatling (Jet Gun Battering): arms so fast only jet trails show (vs Imu).
- Gomu Gomu no Majin (Genie): becomes a genie - huge upper body, legs fading into a smoke trail,
  long ponytail, beard, big nose, pointed ears with earrings, shirtless, turban with a meat-shaped
  jewel, metal wrist cuffs; hovers. Built from the smoke of Imu's explosion; Luffy emerged unhurt

## 6. Weaknesses and limits (summary for boss design)

- Sea water / seastone: drains him and switches off the fruit. (A "seastone" or "water" weakness
  in Minecraft: damage or slow when in water, or a special seastone item.)
- Cannot swim.
- Cutting/slashing damage works normally (swords = good counterplay; Kaidou, Katakuri, Cracker,
  Hody all cut him).
- Armament Haki / shockwave attacks bypass the blunt immunity; blunt and lightning resistance only
  applies to unimbued attacks.
- Yami Yami no Mi touch nullifies everything.
- Stretch recovery lag after long attacks (punish window).
- Gear 2: stamina/food burn, panting. Gear 3 (old): chibi shrink. Gear 4: time limit, then 10 min
  no Haki and exhaustion.
- Gear 5: heavy stamina drain; turns old, frail and exhausted after it ends (natural "enrage ->
  collapse" phase); restored by eating huge amounts of food (meat item heal mechanic); risk of the
  Nika animal instinct taking over (berserk black monkey phase).
- Gear 5 changes (rubber terrain, flattened enemies, props) are all temporary.
- Observation can be beaten by unknown mechanics, restraint, or raw speed.
- Hunger in general: he loses power when starving and recovers by eating/sleeping.

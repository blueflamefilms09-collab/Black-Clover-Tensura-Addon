### Final-battle appearance (what the boss should look like)
Base suit is **Costume Zeta**, rebuilt after the Dark Hero arc:
- Dark green full bodysuit (darker in the manga) with **gold outlines** (anime colour scheme green + orange/gold stripes).
- **Green kneepads**; red/black **Iron Soles** (black iron plates with yellow slits/bolts) over his signature
  **red high-top boots** with thick white soles.
- **Air Force Gloves**: white gloves, green palms, yellow sights on the knuckles, black lines on the back of the hand,
  a two-layer wrist band with a blue seam and yellow bolts.
- **Red makeshift Mid-Gauntlets** (from Mei) on the forearms, with a **gold stripe** along the bicep.
- **Gran Torino's cape**, cleaned, worn as a **scarf/cape** (tattered later in the fight, then destroyed by Tomura).
- **No cowl/hood** in the manga (the anime keeps it); the **damaged, chipped metal respirator** from Costume Epsilon
  (diamond centre, 8 holes).
- Utility belt with 4 pouches (one added back at the rear).

Progressive battle damage during the fight vs Tomura (great for boss phases):
1. Respirator and the **right Mid-Gauntlet are destroyed**; right arm becomes wrapped in **black coils** (Blackwhip
   running inside his arm holding torn tendons together, from overusing Full Cowl).
2. **Upper half of the suit is shredded**: shirtless, left arm bare. Cape left in rags, then gone.
3. **Deku Overlay** form: 4 Blackwhip tendrils from the back + 2 from the torso, black claws laced over his fingers,
   Blackwhip snaking over the whole body like dark lines under/over skin, and a jaw-like shape around the mouth.
   Savage, symbiote-like look (Horikoshi's internal name was "Carnage").
4. After transferring all quirks he drops Overlay and looks normal again; in the real world both arms are lost to
   decay, then regrow via Eri's Rewind (her horn).
5. **Costume Origin** (last fight vs All For One): bandages on left wrist and upper right arm, plus a **light blue
   "Men's Nonno" All Might T-shirt** donated by a refugee. Running only on the embers of OFA.

Other looks worth knowing: the earlier **"Dark Deku"/Epsilon** look (filthy torn suit, claw-like gloves, crooked
talon-like iron soles, up to 6 Blackwhips from the back, mask shadowing his face so only green irises glow). After
the war: scar on the top-right of the head, a scar from under the right eye down to the chin, heavily scarred hands,
right side shaved to an undercut. Permanent damage: crooked, scarred right hand, keloid scars on upper right arm,
black compression sleeve on that arm.

## 2. One For All at its peak

### Core quirk
- Transferable stockpile of power built across 9 holders. Transfer needs the recipient to ingest DNA (hair, blood).
  Cannot be stolen by will alone; can be forcibly *given*. The core grew stronger with every holder, and each
  holder's original quirk merged into it, which is why Izuku alone can use the six vestige quirks.
- **Visuals:** the powered body part glows with **red vein-like lines**; Full Cowl adds crackling **light cyan /
  turquoise / green lightning** over the whole body, and the **eyes glow green**. Heavy use can show visions of the
  past holders as **glowing sparks** passing between them; the vestiges (ghost silhouettes of the past users) can
  appear behind him ("One For All across the generations").
- **Vestige realm:** a ruined conference room with one chair per holder. By the final war he talks to the vestiges
  freely while awake; they coach him mid-fight.

### Percentage progression (for scaling phases)

|---|---|

| 8% | After the Provisional License exam (fight 2 vs Katsuki) |

| 20% | Short bursts since Overhaul |

| "Faux 100%" | 45% + Fa Jin + Blackwhip slingshot = 100% speed with no recoil |
| **"120%"** | **[FW] Overdrive: 100% Full Cowl + Gearshift + Fa Jin, no damage to his body. His absolute peak with the full quirk** |
| Embers | After giving OFA to Tomura: residual power that fades with use. Still landed the final smash that killed AFO and cleared the sky |

### Full Cowl variants
- **Full Cowl**: power spread evenly over the whole body instead of one limb. Less damage per hit but no broken
  bones; huge mobility (wall-running, bounding between roofs). Look: red veins + green bio-electric lightning, glowing eyes.
- **Full Cowl - Shoot Style**: Full Cowl concentrated in torso and legs; kick-based so he stops wrecking his arms.
  Iron Soles store and return kick impact. Main fighting style from mid-series onward.
- **Full Cowl - 100%**: whole-body 100%. Look: **hair stands up spiky and glows brighter green, energy streams like
  fire trails from the eyes (yellow in the manga, blue in the anime), lightning turns blue instead of green, skin
  goes reddish, pupils/irises distort.** Can push off the air with wind pressure to move in mid-air.
- **One For All - 20% / 30% / 45%**: named percentage states. 45% is activated right at the moment of impact.
- **Faux 100 Percent**: see Fa Jin.
- **Overdrive** [FW]: see Gearshift. Full Cowl 100% plus Gearshift plus Fa Jin = "120%".

### The six vestige quirks

#### Gearshift (変速, Hensoku). 2nd user: Toshitsugu Kudo  [FW only]
- **Original user:** Kudo, leader of the resistance against AFO. Salmon spiky hair, scar across the face, combat
  jacket with big black collar, arm gauntlets that fired bullets he sped up. AFO mocked it as a "peashooter".
- **What it does:** changes how fast anything he touches accelerates (himself, others, objects, even smoke), ignoring
  inertia. After a century in OFA it works down to the cell level and scales to full human bodies; it can slow things
  as well (freeze a target in place). Also seems to speed up perception.
- **Visual:** (anime) the target is wrapped in a **glowing blue aura**; living targets get **glowing blue eyes**. Hits
  break the sound barrier: **sonic booms, the hit is seen before the sound arrives** (delayed audio is a signature cue).
  Gear shifts are shown with a car gear-stick hand motion and gear labels.
- **Deku's use:** speeds up every Ultimate Move; Transmission (gears 1-4); Overdrive with Fa Jin; Detroit Smash:
  Quintuple; Low Gear on Tomura to stop him mid-attack; **Second Gear on Katsuki** so his Cluster blasts launch him
  fast enough to save All Might from AFO; Low Gear on Smokescreen so it hangs in the air.
- **Limits:** a last resort. **About 5 minutes** of use before it incapacitates him. Starves his body of oxygen
  (trouble breathing). Each reuse after the 5 minutes doubles the backlash, leaving him nearly **paralysed** for several
  minutes. The recoil transfers with the quirk (he handed it to Tomura mid-recoil).

#### Fa Jin (発勁, Hakkei, "Force Discharge"). 3rd user: Bruce (Lee)
- **Original user:** Bruce, resistance fighter, cold grey spiky ponytail, black bandana, combat jacket with escape
  tubes at the shoulders. He named the quirk "One For All".
- **What it does:** repeated movements (bouncing, hopping between buildings, spinning) charge kinetic energy, released
  later as an explosive burst of speed and power from any body part. Can be spent in partial chunks for several bursts.
  Stacks with other quirks.
- **Visual:** **red glowing energy** builds up around him while charging.
- **Deku's use:** Faux 100 Percent; Faux 100 Percent: Manchester Smash; Blackchain; Overdrive; **Float + Fa Jin + Air
  Force = jet-like flight across the ocean** to the U.A. Flying Fortress; [FW] a huge Fa Jin Delaware Smash that
  smashed the ground to cut off Tomura's spreading Decay; a Fa Jin rocket-launch kick.
- **Limits:** charging takes time; releasing early gives a weak burst.

#### Danger Sense (危機感知, Kiki Kanchi). 4th user: Hikage Shinomori
- **Original user:** Hikage. White hair, narrow green eyes, a crack-like scar from his left eye to his lip (body
  breaking down from carrying two quirks). Hid and trained OFA for 18 years, died at 40.
- **What it does:** a sixth sense for threats. It reads hostile intent and negative emotions and grades how dangerous
  each person is.
- **Visual:** a **yellow lightning bolt** striking near his head (red when Tomura/AFO uses it). Spider-sense homage.
  Feels like a stab in the head.
- **Deku's use:** tracking criminals in the Dark Hero arc, dodging Muscular, close-range dodging vs Tomura.
- **Limits:** attacks that are fast enough still land (Lady Nagant's bullet); blind to attacks without malice (Toga);
  angry crowds overload it. **[FW] Tomura stole it** with one finger-touch, the only OFA quirk ever stolen. After
  that Deku's reflexes fall off sharply, and he baited Tomura's stolen Danger Sense with Blackchain feints.

#### Blackwhip (黒鞭, Kuro Muchi). 5th user: Daigoro Banjo ("Lariat")
- **Original user:** Daigoro, the "super funky" Pro Hero Lariat, who used it for mid-air mobility and binding enemies.
- **What it does:** energy tendrils from any part of the body, steered at will. Grapple, swing, slingshot, carry buses
  and cars, bind enemies, shield. Fuelled by emotion (anger makes it stronger and less controllable). Also used as an
  **internal brace** (wrapped around forearm muscles and tendons to survive 100% smashes) and, at the end, to
  **puppet his paralysed body** (Deku Overlay) and as armour.
- **Visual:** **black tendrils with a glowing light-teal outline**, crackling energy texture. When out of control it
  erupts as huge thrashing black tendrils and can cover his whole body. Peak use: **up to 6 tendrils from his back**
  acting as extra limbs on their own.
- **Deku's use:** Froppy Style, Pinpoint Focus, Faux 100 Percent, Blackchain, Deku Overlay, recoil buffer, shielding
  against Tomura's barrage (blasted all the way to Mt. Fuji).
- **Limits:** loses control under strong emotion; was painful at first; needs a calm mind.

#### Smokescreen (煙幕, Enmaku). 6th user: En Tayutai
- **Original user:** En. Dark hair, high-collared jacket hiding his mouth, quiet; purely a concealment quirk.
- **What it does:** a thick cloud of smoke from his body that can cover a very wide area.
- **Visual:** **purple smoke**; under Gearshift Low Gear the anime tints it **blue-purple** and it hangs, slowed.
  [FW] also breathed out of his mouth.
- **Deku's use:** blinding Muscular from above while floating; Full Blast to blind Lady Nagant while he charged Fa Jin;
  [FW] Low-Gear smoke + Blackchain feints for a hidden sneak attack on Tomura.
- **Limits:** no offensive power (En said it could never be an Ultimate Move, and Deku proved him wrong with Full Blast);
  too much blinds Deku too; weak in rain/humidity; strong shockwaves blow it away (Katsuki's Land Mine Blast).

#### Float (浮遊, Fuyū). 7th user: Nana Shimura
- **Original user:** Nana, All Might's master (and Tenko/Tomura's grandmother); used it to fly beside Gran Torino.
- **What it does:** levitation; boosted by OFA into **fast flight**. With Fa Jin + Air Force he flies like a jet.
- **Visual:** the wiki gives no special colour; it is shown as plain hovering (combine it with Full Cowl lightning or
  Air Force air-blast trails).
- **Deku's use:** first unlocked in PLW to drag Tomura into the sky and away from heroes; hovering over Muscular; his
  main way of getting around; [FW] stays airborne vs flying Tomura and avoids touching the ground (Decay).
- **Limits:** none listed. It was the last quirk transferred; Nana pushed it in herself inside Tomura's mind.

### Combos summary
- 45% + Fa Jin + Blackwhip -> **Faux 100 Percent** (100% speed, no recoil)
- Blackwhip + Fa Jin -> **Blackchain**
- Gearshift + Fa Jin (+ Full Cowl 100%) -> **Overdrive** ("120%")
- Gearshift x stacked gears -> **Transmission**; Gearshift + Detroit x5 -> **Detroit Smash: Quintuple**
- Float + Fa Jin + Air Force -> high-speed flight
- Smokescreen + Gearshift Low Gear + Blackchain -> stealth ambush that fools Danger Sense
- Blackwhip inside the limbs + 100% -> smashes without shattering bones; while paralysed -> **Deku Overlay**
- Switching every quirk on and off in rapid succession at once makes his body lock up (overload); parallel
  processing (Endeavor's training) lets him run several at a time.

## 4. The final battle (Final War arc)

**Setup.** Deku spends about a month training and is the only one who can fight a fully powered Tomura. Heroes give
him the main role against "Tomura" (AFO's vestige inside Tomura's body). He has all six vestige quirks.

**Round 1: Deku vs "Tomura Shigaraki" (Tomura + AFO).**
- Unlocks **Gearshift** (Kudo warns: about 5 minutes). Every punch breaks the sound barrier; AFO can't track him.
- **Overdrive** ("120%") and **Detroit Smash: Quintuple** wreck Tomura; **Blackchain** flings Tomura across U.A.
  even in his armoured defence form. **Transmission Low Gear** freezes Tomura mid-attack.
- Uses **Second Gear on Katsuki** to rocket him (with Cluster explosions) to save All Might from AFO.
- His raw power disrupts AFO's merger with Tomura; Tomura takes back control and turns on his master.

**Round 2: Deku vs Tomura Shigaraki.**
- Even in Tomura's final form they are equal in strength and speed. Deku shreds Tomura's fingers and arms; Tomura
  admits one all-out blow would kill him, but Deku refuses to kill, so he holds back.
- Deku cuts Tomura's fingers off as they touch his face, but that touch is enough to **steal Danger Sense** (and
  Hikage's vestige). Without it his reflexes drop; a barrage (blocked with Blackwhip) blasts him across Japan to
  **Mt. Fuji**. He takes black lightning and **Impure Beam** and is driven into the ground. Tomura threatens to destroy Mt. Fuji.
- Counterplay: **Low Gear Smokescreen + Blackchain feints** keep Tomura's stolen Danger Sense busy; sneak attack lands.
- Fa Jin-powered Delaware Smash breaks the ground to stop Decay; Fa Jin rocket kick (dodged).
- Last Gearshift use on himself, then **Gearshift recoil paralyses his breathing**. He goes into **Deku Overlay**,
  puppeting himself with Blackwhip.
- **Plan with the vestiges:** they found a mental scar in Tomura's core (left by Star and Stripe), so they **forcibly
  transfer the quirks into Tomura through blood**, each one crashing into his soul. Order: **Gearshift first (with
  its recoil)**, then the rest; at first only **En/Smokescreen** breaks through, then Fa Jin, Blackwhip and the others,
  with **Float last, pushed in by Nana herself**. Each transfer forces Tomura's body into a mutating defence form and a
  huge mass of fingers. Then **One For All itself** is transferred. The leaden shell of hatred breaks, OFA and the
  quirks are destroyed, the vestiges (All Might's too) fade, and Deku reaches **Tenko** inside.
- He loses both arms to decay in the mind-realm exchange; **Eri's horn (Rewind)** brings them back.

**Round 3: Heroes vs All For One.**
- AFO's vestige takes the crumbling body back. Deku, in **Costume Origin** (refugee All Might shirt, bandages), has only
  the **embers**, yet is still the only one who can hurt him; AFO treats only Deku as a threat.
- All the remaining heroes attack at once and overload AFO's Danger Sense. AFO gets one last "wish" (reunion with
  Yoichi's vestige) and breaks down.
- **Final smash**: Deku, the vestiges and even Tomura strike together. The body turns to ash, AFO is gone for good, and
  the punch **clears the storm** made by Shoto, Endeavor and Dabi over **all of Japan and North America**; clear skies
  last over a week and strong winds keep blowing in the US for days.

**Aftermath.** Tomura dies; Deku feels guilt and visits Spinner with Tomura's last words. Embers fade by graduation;
he is Quirkless and becomes a U.A. history teacher. Eight years later Class A (led and largely paid for by Katsuki)
funds an **Armor Suitcase** suit by Melissa and Mei, based on Armored All Might data: nanomachine yellow cape like Gran
Torino's, jet boots (Float-like flight), knuckle energy cables (Blackwhip-like), red Iron-Sole-style boots, Zeta
colours, case marked "18". He returns as the **One For All Hero: Deku**, No. 4 Hero, and gets together with Ochaco.

**Absolute peak:** (a) for raw quirk use, **Overdrive "120%"** with all quirks during Round 1/2; (b) for a single
feat, the **ember-powered final smash** that erased AFO and cleared a storm over two continents.

## 5. Weaknesses and limits (boss design hooks)
- **OFA recoil:** above his safe level, limbs break; years of damage left his arms fragile, and he risks losing their use.
  100% smashes still hurt him even late on.
- **Gearshift:** ~5 minute window, then oxygen starvation and near-paralysis for several minutes; each reuse after
  that doubles the backlash. A natural "exhausted/stunned" phase.
- **Danger Sense:** stealable by touch (Tomura did it). Misses fast-enough or non-malicious attacks; overloaded by
  many weak threats at once (how the heroes beat AFO).
- **Blackwhip:** emotion-driven, goes wild when he is angry or unstable.
- **Smokescreen:** blinds him too; useless in rain; blown away by shockwaves.
- **Fa Jin:** needs a charge-up; early release is weak.
- **Quirk overload:** switching all quirks rapidly at once freezes his body.
- **Holds back:** won't kill; deliberately avoids an all-out lethal blow on Tomura.
- **Self-neglect:** fights for days without food or sleep, then collapses (Dark Hero arc, vs Class A).
- **Durability scales with how hurt he already is**: best when lightly injured.
- **Embers:** after the transfer, power drops each time he uses it until it is gone.

## 6. Notable feats (tuning numbers)
- **Speed:** Faux 100% passed a sniper bullet already in flight; Gearshift hits arrive before their own sound (AFO
  couldn't see him); reacted within a millisecond to cut Tomura's fingers before Decay triggered; 45% dodges Nagant's
  shots; 100% leaves afterimages vs Overhaul and blitzes Tomura; flight across the ocean with Float + Fa Jin + Air Force.
- **Strength:** 5% shatters metal pillars; 8% lifts an I-beam one-handed; 30% lifts a bus of people one-handed; 45%
  one-shots Muscular, casually collapses a building, breaks Tokoyami's Ragnarok; 100% one-shots a giant villain bot,
  levels a 12-storey building, makes wind torrents with a flick; at full power OFA has split Dark Might's city-sized
  fortress with one kick (You're Next movie); 120% would stop even Tomura's regeneration with one clean hit.
- **Destruction scale:** final smash removed a storm over Japan + North America (over a week of clear sky).
- **Durability:** survived AFO's mansion explosion (45%), Ragnarok, ramming Shoto's ice wall, a collapsing building;
  fully powered Tomura's barrage across Japan to Mt. Fuji; black lightning and Impure Beam; reflected 100% smashes and
  lasers (Flect Turn); a clean Tomura punch with quirks erased left no injury.
- **Lifting/blackwhip:** 15% + Blackwhip lifted several cars; hauled a bus of civilians.
- **Baseline (no quirk, year 1):** 50 m in 7.02 s, grip 56 kg, softball 705.3 m with OFA / 46 m without; carried
  255 kg All Might on his back.
- **Suggested boss HP/phase mapping (own suggestion):** Phase 1 Full Cowl 45% + Shoot Style + Air Force; Phase 2 Faux
  100% / Blackwhip / Smokescreen / Float; Phase 3 Gearshift Transmission + Overdrive 120% with sonic booms (5-min
  timer, then stunned window); Phase 4 Deku Overlay (tendrils, claws, slower but relentless); optional finale: the
  ember-powered final smash as a sky-clearing ultimate.

# Rimeheart Runeblade: technical & lore specification (0.32)

Reference: the owner's ice-blue glowing runeblade, the pixel-art arsenal sheet (@LoFolyCookie style: chunky 32 px items,
3-4 step palette ramps, dark outlines implied by shadow tones), and the in-game screenshots (large extruded swords, dual
wielding, a grimoire at the hip). In game: item `nusmp:rimeheart_runeblade`. The sprite is generated by
`tools/gen_runeblade_texture.py`, and the model is `models/item/rimeheart_runeblade.json`.

---

## 1. Lore & mana system integration

### Designation & classification
- **Rimeheart Runeblade**: *Cryo-Lattice Mana Runeblade, Class IV Arcane Longsword (one-handed, dual-wield rated)*.
- Lineage: a Clover Kingdom magic tool (the same family as the mod's Magic Tool Sword, which conducts its wielder's attribute),
  forged not to conduct magic but to **store** it. The navy steel is folded around a lattice of frozen mana, ice that never
  melts because it is not water but crystallised magicule.

### Mana circuitry
- **Intake: the pommel gem.** The four-pointed crown at the butt holds the *intake node*, a cyan gem that draws ambient
  magicules from the air (or from the wielder's grip). In game this is passive. The blade is always "charged", and the
  cooldown is the lattice refilling.
- **Conduit: the grip.** The wrapped grip insulates the hand. The raised bands every third row are mana-proof shims, so
  the stored charge never bleeds into the wielder.
- **Heart: the crossguard core.** The bright gem at the guard's centre is the *rimeheart*: the regulator that decides when
  the lattice vents. The hooked silver wings are radiators. Their two outer mana nodes glow when the lattice is full.
- **Lattice: the fuller runes.** One rune every third pixel down the dark fuller is a storage cell. The runes brighten toward
  the tip because charge accumulates at the point.
- **Discharge: the tip and barb.** The light edge near the tip runs with mana: that is where *Glacial Matrix Burst* leaves the
  blade. The barbed fin is a vent that spills the overflow on a critical strike.

## 2. Voxel topology & 3D modelling specifications

### Grid resolution & bounding box
- **Shipped asset: a 32 x 32 sprite, two layers** (base + glow). Minecraft extrudes item sprites into 1-pixel-deep voxels,
  so every pixel is a voxel. 32 px is twice vanilla's 16 px: enough for the hooked guard and runes, but still reads as chunky
  pixel art next to the arsenal-sheet style.
- **Blockbench (if remodelled as a 3D item):** keep the model inside **32 x 32 x 4** units (one unit = one texel). The blade
  is 2 units thick at the fuller and 1 at the edge. The guard can go to 4 units deep. Anything beyond +-16 units from the
  pivot clips through the arm in first person.
- **MagicaVoxel:** model in a 32 x 32 x 8 volume (blade 2 voxels thick, guard 4, gems 3) and export as OBJ, or reduce to the
  sprite. **Do not go to 48^3** for a hand-held item: the extra resolution is lost at in-hand scale and only adds shimmering.
- **In-hand scale:** the model's display block scales it 1.36x in third person (`scale 1.36`, lifted 5.9 px) and 0.71x in
  first person, so the 32 px sword is about 1.4x vanilla length, the same as the other large swords in the mod.

### Layout on the sprite (pixel coordinates: d = x - y along the weapon, s = x + y - 31 across it)

| Part | d range | width (s) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pommel crown | -25 .. -20 | up to 5 | diamond plus 4 spikes, gem in the centre |
| Grip | -19 .. -5 | +-1 | raised band every 3rd row |
| Crossguard | -6 .. 2 | up to 14 | main bar, forward-swept tips, back hooks, inner fins, core gem |
| Blade | 3 .. 27 | +-2, tapering after d 20 | dark fuller, light edge on the lit side, barb at d 16-18 |

### Surface normals & shading workflow
- **Light from the top-left** (screen space). Every material has a 4-step ramp: shadow / base / light / glint. Pixels on
  the s < 0 side (facing the light) take *light*, the centre line takes *base*, and the far side takes *shadow*. The fuller is
  always the darkest steel, which fakes a groove (ambient occlusion baked into the palette).
- **Flat shading, not smooth normals.** Minecraft item quads are lit per face. Keep it that way, since a smooth-normal
  override smears the 1-px ramps. If remodelled in Blockbench, set every cube to *shade: false* only on the emissive gem
  faces. Everything else stays shaded so the extrusion's sides read darker than its face.
- **Glints:** a single `glint` pixel per material, at the tip, on the core gem and on the pommel gem, never in a line.
- **No pure-black outline.** The arsenal style outlines with the material's own shadow tone. Pure black disappears against
  night skies.

### Emissive layering
- `rimeheart_runeblade.png` (layer0) holds everything. `rimeheart_runeblade_glow.png` (layer1) holds **only** the mana
  pixels (runes, gem cores, wing nodes, tip edge, barb). Under every glow pixel the base layer has the mana *shadow* colour,
  so there is never a hole when the glow layer is off.
- The model uses NeoForge's `neoforge:item_layers` loader with
  `"neoforge_data": {"layers": {"1": {"block_light": 15, "sky_light": 15, "ambient_occlusion": false}}}`. Layer 1 is always
  drawn at full brightness, so the runes glow in the dark with no shader pack. The keys were checked against NeoForge
  21.1.234's `ItemLayerModel$Loader` / `ExtraFaceData`.
- **Shader packs (Iris / Oculus):** packs that read the lightmap (most "emissive items" packs) treat full block light as
  emissive and bloom it. Packs that use LabPBR specular maps can be given a `rimeheart_runeblade_s.png` later. The emissive
  strength goes in the alpha channel of the specular map, so copy the glow layer's alpha there at 254.

## 3. Modded gameplay stats & NBT mechanics

### Attributes
| Stat | Value | How |
|---|---|---|
| Attack damage | **10** (1 base + 4 Netherite tier + 5) | `SwordItem.createAttributes(Tiers.NETHERITE, 5, -2.4f)` |
| Attack speed | **1.6** (4.0 - 2.4) | same call |
| Knockback resistance | **+0.25 while held in the main hand** | extra `ItemAttributeModifiers` entry, id `nusmp:rimeheart_stance` |
| Durability | **2031** (Netherite tier) | from the tier |
| Rarity / fire | Epic, fire resistant | item properties |

The same numbers as data (what the item component looks like when you `/data get` the stack):

```json
"minecraft:attribute_modifiers": {
  "modifiers": [
    {"type": "minecraft:generic.attack_damage", "id": "minecraft:base_attack_damage", "amount": 9.0, "operation": "add_value", "slot": "mainhand"},
    {"type": "minecraft:generic.attack_speed", "id": "minecraft:base_attack_speed", "amount": -2.4, "operation": "add_value", "slot": "mainhand"},
    {"type": "minecraft:generic.knockback_resistance", "id": "nusmp:rimeheart_stance", "amount": 0.25, "operation": "add_value", "slot": "mainhand"}
  ]
}
```

### Active skill: Glacial Matrix Burst (right-click, 10 s cooldown)
1. Every enemy projectile / spell within 5 blocks is erased.
2. Every enemy within 5 blocks takes 10 damage (through the mod's BalanceLaw, like every other sword technique) and is
   frozen (+120 powder-snow freeze ticks: they frost over and shiver; stacking a burst with a few hits pushes them past the threshold and freeze damage starts), gets Slowness II for 4 s, and is knocked back.
3. VFX: a frost-tinted splash crown (`WATER_BURST`, `#8FE9FF`) and an expanding ring (`WATER_RING`, `#3FE9FF`).

### On-hit & critical hits
- Every hit adds 50 freeze ticks and Slowness I for 2 s.
- **Critical hits** (swinging while falling, off the ground and out of water, as with vanilla crits) shatter a **3-block mana burst
  around the target**. Everything else nearby takes 5 damage plus the freeze and slow.

### Dual-wield synergy
- With **any magic sword in the off hand** (the Black Clover swords, Licht's blades, Yami's katana, or a second runeblade):
  - Glacial Matrix Burst recharges **40% faster** (200 -> 120 ticks).
  - Hits freeze for 80 ticks instead of 50.
- The tooltip shows the synergy line.

### NBT / data
- Nothing is written to the stack. The runeblade keeps no per-item state (no owner binding, no expiry), so it stacks and
  trades like any sword. The cooldown is vanilla's item cooldown.

# Painting Magic remake (0.44): palette & brush, mood, element counter, living illustrations

Painting Magic (Rill Boismortier, Lira) is now built around the two things the painter manifests: a **wooden artist's palette**
and a **mana brush**. The paint can become any element, so it counters most magic; whatever is painted comes to life; and the
painter's mood decides how far their imagination reaches. This replaces the 0.41 version: same page ids, old worlds still load.
Code: `book/PaintStudio.java`, `book/PaintingBook.java`, `item/PaintToolItem.java`, `entity/PaintedConstructEntity.java`,
`client/PaintedConstructRenderer.java`, `vfx/client/layer/PaintStudioLayer.java`.

## 1. The palette & brush (items)

| | |
|---|---|
| Manifest | Summoning a Painting grimoire (the **G** key) puts the **palette in the off hand** and the **brush in the main hand**; busy hands mean they go into the inventory instead. A `PAINT_PALETTE` VFX shows the board appearing and paint arcing into its pools. |
| Bound | Each one is tagged with its painter's UUID. It dissolves when dropped, when the grimoire is stowed, if anyone else ends up holding it, and if a duplicate appears (one of each, ever). |
| Palette (right-click) | Cycles the paint: Ink → Fire → Water → Ice → Wind → Earth → Lightning (`PAINT_SHIFT` VFX). |
| Palette (sneak + right-click) | Switches to the paint that counters the last element that hit you. |
| Palette (held) | Incoming damage of the element your paint counters is cut by 40%. |
| Brush (right-click) | One sweep of the current paint (`PAINT_TRAIL`): 5 × power damage plus the paint's effect. 0.5 s cooldown. **Brushwork is physical, so it costs Aura** (1% of max, magicules if your aura is spent). |
| Model | 3D JSON models: a voxel-run thumb-hole palette with 7 raised, full-bright (`neoforge_data` block/sky light 15) pools of animated paint, and a diagonal tapered brush whose tip carries the paint. Custom model data (paint + 1) picks the variant; the selected pool is bigger and wetter. Generated by `tools/gen_paint_items.py`. |

## 2. What each paint does

| Paint | On hit | Tensura effect |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | slows, sticky puddles | `tensura:silence` (magic jamming) |
| Fire | burns (3 s + power) | |
| Water | knocks back | **energy drain**: −3% of the target's max magicules |
| Ice | freezes, slows hard | |
| Wind | strong knockback | |
| Earth | weakness | `tensura:fragility` (resistance shredding) |
| Lightning | strikes twice | **spiritual damage**: −1 spiritual health |

**Countering:**

- **Reading the element:**
  - Fire, freezing, lightning and drowning damage carry their own element.
  - A player's magic is read from the grimoire they have out:
    - Flame, Explosion or Magma → fire
    - Water, Mercury or Mist → water
    - Ice → ice
    - Wind or Star → wind
    - Storm or Lightning → lightning
    - Earth, Plant or Sand → earth
- **The answers:**
  - Water beats fire, ice beats water and fire beats ice.
  - Earth beats wind and lightning, and wind beats earth.

## 3. Mood (emotional scaling)

- **Range and effect:** −100 … 100, shown when you paint ("Inspired +18%").
  - Damage and construct strength are ×0.7 … ×1.3.
  - Sizes are ×0.8 … ×1.2.
- **Joy feeds it:**
  - landing hits: +1 to +2;
  - kills: +8;
  - eating: +5;
  - a friendly player within 8 blocks: +1 per second.
- **Frustration stifles it:**
  - being hurt: −1.5 per damage point;
  - below 30% health: −1 per second;
  - Tensura `fear` or `insanity`: −3 per second each.
- **Over time:** it drifts back toward calm.
- **Thresholds:** crossing +40 says "✦ Inspired!", and crossing −40 says "Frustration clouds your colours...". At +50 you can keep a fourth living illustration.

## 4. EP scaling

`power = (0.9 + (MirrorWorks.power − 0.8) / 1.6 × 0.6) × moodMult`.

- `MirrorWorks.power` is the shared EP + armour curve (log EP, plus armour / 60), clamped to 0.8 … 2.4.
- It feeds:
  - every Painting page's damage;
  - the brush;
  - construct health and attack;
  - the paint effects (burn time, knockback).

## 5. Living illustrations (2D → 3D)

`PAINT_EMERGE`:

1. Brush-line art is painted flat on the ground.
2. The canvas hinges up to standing.
3. The drawing swells into a glowing, breathing wet outline that sheds drips.
4. Then the `painted_construct` entity steps out. It is rendered rising from flat to full height (an ease-out-back scale over 16 ticks) with a pulsing translucent wet-paint shell.

| Kind | From | Size | Health / attack (× power) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beast | Painted Menagerie (3) | 0.9 × 0.9 | 12 / 3.5 | own quadruped model, fast |
| Einherjar | Living Illustration, Master of Valhalla (4) | 0.6 × 1.95 | 20 / 5 | humanoid with a sword, 6 armour |
| Giant | Living Illustration while sneaking | 1.2 × 3.9 | 40 / 9 | twice the size, flings foes |

- **What their blows do:** each one carries the paint's element at 60% power.
- **Weaknesses:** they lose 1 HP a second in water or rain (paint runs).
- **When they end:** they melt into a splash of paint when they die or after 30 s (20 s for Valhalla).
- **Cap:** three at once, four when you are Inspired.

## 7. VFX (`PaintStudioLayer`, Time Magic standard)

Textures come from `tools/gen_paint_studio_textures.py`. Preview them with
`python tools/vfx_preview/preview.py paint_palette paint_trail paint_emerge paint_shift`.

- **`PAINT_PALETTE`:** the board pops in with a warm rim. Six blobs arc in one after another and land as glossy pools, each with a ripple ring. It ends with a flash and drifting pigment motes.
- **`PAINT_TRAIL`:**
  - A thick wet ribbon is painted on along a slight arc, tapering and drying out at the tail.
  - A glinting specular streak slides along its top while the gloss dulls as it dries.
  - A glowing head leads it, heavy drips fall under gravity, and it ends in a splash.
- **`PAINT_EMERGE`:** see §5. The seed's low two bits pick the sketch: beast, knight or giant.
- **`PAINT_SHIFT`:** the palette's colours swirl in and blend into the new paint, then burst out as droplets with a bloom.

## 8. Blender pipeline

`tools/blender/build_palette_brush.py` builds both props procedurally (tested with Blender 5.2; written for 4.2 LTS – 5.x). The preview is `docs/blender/previews/palette_brush.png`.

```
blender --background --python tools/blender/build_palette_brush.py -- --render palette.png --glb palette.glb --paint fire --engine cycles
```

- **Palette:**
  - A kidney outline (an ellipse with a finger notch) and a thumb hole are bridged into one solid with an angle-limited Bevel (3 segments) and Subsurf 1.
  - Wood: Principled BSDF fed by a Wave texture through a ColorRamp, with a light clear coat.
- **Paint pools:** UV spheres flattened to 22%, with a Clouds Displace for lumps. Material:
  - roughness 0.08;
  - Coat 1.0 (the wet gloss);
  - emission in the paint colour at 0.35 (the mana glow, which Bloom picks up).
- **Brush:** lathed profiles.
  - The handle swells and waists into the ferrule (red lacquer, coat 1).
  - The silver ferrule is metallic 1, roughness 0.22.
  - The bristles are drawn to a point.
  - The paint-loaded tip uses the paint material.
- **Trail:** a Bezier with a bevel and radii of 0.4 / 1.2 / 1.0 / 0.25 (thick in the middle, drying at the ends), with emission at 0.8.
- **Hand modelling on top:** sculpt the paint (Draw Sharp for brush ridges, Smooth for pooling) and add bristle hair with a Hair Curves object on the bristle mesh. To match the in-game models, bake the wood and paint into 32 px textures.
- **Export:** `--glb` writes glTF 2.0 (modifiers applied). For Blockbench, import the glb as a reference and rebuild it at 16 px.

## 9. Shaders (Photon) and particles

- **Photon (Iris shader pack):**
  - The paint pools and brush tip are full-bright elements, so Photon blooms them.
  - `tools/gen_paint_items.py` also writes **labPBR** specular maps (`paint_pools_s.png`, animated like the colour map, and `paint_studio_s.png`):
    - wet paint: smoothness 245, dielectric F0, emission 140;
    - lacquered handle: smoothness 215;
    - ferrule: metal F0 230, smoothness 200;
    - wood: satin 120.
  - With Photon's "Specular mapping: labPBR" setting on, the paint shows glossy highlights and its own glow.
- **Particles:** the in-mod VFX framework draws every pigment splatter, gloss highlight and trail itself, with no particle mod.
  - If you use LowDragMC's **Photon particle editor**, the same look is a ribbon emitter (texture `paint_gloss`, additive layer `paint_spec`) plus a burst emitter of `paint_drop` with gravity 0.06 and a 20-tick lifetime.
  - The mod doesn't load `.fx` files, so treat that as authoring reference only.

## 10. Not included

- **Trnightmare:** its status effects are not on this project's build path, so none are used. `MirrorWorks.tensuraEffect` takes any registry id, so they can be added the same way later.
- **Already in the mod:** the grimoire summon key and the creative-mode duplicate safeguard (since 0.22 and the early fixes).

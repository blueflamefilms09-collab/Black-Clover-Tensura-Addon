# Water VFX remake: three art directions (proposal, nothing built yet)

Read first: `water_research.md` (canon, current look, gap). Images: `docs/previews/vfx_remake/water_baseline.png` (today), `water_reference_board.png` (your art).
Owner request: "show me 3 different ideas for each before you start". These are the 3 ideas for WATER. Pick one (or mix) and phase 2 builds it.

## 0. Shared rules for all three (so they can be compared fairly)

**Replaced, not deleted (rule 2).** The new look is added next to the old one behind one CLIENT option (looks only, rule 9): `vfx.element.water = V2 | V1`. V1 = today's code, untouched.
V2 = the chosen direction. Only the live shapes get a V2 path; the six old `WaterSpellLayer` shapes (WATER_DRAGON .. WATER_DRESS) stay registered and unchanged (nothing sends them).

| Live shape | Fired by | V2 replaces (looks only) |
|---|---|---|
| WATERFX_CAST | roar, javelin, wrath, tide lance, waterball | the spark + thin stream |
| WATERFX_ZONE | cradle, nest | sigil + spouts |
| WATERFX_BURST | impacts, scale mail, torrent, 3 forms | ripples + wave crests |
| WATER_SPLASH | Mist Step, blood / paint pages, Tensura shots | flat splash + dots |
| WATER_RING | Mist Veil / Prison, basic arts, paint pages | 3 line rings |
| (new, optional) Valkyrie / Mermaid trail | forms (existing `WATERFX_BURST` calls) | small drip/wing-spray add-on near the 3D armour |

Blood and paint pages reuse WATER_SPLASH / RING with their own colours: V2 must keep honouring `inst.color` (tint, not hard-coded blue) so they do not turn blue.

**Budgets (rule 11).** Per activation, vertices (= 4 per quad), never above today's live cost: CAST <= 204 (51 quads), ZONE <= 156 (39), BURST <= 120 (30), SPLASH <= 92 (23), RING <= 160 (40; today
400). Layer hard cap 400 stays. No shaders: only `buf.quad / plane / ring / billboard / beam` with generated PNGs and the existing blends ALPHA, WATER, ADD. `ctx.seg(n, min)` LOD, existing distance
cull, no per-frame allocation (reuse the Vector3f scratch pattern the layers use). Network: zero change (same packets, same shapes, same params). Server: zero change.
Worst case with 10 casters in view: 10 x 51 quads = 510 quads; far effects are skipped by the existing cull.

**Texture generation.** All sprites generated by one new script `tools/gen_water_v2_textures.py` (Python + Pillow + numpy, deterministic seed), output `textures/particle/waterv2_*.png`, 128 or 256 px,
a `tools/vfx_preview` scene per effect, and a `docs/previews/vfx_remake/water_v2_*.png` sheet. Texture/lang/particle tests run before release (rule 12). Time Magic untouched (rule 3).

**Shared motion vocabulary** (existing `VfxAnim`): easeOutCubic for launches, easeOutBack (overshoot) for opens, easeInOutSine for sways, easeInCubic for fades.

## Direction A: "Anime cel"  (hard-edged cel-shaded water, white highlights, cyan outline)

**Idea.** Water drawn like the stills in your pack (Ice Wedge, the cyan outline on Noelle's grimoire): flat colour bands, one crisp white highlight shape, a bright outline, no soft noise.
Reads as "drawn by Pierrot" at any distance; cheapest to render (few big quads).

**Palette.** Outline #1FA8E8 (glow rim #2FD8FF additive), light band #5CC8FF, body #2E8FE0, shade band #1E5FA8, highlight #FFFFFF, foam #EAF8FF.
Source of the colours: the fan art and the cyan outline still (own art); not measured from the anime.

**Textures (Pillow).**
* `waterv2_cel_body.png` 256: a smooth water body strip with 3 hard bands (shade / body / light) and a white wiggle highlight, tileable along u.
* `waterv2_cel_head.png` 256: a dragon-head silhouette (snout, jaw open, 5 teeth, horn fins) in the same 3 bands with a 6 px cyan outline.
* `waterv2_cel_blob.png` 128: round foam lobe with outline, white crescent highlight (droplets, bubbles).
* `waterv2_cel_crown.png` 256: splash crown, 7 spikes with outlined tips (thrown as one plane ring).
* `waterv2_cel_ring.png` 128: a ring of two outlined concentric arcs.
* `waterv2_cel_swirl.png` 256: spiral band with outline for domes.

**Shapes and motion.**
* CAST (roar): dragon head quad (1.6 x power, billboarded about the flight axis) leading a body of 8 cel ribbon segments (beam, 0.9 to 0.3 x power wide, taper) along a sine path (amplitude 0.5 x power, wavelength 3 blocks, phase -age*0.5); outline pass slightly wider (additive, 0.25 alpha); jaw opens 0 to 25 degrees over the last 30 percent of flight (head quad swaps to a second UV cell); 4 cel droplets shed per tick-pair (budgeted 10 total). At landing a crown (below). Quads: head 1 + outline 1, body 8 + 8 outline strip = 16, droplets 10, charge sigil ring 2, crown 4 = 34 quads (136 verts).
* ZONE (cradle sphere / nest dome): one sphere shell is too many quads; use 3 crossed vertical swirl planes + 2 ground rings turning in opposite directions at 0.06 and -0.09 rad/tick, each outlined; 6 floating cel bubbles orbiting (1 quad each). Cradle: closed look via an additional billboard disc of pale water (alpha 0.35). 3 + 2x2 + 6 + 1 = 14 quads, with an easeOutBack pop-in.
* BURST: crown plane (4 quads from 2 crossed planes + ring), 8 big blobs on parabolic arcs, white flash disc for 2 ticks at t=0 (impact frame, see C), 2 outlined rings. About 24 quads.
* SPLASH: 1 crown (1 plane), 6 blobs. 7 quads. RING: 3 outlined ring planes expanding with easeOutCubic (radius 0.5 to 3.5 x power), alpha 1 to 0; 6 quads.

**Budget:** CAST 34, ZONE 14, BURST 24, SPLASH 7, RING 6 quads (all below today).
**Look in game:** crisp, readable far away, matches the UI/anime art; risk is "flat" next to the lit 3D armour.
**Risks:** flat colour on top of Minecraft's textured world may look sticker-like; outline needs a fixed screen-ish width so far effects keep it (use 0.04 x distance-scaled width, cheap); no refraction, so no sense of volume; blood/paint reuse needs a tint-safe palette (bands derived from `inst.color` by whiten/darken, 3 lerps).

## Direction B: "Volumetric glass water"  (translucent layered sheets, caustics, droplets)

**Idea.** Water as clear blue glass: several overlapping translucent sheets with a bright moving caustic pattern and gradient depth (dark teal core, pale rim), the look of the OneHoox armour
(ripple texture inside the plates, white speculars). No outline; depth comes from layering and scrolling UVs.

**Palette.** Deep #14507F, body #2E9AE0, mid #6FD0FF, rim/Fresnel #D8F4FF, caustic lines #FFFFFF at 0.6 alpha, spec #FFFFFF, shade teal #0E7C93.

**Textures (Pillow + numpy, seeded).**
* `waterv2_glass_sheet.png` 256: vertical gradient deep-to-rim with a baked caustic network (Worley-noise cell edges, thresholded and blurred).
* `waterv2_glass_flow.png` 256x64: tileable flowing streaks for ribbons (domain-warped noise).
* `waterv2_glass_head.png` 256: dragon head as a gradient silhouette (no outline), rim-lit along the top edge, bubbles inside.
* `waterv2_glass_bubble.png` 64: ring-shaped sphere with bright upper-left spec and faint caustic.
* `waterv2_glass_ripple.png` 256: concentric caustic ring, soft.
* `waterv2_glass_splash.png` 256: translucent crown with inner light veins.

**Shapes and motion.**
* CAST: 3 stacked beam bodies of different widths (1.0, 0.65, 0.35 x power) at different UV scroll speeds (0.10, -0.16, 0.22 per tick) so the caustics swim against each other; head from `glass_head` (2 quads, one 1.1x scaled behind at alpha 0.4 for thickness); spec glint additive disc at the nose (1); 12 bubble billboards in the wake (alpha fade over 10 ticks, 0.2 to 0.35 blocks); rim ring additive per segment (4). About 8 + 6 + 3 + 12 + 4 + 2 = 35 quads.
* ZONE: 4 nested translucent vertical sheets in a ring (cradle/nest) rotating at different speeds with scrolling caustics, 2 ground ripple planes, 8 rising bubbles. 4 + 2 + 8 = 14 quads (add a ground-contact light pool, 1).
* BURST: translucent crown plane (2), 10 bubbles/droplets on arcs, one expanding glass ripple, 2 thin sheets thrown outward. 18 quads.
* SPLASH: 1 crown + 6 droplets. RING: 3 ripples, glass. 8 quads.

**Budget:** CAST 35, ZONE 15, BURST 18, SPLASH 7, RING 8 quads.
**Look in game:** the richest, closest to "water"; the best match for translucent 3D Valkyrie armour; scrolling caustics sell motion cheaply.
**Risks:** low contrast in daylight / over water blocks / in snow (translucent pale-on-pale); the WATER blend only "deepens edges", so depth gradients are baked into textures; sorting against terrain is
what the preview cannot show; a screenshot sells it less than a video; needs the most texture work (6 generated textures, caustics must tile).

## Direction C: "Ink swoosh + splash crown"  (hybrid, impact-frame punctuated)

**Idea.** The anime's way to sell a water hit: a fast white-and-blue swoosh drawn as a few long curved ribbons with speed lines, then an impact frame (a 2-tick white flash with a ring of ink-black
radial spikes) and a big splash crown with foam lobes. Calm moments are cel-simple, hits are loud. Takes the outline from A and the foam from the Bubble Refresher still, with the radial
speed-line / ink language of the Hydra and Heavenly Ice Fang panels.

**Palette.** Swoosh core #FFFFFF, edge #5CC8FF, body #2E8FE0, ink #0B2540 (impact spikes, 0.85 alpha), foam #EAF8FF, flash #FFFFFF to #BFE9FF.

**Textures.**
* `waterv2_ink_swoosh.png` 256x64: a tapered comma-shaped swoosh, white core, blue edge, 2 trailing speed lines (tileable end caps).
* `waterv2_ink_spikes.png` 256: radial speed lines + a ring of 12 ink-black pointed spikes (the impact frame), alpha-masked.
* `waterv2_ink_crown.png` 256: a splash crown made of rounded foam lobes (Bubble Refresher style) with a thin outline.
* `waterv2_ink_head.png` 256: dragon head drawn as three overlapping swoosh strokes (snout, jaw, mane), no body fill.
* `waterv2_ink_drop.png` 64: teardrop with a white crescent.
* `waterv2_ink_ring.png` 128: broken ring made of arcs with gaps (hand-drawn feel).

**Shapes and motion.**
* CAST: the roar is 3 curved swoosh ribbons (width 0.7 to 0.15 x power, taper), offset by 120 degrees round the axis and twisting 1 turn per 12 blocks, converging in a dragon head of 3 strokes at the front; 2 speed-line strips beside the path; 8 teardrops flicked off; first 2 ticks: flash disc at the caster (1). Quads: ribbons 3 x 5 = 15, head 3, speed lines 2, drops 8, flash 1, charge ring 2 = 31 quads.
* ZONE: sphere/dome as 3 broken-ring bands turning at different speeds, 6 teardrops orbiting, small swoosh spouts (4) at the rim; 3 + 6 + 4 + 2 = 15 quads.
* BURST: **impact frame**: t=0..2 ticks: white flash disc scaled 2 x power (alpha 0.9 to 0) plus the ink-spike ring (1 plane, scaled 1.4 x power, alpha 0.85 to 0 over 4 ticks) then crown (3 quads, easeOutBack, 0.4 s), 8 teardrops, 2 broken rings. About 17 quads. Optional (phase 2 decision): reuse `VfxShake` already present; no screen-space overlay.
* SPLASH: crown (1) + 5 teardrops. RING: 3 broken rings, 5 quads.

**Budget:** CAST 31, ZONE 15, BURST 17, SPLASH 6, RING 5 quads.
**Look in game:** the most "anime moment" and the most dramatic hits; cheap because the flash and spikes are 2 quads.
**Risks:** repeated flashes on many casters can be tiring or an accessibility problem (add a client option "impact flash" default on and cap at 1 flash per 6 ticks per effect; the light-sensitivity
concern is real); dark ink spikes on daylight terrain may read as black stars not water; mixing styles (cel swoosh + lit 3D armour) needs the armour VFX to share the palette; hand-drawn broken
rings are procedural, so the "hand" feel is fake and must be reviewed.

## 6. Comparison

| | A cel | B glass | C ink + crown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Looks like the show | high (stills) | medium (fan art armour) | high (impact frames) |
| Reads as water | medium | high | medium |
| Daylight / far-away readability | high | low to medium | high |
| Texture work | 6 simple | 6 complex (caustics) | 6 medium |
| Worst-case quads (CAST) | 34 | 35 | 31 |
| Risk to existing blood/paint reuse | low (tint bands) | medium | low |
| Fits the 3D Valkyrie armour (translucent) | medium | high | low to medium |

My recommendation (for you to overrule): A as the base for CAST / SPLASH / RING, with C's impact frame and crown on BURST only (the two share a palette), because they read best in
daylight and are cheapest; B only if you want the zones (cradle / nest) to feel like real water volumes. A phase 2 can ship one direction or an A+C mix under the same `V2` option name.

## 7. Phase 2 plan (not started)

1. You choose (A, B, C, or a mix per effect). 2. Generate textures with `tools/gen_water_v2_textures.py`, send previews of each as soon as it runs. 3. Add the V2 render paths next to V1
(client option `vfx.element.water`, default V2 once you approve), keep V1 untouched. 4. Pure tests (budget per effect <= numbers above, tint-safe palette, texture sizes), self-test prefix in the
smoke list, `PerfSourceScanTest` where a new pattern appears. 5. Release per rule 12 (bump, changelog, `[smoke] [strict]`, CI green) before anything is called done.
Not included in this phase: sounds, the 3D armour models, Demon Water and Tide boss effects, any gameplay number.

# Hercules, Lord of Martial Prowess VFX calls ("a blow that weighs a mountain" look)

The ten shapes are appended after `SOLOMON_ABSORB` in `VfxShape` (new; nothing before them moved, the order goes over the network). Every call is one `VfxSpawn` packet with one `HERCULES_*` shape; the packet colour is ignored. Drawn by `vfx/client/layer/HerculesLayer.java`, registered in `VfxManager` right after `SolomonLayer`; textures `textures/particle/hercules_*.png` (17 files) from `tools/gen_hercules_vfx_textures.py`; previews `python tools/vfx_preview/preview.py hercules_leap ...` (`tools/vfx_preview/scenes.d/hercules.json`, 13 scenes: one per shape, a second STRIKE camera from behind the caster, and one per MOMENTUM stage). New work only: nothing existing was replaced or removed. Sources: this look is written from the owner's brief (the clenched fist and the laurel wreath are the owner-approved icon); no canon pages were needed for the visuals; the server side is the lead's.

## Look (what makes it Hercules and not Solomon, Jibril, Israfil or Sandalphon)

Burnished bronze, bright gold, warm ivory / marble white, ember orange and a little deep crimson, with white-gold flashes. Motifs: the clenched FIST and the LAUREL wreath, shock rings that run over the ground, cracks that glow from inside and cool to ember, slabs of rock thrown up and falling back, dust, speed lines, heavy bronze chains, a web of cracks in the air like broken glass, overlapping bronze plates. The rhythm is slow-then-violent: a long gather, a short sharp blow, an impact frame that holds still (hit-stop), then the shock. It shares none of Solomon's indigo runes and circles, none of Jibril's feathers and wings, none of Israfil's cyan maze and none of Sandalphon's cold white and red judgement; it never touches the Time Magic layer or its textures.

## Calls

- HERCULES_LEAP: Earth-Shattering Tremor take-off, sent at the caster's feet: from = its feet, to = the point the leap is aimed at (up to 24 blocks ahead; the same as from for a straight jump), power = 1, 16 ticks. A crater press with cracks, two shock rings and a wave, a short upward column and flash, a ring of dust and bronze shards blown out of the ground, speed lines that trail the rising body (leaning to `to`).
- HERCULES_TREMOR: Earth-Shattering Tremor landing, sent at the ground point: from = that point (at ground level, y of the floor), power = radius (14 in game; the picture runs from 4 to 24 blocks), 44 ticks. An impact flash, a crater press, three shock rings with a trailing wave and a dust sheet, two layers of cracks that glow gold then cool to ember and crimson, slabs of rock lifted and falling back, a column of light and a column of dust, a dust front and embers; camera shake on spawn (VfxShake, scaled by the radius).
- HERCULES_INVICTUS: Invictus, sent FOLLOWING the caster and re-sent every 80 ticks: from = its centre, power = size (~1.6), 100 ticks. A laurel wreath with a fist in it burns behind the body, a laurel crown with a gold ring turns over the head, tongues of bronze and gold flame stand along the body, a laurel ring and a ring of gold turn on the floor, embers and golden leaves rise; the fist flares on every (re)cast. The effect fades in over 20 ticks and out over the last 20, so a re-send cross-fades with the one that is ending. Vertex budget 200 (50 quads).
- HERCULES_GRIP: Titan's Grip, sent at the caster: from = its centre, to = the centre of the pulled creature (1-40 blocks), power = 1, 24 ticks. A huge bronze hand opens round the target (turned along the line) and closes into a fist on it with a flash, a ring and a web of cracks; three heavy gold chains run from it to the caster and pull taut, speed lines run along the line, a ring closes on the floor under the target and a flash and ring mark the caster's end.
- HERCULES_THROW: Devastating Throw, sent at the thrown creature or the lifted block: from = where it is hurled from, to = the point it lands (up to 40 blocks, on the ground), power = 1, 30 ticks. A burst and ring with cracks at the throw, a hurled streak with a glow along an arc, a cone of three shock rings and speed lines behind it, dust torn up along the way, then a heavy landing: an impact flash, a crater press, two rings with a dust wave, cracks, slabs, dust and embers. The flight takes 6 + 0.2 per block ticks (6 to 14) after the 2 ticks of the throw; camera shake at the landing (VfxShake, from `onTick`).
- HERCULES_ROAR: Battle Roar, sent at the caster: from = its centre, power = radius (30 in game; 6 to 30 drawn), 36 ticks. A breath in for 6 ticks (light drawn to the chest, a closing ring), then an impact flash and three thick rings run out over the ground under a four-layer bronze dome, two faint ground waves, rays, laurel leaves torn from the caster and blown outward, dust at the front, a small crater of cracks under the feet; camera shake at the roar.
- HERCULES_STRIKE: World-Breaking Strike, sent at the caster: from = its centre, to = the point of the punch, 4-8 blocks ahead, power = 1, 44 ticks. Light and leaves are drawn into a fist that grows behind the shoulder and trembles for 18 ticks; the fist crosses the gap in 2 ticks (smear and speed lines), then the picture holds still for 3 ticks on a white-gold impact frame (hit-stop); the air cracks like glass (a web in a disc facing the caster, two layers), shards of space fly out, two shock rings and a wave run out across the direction of the blow, radial lines, and the ground under the caster shakes (ring, cracks, dust, embers); camera shake at the impact.
- HERCULES_SHOCKWAVE: God of Strength, sent at the point a heavy blow lands (at the ground): from = that point, power = radius (3 in game), 12 ticks. A flat ring and wave with a flash, a small crack, a few puffs of dust and stones thrown up. Cheap.
- HERCULES_BODY: Adamantine Body turned a blow aside, sent at the wearer: from = its centre, power = size (~1.5), 12 ticks. Bronze plates flash over the body, a second set rings out wider, a flash and two rings (one on the floor), sparks and bronze motes.
- HERCULES_MOMENTUM: Unstoppable Momentum grew a stage, sent FOLLOWING the runner: from = its centre, power = stage (1, 2 or 3), 40 ticks. Speed lines and a trail of bronze afterimages behind the body (stage + 1 of them), a wind ring and wave and dust at the feet; the higher the stage, the more and longer lines, dust and embers; stage 3 adds a tongue of flame trailing off the back. The direction is the body facing when it is followed; otherwise the line from `from` to `to`; otherwise away from the camera.

Ground-point shapes (TREMOR, SHOCKWAVE, the landing of THROW, LEAP's feet) expect `from` / `to` at ground level: the rings, cracks and craters are drawn a few centimetres above that height. The body shapes (INVICTUS, BODY, ROAR, STRIKE, GRIP) expect the body centre and measure the floor 0.86-0.9 blocks below it.

Preview sheets (6 frames per scene, 3 x 2) are rendered by `python tools/vfx_preview/preview.py hercules_strike hercules_strike_back hercules_tremor ...` into `build/vfx_preview/`.

## Rule 11 cost

Client only: no server work, no extra packets (one `VfxSpawn` per effect, sent to nearby players only through the existing perf budgets; INVICTUS is one packet per 80 ticks, MOMENTUM one per stage change), nothing kept between frames (so nothing to clean up on logout, death or dimension change). Per frame: at most 100 quads per activation (Invictus 50, two of them overlap for 20 ticks: 65 measured), at most 54 quads for the heaviest effect (STRIKE), every loop count a constant or a `ctx.seg` of at most 16 (distance and particle setting thin it), no collections or arrays built per frame (a handful of short-lived pose vectors, as in every layer). Decorative extras (dust, embers, leaves, rocks) fall away with the camera distance (40-100 blocks); the Tremor and Roar rings stop at 24 and 30 blocks; the manager already drops an effect that is out of range. Camera shake goes through `VfxShake` (distance falloff, strongest kept): once for TREMOR, STRIKE, ROAR and the THROW landing. Textures: 17 files, largest 512x512, about 9 MB of VRAM in all (12 MB with mipmaps). Covered by `HerculesShapesTest` (shape order, render cases and durations, textures, no per-frame collections, budgets, hit-stop and radius constants, registration, Time Magic untouched, scenes and this doc).

## Not seen in a game

Everything above was checked in the headless preview (`tools/vfx_preview`, contact sheets of 6 frames per shape) and by the source-scan test; none of it was seen in a running game, so lighting, depth against blocks, bloom and the camera shake feel may look different in game. The preview does not run `onTick` (the shake beats) and has no follow state, so INVICTUS and MOMENTUM were previewed as fixed spawns (MOMENTUM from `from` towards `to`).

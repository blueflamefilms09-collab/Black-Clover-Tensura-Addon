# Dungeon parties on FTB Teams (5.0.0)

* **Leader decides the level.** `[party] leaderLevel` (server, default true). The leader is the owner of the player's FTB team (`QuestTeams.ownerOf`). `MegaTravel.choose` asks `PartyLeader.pick(own, leaderLevel)`: the leader's deepest Landing chain index (clamped to the party's newest finished floor by `LandingRule`, as always) replaces the member's own record. Sneaking still picks the first Landing. Leader offline, no team, FTB Teams absent, `useFtbTeams` off, or the player is the leader: the player's own record.
* **Loot split evenly.** `[party] shareLoot` / `shareRadius` (server). `PartyLoot` listens to `LivingDropsEvent` at lowest priority; for a dungeon dimension kill by a player with at least one more living team member in the same dimension within the radius, the drops are cancelled and divided (`split`: equal parts, the remainder to the members after a rotating per-team cursor). Items go to the inventories (`ItemHandlerHelper.giveItemToPlayer`); every member gets one chat line.
* **Worst case per kill:** at most 8 members and 64 stacks, one chat line per member, one map entry per team (cleared at 256 and when the server stops). No tick work, no scan, no packet beyond the chat lines.
* **Not built:** a popup to pick items by hand; chests / vault pedestals / boss trophies; reading an offline leader's depth.
* Tests: `PartyLootTest`, in-game `party.loot` (CI smoke prefix `party`).

# Idle dungeon lanes (5.3.0)

* **Where lanes are handed out.** Only at the gate (`MegaTravel.enterNow` after the Directional Lock agrees) and by the admin teleports. Joining the server or standing in the Hub never takes a lane.
* **Settings** (`[party]`, server): `laneCleanup = OFF | ARCHIVE | DELETE` (default OFF = 5.2.0 behaviour, nothing is ever touched) and `laneIdleHours` (default 168 hours of server running time; a stopped server does not count).
* **How a lane is freed.** `MegaParties` stamps the last use of a lane (gate entry, and every minute for every player inside the dungeon, written at most every 10 minutes per lane). At server start, with cleanup on, a lane idle for `laneIdleHours` is retired: its party keys leave the book, its sectors are forgotten (`MegaData.removeParty`; sector ids are never reused, `nextSectorId`), and its number stays reserved. A lane with no stamp (a world from before 5.3.0) is stamped at that start, so nothing is taken at once. Lane 0, the shared lane, is never freed.
* **How the files are handled.** The region, entities and poi files of the freed lane (selected by the lane's z range) are moved to `<world>/dungeon_archive/lane-<n>/` (ARCHIVE) or deleted (DELETE) at the NEXT server start, in `ServerAboutToStartEvent`, before the dungeon dimension is created, so no file is ever touched while it is loaded. Only when every file of a lane was handled is its number released again; a file that could not be moved keeps the lane reserved.
* **What a returning player gets.** Their deepest-Landing record lives in their own player data and is kept, but the party's floors are gone: they start at the first Landing and the floors are built again as they walk (`MegaTravel.choose` clamps the record to what exists). A player who logged out inside a freed lane is sent out of the dungeon at login.
* **Admin.** `/multiverse mega party lanes` lists every lane with its last use, size on disk and players inside, plus the lanes waiting for the next start; `/multiverse mega party free <n>` frees one by hand (refused while `laneCleanup` is OFF, while the lane is lane 0, or while somebody of that party is inside).
* **Worst case.** Start: one pass over the book and one directory listing of three folders. Running: one loop over the players inside the dungeon every 60 seconds, at most one save per lane per 10 minutes, one check per login. No packet, no entity, no chunk load.
* **Not built / not proven:** a cap of mobs per lane, forced chunk loading only while players are in the arena, spreading floor building over ticks, and the dimension-per-player realm cells of the brief (`BossRealm`) were not touched. Waypoints, rift tombs and boss records that stored a freed sector's id are not cleaned up one by one: they find no sector and are skipped, like a sector that failed. Nothing was run on a real server; the file handling is covered by the in-game self-test `party.reclaim` on a temporary folder and by `LaneReclaimTest`.
* Tests: `LaneReclaimTest`, in-game `party.reclaim` (CI smoke prefix `party`).

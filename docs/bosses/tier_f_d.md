# Tier List bosses: tiers F and D (3.33.0)

The first batch of the owner's tier list (`docs/bosses/tierlist.md`): **16 light bosses** of the Mega-Dungeon, six of tier F and ten of tier D. They are floor bosses of floors 1 to 20 (the early floors, as the owner chose), with the arcs' usual boss bar, power curve (20% to 80% and the Gates), per-second damage budget, no regeneration, magic stones and the saga rewards of **The Tier List**.

## What a light boss is

- One entity class (`arc/tier/TierBoss`) with one entity type per character (own id, egg, name, floors). The body is **the mod's player-model kit** (`docs/playermodel.md`): vanilla player model + a painted 64x64 skin (`textures/entity/playermodel/<id>.png`, `_glow.png` for glowing parts) + a hair style, scaled to the character's height, look in `playermodel/looks_tier.json`. Generated by `tools/tier_bosses/gen_tier_looks.py` in the anime skin style v2 of `anime_skin.py` (3.37: the kit's volumetric `anime_*` hair + armour and costume pieces on the overlay layers, see `anime_style_v2.md`; 3.33.1 painted the hair flat on the skin, still reachable with `--flat`) (specs in `tier_specs.py`). The older recolours of the shared `arc_npc_default` body (`textures/entity/arc_<id>.png`, `gen_tier_skins.py`) stay as the fallback when the kit is off.
- Stats come from the **tier letter** (`TierLetter`): F 700 health / 10 attack, D 1000 / 13, then C 1500 / 16, B 2200 / 20, A 3200 / 24 (later releases). A row may scale them a little (`scale(hp, atk)`).
- Every character has **five moves** (four ordinary, one ultimate that needs 60%+ power and leaves the boss exposed), from nine bounded kinds (`TierMove.Kind`: cleave, slam, wave, bolt, volley, marked, rush, beam, swarm). A move is data (`TierRosterF` / `TierRosterD`); `TierRules` checks every row (telegraph survives the Gates, cooldowns, something to answer at every distance, bounded counts/sizes/damage/effects).
- The **basis** column says how sure the move is: **named** = a move the character is known for by name, **theme** = fits what the character does but is not a named move, **filler** = a generic attack because the character has no clear move to cite.

## Sources (rule 10) and what could not be fetched

The English and Japanese wikis (fandom, wikipedia) were **not reachable** from the build environment (DNS/proxy refused every host), so **no entry was checked against a wiki**. All moves and looks are from memory and are tagged named / theme / filler above; the three characters with little to cite (Rachel, Code, Ao) are mostly filler. Everything can be corrected in one place per character (`TierRosterF` / `TierRosterD`) once the hosts are allowed. Nothing was fetched at all.

## Floors, settings, cost

- Tier F and D stand on floors 1-20 (each character has its own sub-range inside that band); `FloorBossChoice` picks between the arcs' floor bosses and these; growth per floor is the usual `FloorScale`.
- Settings (server config `nusmp-arc-server.toml`, section `arc`): `tierBosses` (default true; false restores the dungeon without them and refuses to summon them) and `tierBossMax` (default 6, 1..24: how many may be alive on the server at once).
- Worst-case cost: one boss decides once a second and runs one move at a time; a move touches at most 24 foes (`EarlyMoves.MAX_FOES`), 5 bolts/volley shots (staggered), 4 wisps, 5 swarm bursts; no per-tick world scans, no chunk loads; a live cap of `tierBossMax` bosses; effects are sent to nearby players only through the existing perf budgets; the boss removes itself from the live set when it leaves the world.
- Saga: `ArcSaga.TIER_LIST` ("The Tier List", appended last); its reward is 24 magic stones, 32 experience bottles and 4 golden apples; its arena shape is the stone ring.
- Loot: `nusmp:magic_stone` (max/2 .. max by tier) and the saga reward; Charter grimoires are not given (these are light bosses).

**Style rule (owner, 3.37, replaces the 3.33.1 rule "hair painted flat on the skin"): every tier boss, now and in every later tier, is in the anime skin style v2 (`tools/tier_bosses/anime_skin.py`, `docs/bosses/anime_style_v2.md`): volumetric kit hair (an `anime_*` cut of `hair_styles.json`, named in the look as `hair` + `hair_color`, only the scalp painted on the skin), shaded eyes, and armour / collar / tabard / cuffed-boot details on the overlay layers. `TierBossesTest` guards it.**

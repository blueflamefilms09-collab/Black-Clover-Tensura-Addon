## UNVERIFIED / INVENTED (not stated by any fetched source)
* The shadow theme itself: "Shadow Step", "Shadow Step: Shift", the shadow-pool visuals, the night damage bonus ("Shadow Hunter"). No source mentioned a Regis shadow technique (a search for "shadow form" came back empty). INVENTED in the spirit of the design doc's "Devour, Shadow" title.
* "Lance" in the title: no source tied Regis to the Lance rank. Not used in any name.
* "Dread Aura" passive, "Fed" kill reward, all numbers: the mod's own design.
* Nothing was turned into a canon number. Every value is the mod's own.

# DanMachi canon research, batch B: Ais Wallenstein (`ais_sword_princess`)

Rule 10 applied: English wiki first, Japanese as the fallback.

## How the data was obtained (honest caveats)
- WebFetch could not open any page: danmachi.fandom.com, en.wikipedia.org and the other wiki hosts all failed with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`.
- The only working channel was WebSearch, which returns short model-written summaries, not page text. Every entry below is "as summarised by a search result", tagged `search (en)` or `search (ja)`. Nothing is a direct wiki quote.
- Anything not confirmed by a search result is marked UNVERIFIED and was not turned into a name or number. All numbers in the line are this mod's own design.

# Solo Leveling canon research, batch B: Cha Hae-In (`cha_hae_in_sword`) and Baran (`baran_demon_monarch`)

Rule 10 applied: English wiki first, Japanese (and Korean) as the fallback.

## How the data was obtained (honest caveats)
- WebFetch could not open any page: solo-leveling.fandom.com, solo-leveling-arise.fandom.com, daddyjim.ai and en.wikipedia.org all failed with `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`.
- The only working channel was WebSearch (model-written summaries of pages, not page text). A Korean search (namu wiki) found no wiki page, only the Netflix live-action cast news. Every entry is tagged `search (en)` or `search (ko)`. Nothing is a direct wiki quote.
- Anything not confirmed is marked UNVERIFIED and was not turned into a name or number. All numbers in the lines are this mod's own design.

# Fairy Tail research, batch C (3.28.0): Gajeel, Mirajane, Gildarts, Juvia

Rule 10: English wiki first, Japanese as the fallback.

## Sources and what could not be fetched
* Direct fetch of `fairytail.fandom.com` failed from this environment (`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`). Every entry marked "en wiki (search digest)" comes from a WebSearch digest of fairytail.fandom.com / en.wikipedia.org result pages (page titles seen: Mirajane Strauss, Satan Soul, Satan Soul (Spell), Satan Soul: Sitri, Crush, All Crush, Juvia Lockser, Gildarts Clive, Gajeel Redfox, and the spell/file pages). The digests quote short snippets only.
* Japanese fallback: one WebSearch per character in Japanese. It confirmed only generic facts (Gajeel's magic is the Iron Dragon Slayer type that works with iron; Juvia's body is water and she is called "Ocean Juvia"; Gildarts' Crash is a caster magic that smashes what he touches, and All Crush (オールクラッシュ) is a cross-shaped wave; Mirajane's Seilah form and Satan Soul takeover in the civil-war arc). The ja wiki pages themselves were not opened.
* NOT fetched at all: the full text of every wiki page, so no spell costs, ranges or chapter/episode references; no Japanese wiki pages; no Gildarts or Gajeel ultimate-level technique lists.
* All numbers (damage, radius, seconds, cooldowns, percentages) are the mod's own design, not canon.

# Fairy Tail research, batch B (3.27.0): Natsu, Laxus, Wendy

Rule 10: English wiki first, Japanese as the fallback.

## Sources and what could not be fetched
* Direct fetch of `fairytail.fandom.com` failed from this environment (host unreachable: `getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND`). Every entry marked "en wiki (search digest)" below comes from a WebSearch digest restricted to `fairytail.fandom.com` / `en.wikipedia.org`; the digests quote the wiki pages "Natsu Dragneel", "Fire Dragon Slayer Magic", "Lightning Dragon Slayer Magic", "Sky Dragon Slayer Magic", "Wendy Marvell", "Laxus Dreyar" and the single-spell pages.
* The Japanese fallback (one WebSearch in Japanese) returned only generic Natsu material (Iron Fist and Roar confirmed in Japanese, plus a "Flame Dragon King's Roar"); nothing for Laxus or Wendy. So `ja wiki` supports only Natsu's two basic names.
* NOT fetched at all: the full text of every wiki page (so no spell costs, ranges, manga/anime chapter references or exact effect wording), the Japanese fandom pages, Wikipedia pages for the Fairy Tail characters beyond the search digest.
* All numbers (damage, radius, seconds, cooldowns, percentages) are the mod's own design, not canon.

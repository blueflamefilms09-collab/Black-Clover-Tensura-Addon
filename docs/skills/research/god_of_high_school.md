## 1. Han Daewi (Han Dae-Wi) - Haetae -> Jade Emperor's Wisdom of the Sage

**Role.** Supporting hero (Nationals competitor, later one of The Six). Water-manipulating swordsman. Resurrected and made Jade Emperor successor after Ragnarok.

**Sources (English fandom, summaries only):**
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Han_Daewi
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Han_Daewi/Abilities_and_Powers
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Han_Daewi/Synopsis
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Haetae_(Charyeok)
- Mirror: https://thegodofhighschool.miraheze.org/wiki/Han_Daewi/Abilities
- Community (low reliability): https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Daewi_Han_(Post-Timeskip)

**Canon techniques (all from summaries, verify):**
| Name | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Haetae (Charyeok) | Water control: bubbles to float himself or others, water waves for defence, water arrows for attack | Haetae is a water creature |
| Hard water and ice | Raises water density to make solid constructs or ice to attack and defend | |
| Water barrier / Water Shield | Barrier around himself that blocks strong attacks, including another Charyeok | Also lists minor healing and light beams |
| Direct Contract (Haetae) | Adds a breath attack. Techniques that normally need Direct Contract become usable as his Haetae grows | The "breath attack" name is not given; mark UNVERIFIED |
| Wisdom of the Sage (Jade Emperor) | Creation from nothing (regrows missing limbs plus the clothing), gravity up to about x1000, and by fan-wiki claims control of the four fundamental forces (gravity, electromagnetism, strong, weak) | Electromagnetism use is a fan inference |
| Martial base | Full-contact karate | Fan wikis tag him a swordsman |

**Drawbacks and costs (summaries):**
- He lost his Charyeok after losing his right eye to the King. He got it back only as the King's successor.
- Using the Wisdom of the Sage without the King's body weakened him. Using it without first having the Body of the Jade Emperor affected only his lifespan.
- Healing is limited (cannot cure illness such as cancer).

**Growth / evolution (canon):**
- Haetae was a child when it first bonded with Daewi and had not matured.
- It reached maturity after Daewi's resurrection and made a direct contract with him, which added the breath attack.
- During Ragnarok he received Okhwang's (the Jade Emperor's) Wisdom of the Sage.
- Mira later gave him the Body of the King to save his life, completing him as a full Jade Emperor.
- His right eye reopened with a glowing Omega symbol in the pupil.

**Why a good Unique Skill -> Ultimate:** It is a clean three-step curve. Water Charyeok (Haetae), then Direct Contract (breath attack and stronger water), then the gravity/creation "Wisdom of the Sage" as the Ultimate. The only hard cost is lifespan or weakness if you take it too early.

## 2. Yoo (Yu) Mira - Yeo-Po Bong-Seon (Lu Bu) + Moonlight Sword Style

**Role.** Main heroine and Nationals finalist. 25th and last master of the Moonlight Sword Style (Wolgwang). Wields the national-treasure wooden sword Bongseon. Later gives her power and body to Jin Mori and Daewi.

**Sources (English fandom, summaries only):**
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Yoo_Mira/Abilities_and_Powers
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Yeo-Po_Bong-Seon
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_86
- Others: https://gohdatabase.wixsite.com/the-goh-database/yoo-mira ; https://tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Characters/TheGodOfHighSchool
- Japanese review (only the Charyeok announcement): https://www.animekansou.com/thegodofhighschool.html

**Canon techniques (verify):**
| Name | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Moonlight Sword Style | Can wield the sword in either hand, cuts steel and concrete even with a wooden sword, can return projectiles with the blade | 25th master |
| Mountain Split | Sword technique (listed on one page) | Details UNVERIFIED |
| Swordless Style | Moonlight Sword variant using barehanded technique | |
| Bongseon (national treasure) | Her sword. She was initially unaware of its status. It was stolen from her family and she reclaimed it | |
| Yeo-Po Bong-Seon (Lu Bu) | Raises stats and offensive power, adds feathery antennae to her hair, and summons Lu Bu's horse Red Hare | Armour and tiger cloth echo Sun Wukong's design |
| Red Hare | Flying mount that carries her | |
| Bangcheonhwageuk: Sky Piercer | Named technique used in Chapter 86 with the summoned horse | Countered by Ilpyo's Nine-Tails Guardian there |

**Post-timeskip claim (community, UNVERIFIED):** She can summon the 108 Agonies and every Borrowed Power on Earth.

**Drawbacks (summaries):** She lost her Charyeok and weapons when she used the "Wi-Fi Project" to complete Jin Mori's transformation into Maitreya. A "halberd" is not confirmed by any source; the wiki names a falchion-type weapon for the Charyeok.

**Growth / evolution (canon):** She learned the true nature of Charyeok from Judge P and showed her own at the Nationals. She is one of the few who combine Charyeok and high-level martial arts at once. She later sacrifices her power to complete Mori's transformation.

**Why a good Unique Skill -> Ultimate:** A sword master whose Charyeok summons a legendary warrior and mount. The "hero of legend" Charyeok can scale to the Ultimate, with the wooden-sword swordsmanship as the base. Easy to build as sword style, then a summon-mount stage, then a final sacrifice-for-power stage.

## 3. Park Ilpyo - Nine-Tails Guardian (Hojosa) + Ssam-Su Taekkyeon

**Role.** Main hero and Mori's friend and rival. Successor to Ssam-su Taekkyeon, founded by his maternal grandfather Park Il-tae. The first Key introduced in the series.

**Sources (English fandom, summaries only):**
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Ilpyo

- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Nine-Tails_Guardian/Charyeok
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Tails_Activation
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Ninth_Tail_Unleashed
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_94
- Mirror: https://thegodofhighschool.miraheze.org/wiki/Park_Ilpyo

**Canon techniques (verify):**
| Name | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nine-Tails Guardian (Hojosa) | Physical enhancement, fox ears, tails made of flame, fire creation and control, summons fox-shaped flames | Tail count and look vary by user |
| Tails Activation | Power-up: more tails means more power and more strain on his body | Debuted Chapter 151 |
| Ninth Tail (Unleashed) | All nine tails: hair longer and darker, eyes red, all physical abilities greatly multiplied; one wiki says it let him fight even with Shiva | Named debut Chapter 299 |
| Heavenly Fire | Uses the scythe-like blades from his elbows | Technique name per one summary |
| Triple Destruction Infernal Fang | Ninth tail combined with a Ssam-su Taekkyeon kick, used to defeat the Third Crown Prince | |
| Analysis | Spots a martial art's weakness after seeing it once and plans a counter | Martial-art trait |

**Drawbacks (summaries):** Cost and burden on the body rise with the number of tails. In the fight with Taek, Greed absorbed part of his Charyeok (Chapter 96 per the search). Sources disagree on whether he lost it all; he kept fighting regardless.

**Growth / evolution (canon):** The Charyeok comes from a direct contract with a legendary god, the Jade Emperor's personal bodyguard. Gaining more tails means evolving through stages, with the Ninth Tail as the awakening. He later fights Shiva-level opponents with it.

**Why a good Unique Skill -> Ultimate:** The tail count is a built-in evolution ladder (1 to 9), so each tail is a tier and the Ninth Tail is the Ultimate. The strain mechanic gives a natural cost.

## 4. Jegal Taek - Greed (and Original Greed)

**Role.** Major antagonist, a member of the antagonistic group. His Charyeok Greed is fused into his body.

**Sources (English fandom, summaries only):**
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Jegal_Taek
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Greed
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Charyeok
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Chapter_109
- Community: https://vsbattles.fandom.com/wiki/Jegal_Taek ; https://topstrongest.fandom.com/wiki/Jegal_Taek

**Canon techniques (verify):**
| Name | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Greed | Absorbs other users' Charyeok by touch or by a shadow-like mass that devours opponents; each absorption makes him stronger and lets him regenerate | A victim cannot use the stolen power again (VS Battles summary) |
| Absorbed abilities | Reportedly Aegis Shield, Doppelganger, Golem, Jack the Ripper, Mage and others | Fan list, UNVERIFIED |
| Original Greed | The first version, stronger Erosion, able to eat everything on Earth; the last born with it is Ultio R | Which version Taek holds is unclear |

**Drawbacks (summaries):** Greed is sentient and fused to the host. Taek ended up consuming Key and Angelos power (community tiers "Key Absorbed" and "Angelos Absorbed"). A gallery caption shows him dying after Greed went out of control, ending in total loss of humanity. Details of the final sequence are UNVERIFIED.

**Growth / evolution (canon):**
- Each absorbed Charyeok adds to the power.
- He absorbed Ilpyo's Nine-Tails Guardian (Chapter 96).
- Later tiers: Greed Consumed, then Key Absorbed, then Angelos Absorbed.

**Why a good Unique Skill -> Ultimate:** Collection mechanic. Defeating a foe banks a trait, and the Ultimate is a mass-absorb with a loss-of-self drawback. Best used as an antagonist-style or risky skill.

## 5. Park Mujin (Mubong Park) - The Cross / Longinus / Tathagata (Supreme God)

**Role.** Final antagonist. Prodigy who joined The Six at 18 by defeating Nah Chalban. After Ragnarok he amplified his power with "The Cross", then absorbed Tathagata to become the Supreme God.

**Sources (English fandom, summaries only):**
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Mubong
- https://godofhighschool.fandom.com/wiki/Park_Mubong/Image_Gallery

**Canon techniques (verify, several are fan-wiki claims):**
| Name | Effect | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| The Cross | Big power increase after Ragnarok; let him overwhelm Ilpyo and Seungchul and kill Jin Taejin | Used his own life force to amplify it |
| Longinus (Holy Spear / Longinus' Hand) | Spear creation (including "legion spears"), seal divine beings' powers with the Holy Grail (fan wiki) | Grail sealing is UNVERIFIED |
| Gravity manipulation | Crush targets, reverse gravity to levitate and launch objects, reshape terrain | From gallery captions |
| Mandala | Described as "Maitreya's first weapon" in a gallery caption | Source is a single caption, UNVERIFIED |

**Drawbacks (summaries):** He lost his seat in The Six through the strain of The Cross (using his own life force) and Jin Taejin's counterattack.

**Growth / evolution (canon):** Prodigy martial artist and Charyeok user, then The Cross, then devouring Tathagata for Supreme God status.

**Why a good Unique Skill -> Ultimate:** Gives the designer a spear-and-gravity skill that evolves by "consuming a higher being" into a Supreme-God Ultimate with a life-force cost. Overlap check: this is separate from Heracles's Labors and Monkey King's Charyeok already in the game, but the Tathagata/Buddha link touches the Maitreya theme (Mori), so confirm before using.

## Recommendation (designer's choice)

Strongest-documented evolution ladders, in order: **Han Daewi** (Haetae to Direct Contract to Sage Wisdom), **Park Ilpyo** (tail count 1 to 9), **Yoo Mira** (Lu Bu summon plus sword), **Jegal Taek** (Greed absorption), **Park Mujin** (Cross, Longinus, Tathagata).

## 1. Ha Yuri Zahard - "Rose Shower" line (Shinsu Flower / Red Velvet Sword)
Role: High Ranker, adopted Princess of Zahard from the Ha Family (best Body Strengthening family); Ha Yurin (family head) is her
great-grandmother. Fights as close-combat/wave-blast specialist, ally of Baam's group on the Hell Train.
Sources: https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Ha_Yuri_Zahard , .../Ha_Yuri_Zahard/Abilities_and_Powers , .../Red_Velvet_Sword , .../Rose_Shower ,
.../Shinsu_Flower , .../Shinsu_Bubble , .../Chiffon_Sword (all English wiki, via search summary).

Canon techniques / arts:
- **Red Velvet Sword** - charges red-coloured Shinsu into a small orb between both hands, looses it as one great wave blast.
  Destroyed the ancient Observer "Purple Dementor" in one shot (Hell Train, Vol.2 Ch.191 area). Taught to Androssi (her Chiffon Sword is a weaker copy).
- **Rose Shower** - "extremely powerful wave blast designed to kill". Pink Shinsu is first charged into a huge baang held above her head on her palm;
  red rose petals gather into several enormous pillars, the blast turns them into white light and red petals cover the whole area. Classified as
  projectile + Shinsu Flower control, first seen Vol.2 Ch.225 (summary says Ch.225; UNVERIFIED chapter number).
- **Shinsu Flower** - Shinsu shaped as a red rose; Yuri is the first person shown doing it; the wiki calls it rare and poorly documented.
- **Shinsu Bubble** - bubbles from her index finger and mouth; used to carry/teleport large groups and to attack/defend. One green bubble came from her finger, not Green April.
- **Close-combat Shinsu injection** - injected Shinsu into a claw of the Purple Dementor to destroy it from inside.
- Resistance: wiki calls her Shinsu resistance "miraculously high". Equipment: Black March, Green April (13 Month Series).
- Drawbacks / limits: the huge charge pose for Rose Shower (palm baang overhead) is an obvious wind-up. Exact cost/cooldown: not documented (UNVERIFIED).

Growth / evolution moment: Red Velvet Sword (small two-hand orb, one blast) -> Rose Shower (palm-held huge baang, rose pillars, area kill).
The wiki presents Rose Shower as her strongest move; no explicit "training montage" is documented. The step-up is "orb in both hands -> huge baang above the head,
rose pillar area blast"; an in-story trigger for the step: UNVERIFIED. Yihwa's True Flame reportedly survived a tiny fraction of Rose Shower (source below, candidate 2).
Why it fits "Unique Skill -> Ultimate": a charge-and-release wave attack with a built-in visual escalation (orb -> rose pillars -> petal storm), and a utility branch (bubble transport) for the base form.

## 2. Yeon Yihwa - Yeon Family Flame ("Hwayeomsa" -> True Flame)
Role: Regular, member of Baam's party; direct female descendant of the Yeon Family (a flame girl is born about once per 100 years). SIU (via wiki) said her power is unrivalled among Regulars excluding Viole.
Sources: https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Yeon_Yihwa , .../Yeon_Yihwa/Abilities_and_Powers , .../Yeon_Yihwa/History , .../Hwayeomsa ,
.../Yeon_Family , .../Yeon_Family_Flames , .../Healing_Flames , .../Yeon_Yirang (English wiki, via search summary).

Canon abilities:
- **Hwayeomsa (flame control)** - turns her Shinsu into fire. Shinsu form listed as Light + Gas; basic colour pink, flames red-yellow, hidden lava flames crimson; attribute Fire. Used mostly with one baang at maximum Soo (weakly sourced: a fan forum thread, UNVERIFIED).
- **True Flame of the Yeon Family** - separate dormant power; forms a flame barrier around her body and lets her fly; activated by itself to protect her life. Family lore: the Yeon head received two primeval flames, one that burns anything and one that saves anything (the Healing Flames, now in Khun Aguero Agnis); family flames cannot be put out by normal water.
- **Combo with Hong Danhwa's Krishna** (Ignition Weapon) - combined attack; Arie Inieta blocked it with a sword and was almost unscratched.
- Physical: threw Bero Bero one-handed like a baseball; survived a direct Ignition Weapon hit without losing consciousness. Immune to Quaetro Blitz's flames.
- Drawbacks: early on she could not control her flames (explosions from practice, poor accuracy; could not hit specific targets). Quaetro told her she could not master the flames while she feared them (fear = control penalty).

Growth / evolution moment: contradictory in the summaries. One summary: True Flame awoke unconsciously during the Hell Train "Dallar Show" fight against Raguel while she was in Buelsar's prison chains, triggered by a mental breakdown (seeing Pompidou killed); she went berserk and lost control. Another summary: the True Flame
activated to defend her life against Rose Shower (and withstood a tiny fraction). Treat the exact trigger as UNVERIFIED; both agree it is an emotional / life-or-death awakening. Control then improved: Workshop Battle Development Tournament (better accuracy), Hell Train (impressive improvement), Name Hunt Station (flames bigger).
Why it fits: textbook "unique skill that evolves": ordinary flame-baang (needs fear/accuracy training) -> True Flame (barrier + flight) at near-death; the skill can gate the upgrade on a low-HP/emotion trigger and add a "control" stat.

## 3. Hatsu (Hatz) - Demonic Fish Wheel Dance swordsmanship + Donghae
Role: Regular, Fisherman-type swordsman and Baam's early companion; in Part 2 uses one sword (two in Part 1). Not a Princess or a Ranker, a pure technique fighter.
Sources: https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Hatsu , .../Hatsu/Abilities_and_Powers , .../Hatsu/History , .../Single_Annihilation , .../White_Wave_Demonic_Explosion ,
.../Ascent_of_the_Dragon , .../Donghae (English wiki, via search summary).

Canon techniques / equipment:
- **Demonic Fish Wheel Dance: Single Annihilation** - Shinsu-enhanced one-handed vertical slash that makes a gust of Shinsu to cut the target. Part 1: threatened Rankers lacking Shinsu protection (used on Quant in Hide-and-Seek). Part 2: cuts deeply enough to wound Cassano (a living Ignition Weapon).
- **White Wave Demonic Explosion** - weapon charged with Shinsu, then a fast thrust with extreme piercing and a wide area effect (first seen Vol.2 Ch.77 per the wiki summary, UNVERIFIED).
- **Ascent of the Dragon (Seventh Annihilation)** - series of rising vortex slashes that blow the target back (first seen Vol.2 Ch.206 per summary, UNVERIFIED).
- **Donghae** - Ignition Weapon, originally the sword of Wolhaiksong (Urek Mazino's organisation); given to Hatsu by Lero-Ro after the 30F Workshop Battle. Not often used: hard to wield. First ignited on the 52nd Floor (Cat Tower) while he was breaking Kendrick Diel's mind control spell.
- Passive: ambidextrous even with one sword; strong grip that briefly held a Ranker still; long-range Shinsu sensing.
- Drawbacks: Lero-Ro warned that many Rankers who tried to ignite Donghae died (they pass out and never wake up) - a real risk/reward gate. Physically outclassed by Great Family members and Princesses even though he can challenge them 1v1.

Growth / evolution moment: at the 30F Workshop Battle he thanked Cassano for "making my sword grow stronger", then Single Annihilation cut Cassano deeply. Hatsu himself says the nature of the attack did not change, only the power (technique = same, Shinsu control better). Then the Donghae ignition on 52F (survive the ignition risk).
Why it fits: a sword-art chain (slash -> thrust -> rising vortex) that "grows" through hard fights; the Ultimate = igniting a high-risk Ignition Weapon (flames + Shinsu slash) with a fail-state penalty.

## 4. Khun Maschenny Zahard - Maschenny Style: Lightning Spear Technique
Role: High Ranker (top 100), Princess of Zahard, mother of Khun Ran; Compression Licenser (can change her size with an Administrator's permission); owns Yellow May (13 Month Series). Antagonist of FUG in Part 2.
Sources: https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Khun_Maschenny_Zahard , .../Khun_Maschenny_Zahard/Abilities_and_Powers , .../Maschenny_Style:_Lightning_Spear_Technique ,
.../Great_Spear_of_Dawn , .../Tres_Cuernos , .../Lanza_de_Relámpago , .../Espada_de_la_Luz (English wiki, via search summary).

Canon techniques:
- **Maschenny Style: Lightning Spear Technique** (she created it) - lets the user act as a Spear Bearer WITHOUT a physical spear; hers is far stronger than a normal High Ranker's. Inherited only by her children (Khun Ran uses it). Attribute: lightning, very destructive and wide-ranging; she makes large lightning barriers to shield troops.
- **Great Spear of Dawn** - transcendental skill: a huge electric baang forms above her and is sent down on the target with a downward hand motion (Data version only cracked Data Zahard's mask).
- **Tres Cuernos** (Three Horns) - fork-like long spear with a condensed electric baang, thrown at Ha Jinsung and never hit directly (first seen Vol.2 Ch.312, UNVERIFIED).
- **Lanza de Relámpago** - thrown lance that flies in a zig-zag, lightning-fast; powered-down Espada de la Luz with less toll on the body.
- **Espada de la Luz** - lightning sword flung and extended until it hits; extremely piercing but takes a toll on the user's body (Ran used it to put Anak down).
- **Deadly Lightning** - bolt fired from user to target.
- Princess blood: durable body and strength, scaling with Shinsu control and experience.
- Drawbacks: Espada de la Luz damages the user; lineage-locked style.

Growth / evolution moment: documented as a ladder of costs: Lanza de Relámpago (cheap, zig-zag) -> Espada de la Luz (piercing but body toll) -> Great Spear of Dawn (transcendental, huge baang). A specific "she endured X to reach Y" moment: not found (UNVERIFIED). A "Data" copy was made after she cleared the Hell Express tests (summary only).
Why it fits: lightning "spear without a spear" with a clean cost-ladder; the Ultimate = Great Spear of Dawn.
Overlap warning: the game already has Spear Bearer; make this a lightning attribute skill, not a spear-weapon skill.

## 5. Ja Wangnan - Shinsu Bombs / Antimatter Bomb
Role: Regular (Hell Train party member), a tactician/support fighter, weak in raw Shinsu control (placed 15th in the World's Strongest Regular Test), strong in cunning, agility (runs on walls) and recovery (heart wound healed in a day, wiki says method unknown).
Sources: https://towerofgod.fandom.com/wiki/Ja_Wangnan/Abilities_and_Powers , .../Ja_Wangnan/History , .../Antimatter_Bomb , .../Shinsu_Bomb (English wiki, via search summary).

Canon abilities:
- **Shinsu Bombs (various types)** - thrown bombs chosen per situation: some filled with pepper powder (blind/incapacitate), most make a smoke screen.
- **Antimatter Bomb** - special Shinsu Bomb designed by Sophia Amae as a control measure for Living Ignition Weapons; temporarily disables the subject's power / destroys the body composition of living Ignition Weapons. Looks like a normal bomb container, resembles a smoke bomb once opened. First used vs Beniamino Cassano at the Train City Skyscraper Platform, again inside the Hell Express (Vol.2 Ch.226); it was filled with the same sleeping drug Sophia gave him in a syringe.
- Drawback: touching the antimatter slightly destroys his own body. After a full year of practice he can freely steer a thrown bomb's movement and trajectory.
- UNVERIFIED: a summary says he later acquired a speaking sword on a Hidden Floor that can teleport itself and nearby people, at the price of killing one person; a second summary did not mention a sword at all. SIU hints he may become strong later (UNVERIFIED, quoted via summary).

Growth / evolution moment: "took an entire year to learn to control the bomb" -> steerable thrown bombs, and the Antimatter Bomb as an anti-Ignition-Weapon counter at the cost of self-damage. No larger "ultimate form" is documented.
Why it fits: an unusual non-melee "tool" skill with a documented training cost, a self-damage drawback and a clear anti-"Ignition Weapon" niche; the Ultimate could be the Antimatter Bomb itself.

# Tower of God, second batch: Anak Zahard and Rak Wraithraiser (3.27.0)

Rule 10 (English wiki first, Japanese wiki as the fallback). FETCH LIMITATION: in this session WebFetch could not resolve any host
(`getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND towerofgod.fandom.com`), so NO page was read directly. All canon below comes from WebSearch result summaries
(English fandom wiki pages named in the results, plus one Japanese search). Summaries are noisy; anything thin or contradictory is marked
UNVERIFIED and was not turned into a name or a number. Every number in the line is the mod's own design.

## 1. Anak Zahard (`tog_anak_zahard`)
Who: the half-Princess of Zahard (daughter of the original Anak Jahad), climbing the Tower for revenge; the Green April passed to Ha Yuri later.

Canon used:
- **Green April** - one of the 13 Month Series weapons, a Compression weapon that works as a long hook; when Ignited it becomes a huge whip that
  changes length and shape and can split into several branches. Source: en wiki (Green April / The Green April pages, search summary). Used for Green April Lash, Hook Pull,
  Ignition Branches, Green April Lash: Shift, Hook Pull: Shift, the finisher Whip Storm and the Ultimate "Green April, Full Ignition".
- **Shinsu strengthening of the feet / body** - she strengthens her feet with Shinsu to move extremely quickly and charged Androssi, throwing her in the air. Source: en wiki (chapter summary, search summary).
  Used for Foot Reinforcement Dash (+ Shift), Reinforced Stride stance, Reinforced Legs stat, Shinsu Legs passive.
- **Half-Princess** (diluted Zahard blood, "demi-Princess") - Source: en MyAnimeList entry via search summary, and the ja game wiki (gamerch.com/towerofgod, passive named "ハーフの姫" that negates one large hit now and then). Used for the Half-Princess Ward art, Half-Princess Guard stance and the Half-Princess last_stand passive.
- Crown Game: she Ignited the Green April and destroyed enemies when her teammates were overwhelmed (en wiki summary). Used for the Ignition finishers.
- Ja fallback: gamerch.com/towerofgod entry for the special skill "緑の四月、始動" (swing down, damage and knockdown, then an explosion) - ja wiki (game wiki) via search summary; only the idea was used.

Invented (no named canon technique): the names "Hooked Vengeance", "Chained Lashes", "Climber's Finish", "Hook Venom", "Slip the Blow", "Running Hook", the Debt (stored damage) mechanic and all stats/numbers. The chain_hit jump of the Whip Storm is an invention in the spirit of the branching whip.
UNVERIFIED and not used: whether she owned two Month weapons at once (a MyAnimeList line says so); the exact ownership history of Green April / Black March; the Workshop-battle fight with Rak.

## 2. Rak Wraithraiser (`tog_rak_wraithraiser`)
Who: a C-rank Regular of the Wraithraiser (giant) species, Position Spear Bearer, loud and proud fighter.

Canon used:
- **Spear Bearer, spear as main weapon, throwing** - en wiki (search summary of the Rak Wraithraiser page; the Appearance and Personality sub-page). Used for Spear Throw (+ Shift) and the Thrower's Eye / Spear Thrower's Arm nodes.
- **Mad Shocker** - a spear made by the Workshop, in Rak's possession, listed for throwing, an Ignition weapon. Source: en wiki (Mad Shocker page, search summary). Used for Mad Shocker Strike, Mad Shocker: Shift, the finisher and the Ultimate "Mad Shocker". The lightning theme (LIGHTNING_SPEAR / stun) is an INVENTION from the name; the wiki summary did not describe lightning.
- **Unnamed Ancient Spear** - a second spear he uses, painful to non-Ancients. Source: en wiki (search summary). Not turned into an art (only the flavour "Ancient Endurance").
- **Compression Licenser** - can shrink his size at will. Source: en wiki (search summary). Used for Compression Step, Compression: Shift, the Compressed Guard stance and Giant Form / Giant's Stance (his giant body).
- Boisterous and proud warrior who thrives on combat and bravado - en wiki personality page / Ranker summary. Used for Spear Bearer's Boast (taunt), Proud Warrior's Bravado, Victory Roar.

Invented: all passives other than the flavour above, the Debt mechanic ("Shock Left Behind"), the Heavy Landing / Giant's Stomp nodes, and all numbers.
UNVERIFIED and not used: a search summary said his power comes from earth / stone manipulation inherited from his ancestors (only from low-quality aggregator sites); a "Lightning Spear" style belongs to Maschenny / Khun Eduan lines, not to Rak; the MyWaifuList entry gives his sex and height inconsistently with the wiki.

## 1. GRAY FULLBUSTER - Ice-Make

Role: Fairy Tail wizard, ice-based all-rounder (fast, versatile, close and ranged). Pupil of Ur; later inherits his father
Silver's Ice Devil Slayer Magic.

### Canon spells (Ice-Make, Static type)
- Static vs Dynamic: Static Ice-Make creates inanimate objects / weapons / alters the environment; Dynamic makes moving ice
  creatures. Gray mainly uses Static, occasionally Dynamic. He casts with both hands (Ur's teaching habit).
- Ice-Make: Lance - long curved ice lances shot from extended arms; impale; ranged.
- Ice-Make: Cannon - small ornate ice cannon; fires nearby rubble as ammo; several can be made and fired together.
- Ice-Make: Knuckle - freezes ground under foe, a tower of large ice fists rises and strikes.
- Ice-Make: Floor - freezes the floor (or water); everyone in the area slips; Gray can slide on it.
- Ice-Make: Grappling Hook - two four-pointed hooks on chains to hold high surfaces / lift himself (mobility).
- Ice-Make: Saucer - large spinning ice disc, buzz-saw, launched at the target.
- Ice-Make: Hammer - big ice hammer floats above the foe, then drops.
- Ice-Make: Shield - large flower-shaped ice shield in front of him.
- Ice-Make: Ice Geyser - freezes ground ahead, tower of ice spikes impales enemies. (Ice-Make: Ice Wall is an alternate
  version that freezes instead of impaling.)
- Ice-Make: Rosen Krone - giant ice roses and thorned branches surround and attack the target.
- Ice-Make: Unlimited - One Sided Chaotic Dance: Gray creates a large number of big ice swords and commands them against
  one or several enemies; fast enough in the Grand Magic Games to overwhelm Rufus's Memory-Make "memorization".
- Iced Shell (forbidden Ice Magic): seals the target in ice by converting the caster's own body into ice (the caster is destroyed
  in the process). Pose: hands in an "X", right arm over left. Only known counter: Moon Drip. Variant Lost Iced Shell (empowered by a
  Lost attribute in the Alvarez arc) is stronger but erases the caster's existence from everyone's memory.

### Ice Devil Slayer Magic (inherited from Silver Fullbuster; Lost Magic / Ancient Spell, extremely effective against demons)
- Like other Slayers, Gray can eat external ice to heal / power up and is immune to it.
- Ice Devil's Rage - Devil Slayer "Roar": a large purple blizzard of cold air, ice and snow from the mouth.
- Ice Devil's Zeroth Destruction Fist - Gray molds ice from Invel's True-Ice Kamui into a pair of destructive gauntlets; used to beat Invel.
- Glittering Ice (Hyoko) - Gray molds Devil Slayer Magic itself into black-coloured ice; hard for an alchemist (Viernes) to transmute,
  froze the Gold Dragon God.
- Drawbacks: excess use spreads black demonic markings over half his body (happened twice); Gray said he gained control with
  Porlyusica's help. Iced Shell costs his own life / existence.
- Spell names Ice-Make: Eagle, Grappling Hook, etc. came up in other digests too; "Hammer Shield" and "Eagle Geyser" were NOT found
  (UNVERIFIED, likely not canon names).

### Growth / evolution
Static Ice-Make -> Ice-Make: Unlimited (mass ice swords) -> inherits Ice Devil Slayer (tattoo on arm appears when Silver passes it
on) -> Lost Iced Shell sacrifice option. Dual-hand Ur style stays the visual identity.

### Why it fits "Unique Skill -> Ultimate"
Ice-Make is a build-anything shape toolkit (shield, lance, floor, hammer) that naturally grows into a swarm (Unlimited) and then a
sacrifice-based Ultimate (Iced Shell / Lost Iced Shell: seal target, pay with yourself) - a clean "versatile -> cost-for-power" arc.

## 2. LAXUS DREYAR - Lightning Dragon Slayer Magic

Role: S-class Fairy Tail wizard, grandson of Makarov; heavy single-target / area striker, leader of the Raijinshu.

### Canon spells
- Lightning Dragon Slayer passive: eats external lightning to heal and restore power; immune to lightning. Magic came from a Dragon
  Lacrima implanted by his father Ivan (wiki: the Lacrima derived from Elexion, the Lightning Dragon King); after it was removed
  Laxus kept the magic by consuming Elexion's Magic Power. (Wiki pages differ on some details.)
- Lightning Dragon's Roar - lightning gathered in the mouth, fired as a wide concentrated blast that can destroy everything in a wide
  area in front; can be curved in an arc to hit non-frontal enemies; paralyses/numbs survivors; needs no special preparation.
- Lightning Dragon's Iron Fist - fist wrapped in lightning, blunt strike (basic).
- Lightning Dragon's Iron Hammer - fist full of lightning punched into the ground, engulfing the target in a torrent of lightning.
- Lightning Dragon's Heavenward Halberd - lightning formed into a thrown spear (Gajeel redirected it onto himself).
- Lightning Dragon's Jaw - strike from above with joined hands; leftover electricity forms a crater.
- Lightning Dragon's Breakdown Fist - listed as an anime-only spell.
- Raging Bolt - big bolt descends on the target (one talk-page editor disputes it being Dragon Slayer magic).
- Lightning Form - body turns into electricity (small sparks to large lightning pillars). NOTE: digest named it "Lightning Form";
  exact wiki spell page name UNVERIFIED.
- Roaring Thunder - Dragon Slayer Secret Art: rush in and punch with a lightning fist that releases a large lightning burst on impact;
  first used to defeat Jura Neekis (a talk page doubts one use).
- Fairy Law - one of Fairy Tail's three great Magics; Makarov-guild legend spell. Laxus gathers a large amount of Magic Power into
  bright light between his hands and releases it as a wave at the command "Fairy Law, activate!", hitting everyone he sees as an enemy.
  It failed once because in his heart he still counted the targets as comrades (conditional on intent).
- Red Lightning Dragon Mode - after the fight against Wall Eehto: dark-red lightning ("lightning that goes beyond lightning") strong
  enough to kill Wall Eehto, proud of his lightning immunity. Signature spell Megaton: Red Lightning (fist coated in dark-red lightning).
  Digest said Laxus stated blood is what makes the power of the Red Lightning; this is only from one digest: treat as UNVERIFIED.
- Lightning Dragon King Mode - power gained from consuming Elexion's Magic Power (described as "like a roaring thunder").

### Growth / evolution
- Dragon-Force-like form: scales along both arms, body bulks up, shown once in the Battle of Fairy Tail arc. The wiki's Dragon Force page
  says the manga never used the name "Dragon Force" for this (only the 2020 video game does) and its Talk page disputes whether
  second-generation users can enter it freely. Treat "Dragon Force" as the game's name, not strictly manga canon.
- Then: Red Lightning Dragon Mode, then Lightning Dragon King Mode.

### Why it fits
Clear three-step ladder (normal lightning -> red lightning -> King mode) plus a built-in Ultimate hook (Fairy Law, a conditional
area wipe that only hits what he sees as enemies) and a sustain mechanic (eating lightning).

## 3. ERZA SCARLET - Requip: The Knight

Role: S-class Fairy Tail wizard, "Titania, the Queen of the Fairies"; swordswoman / armor-swapper.

### Canon magic
- Requip is Spatial Magic: store weapons and armor in pocket dimensions and summon them mid-battle. The pocket dimension has a
  limit (Erza keeps some armors in her room at Fairy Hills). Erza's version, The Knight, swaps armor and weapons extremely fast
  and mid-combat; the wiki calls her (with Coordinator) the only known mage able to switch gear during a fight; advanced use mixes pieces of
  different sets (e.g. wear one armor, hold another's weapon). No numeric Magic cost found (UNVERIFIED); she summoned Heaven's Wheel after
  Lightning Empress without exhausting herself.
- Armors with canon effects (from their wiki pages):
  - Heaven's Wheel Armor - flight; requips 200+ swords to launch at several enemies (anti-group).
  - Adamantine Armor - defensive; held off the Phantom Lord Convergent Cannon: Jupiter; shields combine into a magical barrier; short flight.
  - Purgatory Armor - black spiked; "one of her most powerful"; hammer protects against Real Nightmare and can dispel it; Ikaruga shattered it instantly
    (so strong-looking but fragile). Anime design differs.
  - Flame Empress Armor - halves fire damage; grants Fire Magic.
  - Lightning Empress Armor - reduces lightning damage (withstood Laxus); spear fires lightning beams and can make a lightning barrier.
  - Black Wing Armor - short flight, more offense, summons two axes.
  - Seven Stars Armor - grants Heavenly Body Magic: Meteor (speed + flight), Grand Chariot (seven large blasts).
  - Morning Star Armor - emits bright light; energy blasts from twin swords.
  - Armadura Fairy - Edolas; called her strongest armor and tied to her Fairy Tail pride; destroyed Knightwalker's spear Ravelt but
    shattered from the force of its own attack; swords fire energy blasts like Morning Star.
  - Ataraxia Armor - sword-wings act as shield; swords can dance in the air and attack several enemies; Erza says they land blows
    without direct contact (bypass defences).
  - Nakagami Armor - blue open-front robe with gold trim; one of her strongest. The digest says it consumes all her remaining Magic Power
    and drains a lot even in a short use; its link to a "second origin" is UNVERIFIED (digest wording unclear).
  - Flight Armor / Giant Armor / Sea Empress Armor exist in the armor list; Sea Empress effects NOT FOUND.
- Japanese fallback (names only): kan-so (Requip) / Kishi (The Knight); Tenrin no Yoroi (Heaven's Wheel), Kongo no Yoroi (Adamantine),
  Rengoku no Yoroi (Purgatory), Yosei no Yoroi / Armadura Fairy; 100+ armors claimed (JP fan sites, not the wiki; not verified).

### Growth / evolution
Standard armor set -> Armadura Fairy (strongest, but self-destructing) -> Ataraxia / Nakagami (late, expensive "last stand" armors).
Pandemonium fight: she switched armors repeatedly against 100 monsters.

### Why it fits
Requip is the most "loadout" skill in the series: the Unique Skill = switch kits fast, the Ultimate = a costly top-tier armor (Nakagami / Armadura Fairy
that breaks itself) - a natural risk/reward finale.

## 4. MIRAJANE STRAUSS - Take Over: Satan Soul

Role: former S-class "She-Devil", later bar manager; transformation / Caster fighter. Siblings Elfman (Beast Soul) and Lisanna (Animal Soul)
share Take Over.

### Canon spells / forms
- Take Over (Caster Magic): take on the traits/forms of creatures the user knows. Satan Soul requires Demon Genes; she has them. The wiki also
  notes she can control or destroy a demon without taking its form, and can take over a dying demon to save it (the demon loses control of its body).
- Satan Soul (base): demon form, bat-like wings, flight, higher durability. Her body holds the same Demon Particles as Tartaros' artificial demons.
- Soul Extinctor - Darkness Magic: gathers energy between her hands into a large black globe, then fires a large black-purple beam that causes a
  devastating explosion; defeated Freed; a faster but weaker variant exists.
- Evil Explosion - dark sphere formed in the palm that explodes when fired; wiki also lists a water version (lifts a large body of water, spins,
  throws it as a whirlpool). Details of the water version UNVERIFIED (single digest).
- Demon Blast - large ball of dark energy charged between the hands, fires a dark-purple beam.
- Evil Spark - listed in the Freed fight; mechanics NOT FOUND. Evil Shower (darkness) listed under Sitri.
- Satan Soul: Halphas - flight, speed, durability, strength; destroyed an entire city effortlessly, so Makarov forbade it. (Detail via Spanish wiki digest.)
- Satan Soul: Sitri - fire jets from her feet for flight and speed, boosted strength and durability, fire attacks and Evil Shower; used in the Grand
  Magic Games; Erza called it her strongest spell before Alegria.
- Satan Soul: Hell's Core - commands the demonic tentacles of the Hell's Core laboratory (destroyed it). Digest light; UNVERIFIED depth.
- Satan Soul: Mirajane Seilah - copies Etherious Seilah's look and abilities including the Macro curse (control victims by orders); backstory:
  she took over Seilah after Tartaros to save her.
- Satan Soul: Mirajane Alegria - formed by absorbing scattered demon souls after the Tartaros war; extreme speed, strength and Magic Power;
  activation alone evaporated most of nearby seawater; using full power makes a massive explosion and consumes all her Magic Power for that single attack;
  she was nearly empty after using it against the Irene Squad. Briefly held because of the cost.
- (Elfman's Beast Soul is NOT Mirajane's; do not attribute it.)
- Japanese fallback (names only, fan-site list): Evil Explosion, Soul Extinctor, Evil Spark, Demon Blast, Satan Blast; forms Shutori (Sitri), Harfas
  (Halphas). Satan Blast is NOT in the English digests: UNVERIFIED.

### Growth / evolution
Satan Soul -> Halphas -> Sitri -> Mirajane Alegria (the "levels"), plus the Seilah take-over.

### Why it fits
Built-in power ladder with canon names; drawback is canon (Alegria burns all Magic Power, Halphas is banned for city-level damage) - ideal
"unique skill that evolves into a one-shot Ultimate with cooldown/exhaustion".

## 5. JELLAL FERNANDES - Heavenly Body Magic

Role: former Tower of Heaven leader, later Crime Sorciere / Fairy Tail ally; speed caster, light/star attacker. Wiki design note: he is an "expy" of Sieghart
from Rave Master (Space Magic inspiration).

### Canon spells
- Heavenly Body Magic: Caster and Holder Magic that channels astronomical objects: meteors, stars, black holes.
- Meteor - body wrapped in a glowing Heavenly Body aura for very fast flight and close-range attacks; amazed Jet during the Jura fight. Also used by
  Ultear and by Erza via Seven Stars Armor. In the 100 Years Quest he tried to use Meteor against a slowing Magic and only an image appeared (the effect stopped him).
- Grand Chariot - seven magic seals form a constellation (Big Dipper-like) and each releases a light blast onto the target; Jellal says its destructive power rivals a
  meteor. Used in the Acnologia fight (Acnologia, the Dragon of Magic, ate the Magic).
- Altairis - dense black orb that pulls in surrounding shadow and light, like a black hole; fired at Natsu and Erza at the Tower of Heaven (Simon shielded them).
  The digest also noted a Rave Master equivalent; fine.
- Abyss Break - combines four elemental Magics (fire, earth, water, air); casting FAILED at the Tower of Heaven when Erza's wound caused an aftershock.
- True Heavenly Body Magic: Sema - advanced; Jellal bows, points hands downward, slowly raises his right hand (index and middle fingers extended) and the clouds start to
  circle; a cyclone forms and a huge meteor falls on the target. Used to defeat the four Oracion Seis members. Using Sema in the Grand Magic Games exposes him.
- Drawbacks listed: the Thought Projection body halves his Magic Power while it exists; against Acnologia his Magic is drained every push; Midnight's Zero illusion
  made him injure his own eyes.

### Growth / evolution
Meteor (basic speed) -> Grand Chariot (multi-seal strike) -> Altairis (black-hole orb) -> True Heavenly Body Magic: Sema (meteor drop, area, identity-revealing). Abyss Break exists as a
failed/unfinished top spell.

### Why it fits
Sky-themed mobility skill (Meteor) that grows into a constellation of seals and then a falling-meteor Ultimate; the ability to hit a seven-point pattern is easy to represent.
